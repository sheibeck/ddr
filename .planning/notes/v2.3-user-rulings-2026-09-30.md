## Parley rewards (PARLEY-01) — Phase 91

- **Today:** "can talk to" means the hero may attempt a parley with that foe family (`engine/combat.js#canParley`). One attempt per fight, a d20 check with bonuses (`parley`). Success ends the fight for **half** the fight's experience and no loot, except Human foes, who pay d6×100×depth wilmst one time in six. Failure insults the foes (they hit more easily).
- **Ruling:** a successful parley should still give loot and experience ("We should definitely"). Recommended default for the discuss: full experience and the fight's normal spoils. Say so clearly to the player (parley card, sub-class and race text, the Helm of Knowledge).

## Races and sub-classes — Phase 91

- **Cleric (IDENT-15):** new bad: cannot cast offensive spells, because they gain more hit points. Always starts with the level-1 Heal spell.
- **Master of Arms (IDENT-16):** "no clean withdrawal in round one" is too small a drawback. **Today:** other Fighters get a free, clean escape in round one of a fight they saw coming (`C.tracked`); a Master of Arms rolls the ordinary flee instead. Find a new, real drawback (discuss with the user).
- **Bard (IDENT-17):** songs become a song table: once every **50 squares** (twice a day) the Bard sings a **random** song with a magical effect equal to a random **offense or defense spell of the Bard's level or lower**, so it is combat-effective, not just a way to talk out of a fight. **Today:** one song per 100 squares, always the highest of five fixed songs up to the Bard's level (Soothe the Savage, Inspire the Heart, Lullaby, Cry of Thunder, An Ode to Death; `engine/combat.js` `SONGS`). The bad "dim-witted foes come for you first, even with a party beside you" means **foes with intelligence 3 or less always attack the Bard when a Joiner is in the fight** (`pickFoeTarget`); keep it and say that plainly.
- **Pickpocket (IDENT-18):** new good: whenever you gain an item (from a chest or a monster), you gain one extra item as well. Bad stays. **Today (checked):** the shop bad works: buying costs +25% (`priceFor`, every stock line except the three flat-priced tools) and selling pays 25% less (`sellPriceFor`).
