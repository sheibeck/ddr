<objective>
Merge the Play Games account chip into the ☰ menu button, and move Save & quit and Abandon this character from the HERO tab into the ☰ dropdown. This is user device feedback from 2026-09-24 on milestone v2.0: "I wanted the hamburger menu icon to be our signed in profile icon, and clicking that would open the menu of options plus have the sign in option in it." A scope addition, also from the user, moves the two HERO-tab quit buttons into the same dropdown.

This SUPERSEDES Phase 67 D-05 (a separate chip on band 2, left of the ☰). D-06..D-10 stay as they are: the title chip and the account sheet remain, and are used only from the title.

Purpose: band 2 gets its room back. The ☰ becomes the player's identity and their one place for account and run-exit actions.

Output: pure view/copy/reducer additions (Task 1), the account merge in the shell (Task 2), the quit rows moved into the ☰ with the Delve panel deleted (Task 3), plus test pins and a SHELL-MODULES.md update.

Orchestrator/user rulings (locked, do not reopen):
- R-01 Greenfield: remove #mw-acct-chip and its wrapper outright. Restore the counters' gap to 8px, re-measure the width budget at S/M/L and pin it.
- R-02 The ☰ face is the initials avatar when signed in, and the unchanged ☰ glyph when signed out, signing in or Compete OFF. Size, id, aria wiring and z-order are unchanged. The aria-label names the player when signed in.
- R-03 The ACCOUNT block sits at the top of the dropdown and reuses accountSheetView/ACCOUNT_COPY and the existing controller, with no new copy unless unavoidable. The existing rows follow unchanged.
- R-04 Same ☰ availability rules. Touch targets are at least 44px. The dropdown fits 411px wide at S/M/L and scrolls if it runs too tall.
- R-05 The title chip and the account sheet are unchanged. The leaderboards strip, rail cards, the zero-call boot with Compete OFF, and the Android back button are unchanged.
- R-06 Update every pin on the band-2 chip, and remove the tests for removed elements. Add tests for the ☰ face, the ACCOUNT rows and handlers, and the width budget. Update SHELL-MODULES.md and run bridge-doc --check, saving LF.
- R-07 Presentation only: engine/ and test/parity/ are untouched; content/ changes only in ACCOUNT_COPY.
- R-08 Quit rows: delete the Delve panel and both buttons from the HERO tab. Keep the same bridge calls and the dead-state behaviour. Order is ACCOUNT, the existing rows, SAVE & QUIT, then ABANDON THIS CHARACTER last in the danger look. Abandon uses an in-row two-tap confirm: TAP AGAIN TO BURY THEM, reverting after about 3 s or when the menu closes, with no modal. Save & quit needs no confirm. The menu closes before the action runs.
- R-09 Device look is a deferred human item, not a blocking checkpoint.

Claude's-discretion decisions recorded here (flag in SUMMARY):
- DISC-1 Confirm before quit: today this Settings toggle gates only the Abandon dialog. The in-row arm now IS that confirm, so the setting governs the arm. On (the default) means two taps. Off means one tap abandons. The dialog is removed (R-08 "no modal"). This keeps the Settings row meaningful and not dead. Making the arm unconditional instead would be a one-line change in abandonRowNext's caller.
- DISC-2 Save & quit's existing confirm dialog in window.mzAbandonRun is removed, per R-08 "Save & quit needs no confirm". The action is non-destructive, since ENTER resumes the run.
- DISC-3 Reachability: hasActiveEncounter() includes S.dead, so under R-04 the ☰ refuses to open while the hero is dead. The NEW CHARACTER branch is still implemented and tested per R-08, but in normal play a dead hero reaches a new roll through the Dead tab's Leaderboards panel (ROLL A NEW HERO → routeFromBoards "roll" → window.mzStartRoll). The old HERO-tab New Character button, which was reachable while dead, goes away with the Delve panel. Report this to the orchestrator. Changing it needs a ruling (a dead-state exception in hudMenuNext).
- DISC-4 The one unavoidable copy change: ACCOUNT_COPY.cards.failed.line currently says to use "the little face in the corner". In the dungeon, where that card lands, a signed-out player now sees the ☰ glyph, not a face. The line changes its tail to "from the menu in the corner." The welcome card is still accurate, because signed in the ☰ wears the face. It is kept.
- DISC-5 New copy is limited to ACCOUNT_COPY.menuLabel (the ☰ aria-labels) and HUD_MENU_QUIT_COPY (the four quit-row labels, three of them the old button wording in menu caps). Both are registered in the voice safety scan and the HP-not-WP scan.
