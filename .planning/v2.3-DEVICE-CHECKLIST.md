# Delve, Die, Repeat v2.3 - Pixel 7 device checklist

**Items: A = 92 (phone, installed debug build, Compete OFF) | B = 21 (needs the live gate first) | C = 17 (desk checks)**

**How to report back:** reply with the item number and what happened, e.g. "A12 failed: the chip stayed on after I took the cloak off", or "A1-A20 pass, A21 skipped".

Source tags in [brackets] are the v2.3 phase numbers (87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 91.1, 91.2, 92) the item came from.

## Getting to hard-to-reach states

- **Dev row.** Open the menu (the hamburger at the top of the play screen) then SETTINGS. Press and HOLD the "Version ..." line at the bottom for about 1.2 seconds. Two hidden rows appear: **Start at depth (dev)** (a number box plus a **Start** button) and **Play Games probe (dev)** (a **PLAY GAMES PROBE** button).
- **Start at depth.** Type a floor number (1 to 999, default 20) and tap **Start**. You get a fresh run on that floor, flagged with a DEV chip. A dev run is never saved as a best depth and never reaches the board. Use it for: depth 7 stores, floors 8 to 12, anything "past floor 12" (type 13 or more), and quick Cloak and Staff tests where you need loot. It does NOT hand you items. For gear, play on from the start floor, or look in stores and chests.
- **Real runs.** Anything about where a run ends, records, YOUR DEAD or the board needs a normal run, not a dev run.
- Items that need a particular race, class or sub-class: roll new characters from the title screen until you get it (the roller lets you re-roll).
