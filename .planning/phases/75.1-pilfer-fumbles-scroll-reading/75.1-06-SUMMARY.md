# Phase 75.1 Plan 06: The intelligence read in readScroll (RULES-10) Summary

**canRead is gone: anyone may now attempt any scroll, consumed every time — a Magic User and a Runes/Signs holder still read automatically, everyone else (including a Pilfer) rolls their own intelligence on a derived stream with no intel-12 floor, a plain failure crumbles honestly, and a fumble hands off to 75.1-05's resolver in combat or just fizzles outside it**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 — the reader modes, the bands and the new readScroll:** `engine/derived.js` gains `scrollReaderOf(c)` ("magicUser" for a Magic User, "runes" for a non-Magic-User with Runes/Signs, else "intel"), `scrollReadBands(intel)` (`atLeast = atLeastFor(intel − 1, 20)` = `22 − intel`, `fumbleAtLeast = Math.ceil(atLeast / 2)`, `dieN` 20, no intel-12 floor, worked examples pinned: intel 14 → 8/4, intel 13 → 9/5, intel 20 → 2/1, intel 1 → 21/11, missing intel → 22/11), and `scrollReadOutcome(check, bands)` ("read" on `check.ok`, else "garbled" at or above `fumbleAtLeast`, else "fumbled" — written roll-high throughout). `engine/magic.js` deletes `canRead` outright and exports `scrollReadRng(state, rng)` (`derivedRng(<main cursor or 0>, "scrollRead", <state.acts or 0>)`, mirroring `items.js#pilferFumbleRng`'s own shape). `readScroll` is rewritten: the pending/`noScrolls` refusals and the scroll-consume/spell-pick are unchanged; it then branches on `scrollReaderOf` — `magicUser` keeps today's grimoire-copy/`scrollTooAdvanced`-then-free-cast path verbatim (an additive `reader` field on `scrollRead`); `runes` goes straight to the free cast, no roll, no grimoire copy; `intel` rolls one d20 on its own stream against `scrollReadBands(c.intel)` and branches on the outcome — `read` pushes `scrollDeciphered` then casts free; `garbled` pushes `scrollGarbled` and, in combat, spends the turn (`afterPlayerAction`); `fumbled` pushes `scrollFumbled` (with `fizzled: true` outside combat) and, in combat, calls `resolveScrollFumble` then spends the turn. `test/unit/scroll-read.test.js` (new, 19 tests) covers every reader/band/outcome/edge case, forcing outcomes by searching `state.acts` against the real `scrollReadRng`/`rollCheck`/`scrollReadOutcome` path — never a mocked `derivedRng`.
- **Task 2 — the read lines, the bottom-range formatter, and every canRead consumer:** `src/browser/rollRange.js` gains `bottomRangeText(below)` — the faces strictly below a cutoff ("1–3" for 4, "1" for 2, "nothing" at or below 1, "?" for a non-finite input), U+2013 throughout. `eventNarration.js`/`narrationLines.js` gain `scrollDeciphered` (reads like `heroResisted` — roll vs range, intel), `scrollGarbled` (a plain failure: "You squint at runes you can't make out" — never the spell's name, never "refuse"/"cannot"), and `scrollFumbled` (names the spell and the fumble band via `bottomRangeText`, plus a fizzle line outside combat); `scrollRefused`'s retired `pilfer`/`noRunes` branches are deleted from both files (the only reasons left: `noScrolls`, `notFought`). `gearTab.js` drops the `canRead` import — the SCROLLS row is now always enabled with an empty reason whenever `c.scrolls > 0`. `narrationLinesTable.test.js`'s Pilfer-refusal check becomes a `scrollGarbled` voice check; `usable-features-audit.test.js`'s Pilfer/no-Runes scroll cases become successful reads (`{ok: true}`, since neither `scrollGarbled` nor `scrollFumbled` is a `REFUSAL_TYPES` member); `docs/USABLE-FEATURES-AUDIT.md`'s Scrolls section and its Pilfer refusal-vocabulary row are rewritten for the new rule; `afraid.test.js`'s `FEAR_FREE_VOCAB` drops the two retired reasons.

## Decisions Made

- **`scrollReadOutcome` is written roll-high throughout** (`roll >= bands.fumbleAtLeast`, never `<=`/`<`) so the Phase 73 shape guard never needs a carve-out for it.
- **`readScroll`'s magicUser branch condition is reduced** to `canLearn(c.sub, sp) && !c.grimoire.includes(sp.n)` (the redundant `c.cls === "Magic User"` half is already covered by `scrollReaderOf`'s own gate) — the grimoire-copy/`scrollTooAdvanced`/free-cast control flow is otherwise byte-identical to before this phase.
- **Every forced-outcome test re-derives the real scroll-stream cursor** (a fresh `makeRng(seed).pick(options)` draw, then `scrollReadRng` off that exact cursor) and searches `state.acts` for the wanted band, rather than mocking `derivedRng` — mirroring 75.1-01's own established technique for `pilferFumbleRng`.
- **Two state pins and two no-stall seeds moved because the BOT's own decision policy changed** (it now attempts a scroll read for every class), not because any Magic User path changed — confirmed live via `playRun`'s `onStep` hook before re-pinning/re-seeding, satisfying the plan's own STOP condition (which never fired).
