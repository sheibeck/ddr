Purpose: the user's rulings: "a Fireball burns the reader", "an area damage scroll that fumbles hits the reader and everyone in his party", "a shield scroll with a fumble means you shield the target enemy instead", and the fumble-severity rulings of 2026-09-25 (no fumble kills outright; disabling spells cost the hero turns; a fumbled summon joins the foes). 75.1-06 calls this resolver from readScroll for an in-combat fumble.

Output: engine/scrollFumble.js, a shared reinforcement helper in foeAbilities.js, the three outcome events and their lines, and tests.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- The table's calls (area means area damage, always-landing and unsoaked, wasted helpful spells, Noxious Vapor's sleep at d4, hero Shrink leaving max hp alone) are 75.1-02's and flagged there; the heavy blow, the d4 turn loss, Blind and Shrink as on a foe, normal foe hits and the summon joining the foes are the user's rulings of 2026-09-25.
- **Magic User traits never apply here.** Only non-Magic-Users without Runes/Signs can fumble, so the Cleric's +3 and the Summoner's halved healing (75-10) never meet a fumbled Heal; the foe's heal is the spell's own dice.
- **The reinforcement is a Demons creature** at tier = the summon's level (Lesser Summon: one under the reader's level, 1 to 3; Summon and Phantom Host: the reader's level, at most 5), picked from the fumble stream.
- **Area order:** party members in fight order, then the summoned ally, then the reader last, so a lethal hit on the reader never cuts the others' resolution short.
- **Edge probe (RULES-10), empty:** an area fumble with no party and no summon lands only on the reader; covered by a truth above.
