# Phase 75.1: Pilfer Fumbles & Scroll Reading - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-25 (rulings 2026-09-24 from the capture session; last edges accepted 2026-09-25)
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The Pilfer's "bad" changes from a heal-only lockout into a fumble risk on use-activated magic items (RULES-09), and anyone may attempt a scroll (RULES-10):
- Magic Users and Runes/Signs holders always succeed.
- Everyone else rolls intelligence.
- A bad miss (a fumble) turns the scroll against the reader's side.

Both rules are written natively roll-high, on Phase 73's helper and event fields.

**In scope:**
- `engine/items.js#useItem`'s Pilfer refusal becomes the fumble.
- `engine/magic.js#canRead`/`readScroll` become the intelligence read and fumble.
- New events and narration.
- Pilfer, class and skill blurbs that mention scrolls, Runes/Signs or the Pilfer's bad.
- `docs/CLASS-PASS.md` and the Phase 24 identity contract row.
- Tests and fixture declarations.

**Not in scope:**
- Any other sub-class "bad" (Phase 79 VOX-04 covers blurbs generally).
- Effect indicators for a foe shielded by a fumble (CMBUI-13, Phase 77). This phase only makes sure the effect is applied to that foe in state.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Pilfer fumble — RULES-09 (user rulings 2026-09-24 + 2026-09-25)
- A Pilfer can use magic items under the normal rules. The heal-only refusal (`useItem` `reason: "pilfer"`, items.js ~L1278-1292) is REMOVED.
- **"Magic items" here means use-activated jewelry, cloaks and staves.**
  - Potions (a Pilfer drinks any potion normally), scrolls and tools (`kind:"tool"`) never fumble.
  - Worn passive bonuses never roll.
- **When it rolls:** once per USE/ACTIVATION of such an item. Equipping never rolls (accepted 2026-09-25).
- **The fumble die:** a d20. On a **1** (the worst face, roll-high convention), the use FAILS: no effect takes place.
  - The item EXPLODES for **d10 damage to the Pilfer only**. Armor does NOT soak it, and party and foes are unaffected.
  - The item TURNS TO DUST: it is removed from the bag or worn slot, with nothing left.
- **The blast CAN KILL** (accepted 2026-09-25). It is real HP loss through the normal death path, with its own epitaph line in voice (e.g. "fiddled with it until it came apart").
- Both draws (the d20 fumble check and the d10 blast) come from derived rng streams (`makeRng(hash(seed, "pilferFumble", …))`), so the main stream is never reordered.
- Narration: a new event (e.g. `pilferFumbled`) with an `EVENT_NARRATION` entry. The voice is the user's framing ("constantly fiddling with things like an ADHD teenager"), kept family-friendly, and it NEVER names a diagnosis in player text.
- The Pilfer's good (traps disarm themselves, locks open) is unchanged.

### Scroll reading — RULES-10 (user rulings 2026-09-24 + 2026-09-25)
- **Anyone** (every class, sub-class and race) may attempt any scroll. `canRead`'s class/skill gate and the Pilfer lockout (magic.js ~L586-590) go away.
- **Magic Users always succeed** and keep today's path unchanged: cast, plus the grimoire copy when learnable.
- **Runes/Signs holders who are not Magic Users** read automatically (no roll, no fumble). They get the free cast only; the grimoire copy stays Magic-User-only.
- **Everyone else rolls intelligence:** a d20 against their own `c.intel`, expressed roll-high. Winning faces = `intel − 1` (today's `roll < intel` shape), so the lowest winning face is `21 − (intel − 1)`. Worked example: intel 14 wins on 8–20.
  - There is **NO intel-12 floor**: `resistRoll`'s gate is for foe abilities and does not carry over. Low intel just means worse odds.
  - A success casts the scroll's spell through the free-cast path (`scrollCast`). A failure casts nothing.
- **A fumble is a roll below half the target**, `roll < T/2` where T is the lowest winning face. Intel 14: T = 8, so a fumble is 1–3 (15%) and a plain failure is 4–7 (20%). Pin exact-half rounding in a test.
- **Fumble effects in combat:**
  - A **harmful** spell takes effect on the READER instead of its target.
  - An **area-damage** spell hits the reader AND everyone on the reader's side: every party member AND any summoned ally, each as if targeted (accepted 2026-09-25).
  - A **helpful** spell (heal, Shield, Sense Presence and the like) takes effect on the TARGETED enemy (the combat's current target). A fumbled Shield shields that foe.
- **A fumble outside combat fizzles:** no effect, and the scroll is destroyed.
- **The scroll is consumed on every attempt**, success or failure.
- **A Pilfer reads scrolls under this rule unchanged.** The RULES-09 d10 blast NEVER applies to scrolls.
- The intelligence roll comes from a derived stream (`makeRng(hash(seed, "scrollRead", …))`).
- **Narration:** both outcomes in voice. A plain failure reads as squinting at runes you can't make out while the scroll crumbles, not as a refusal. A fumble names who it hit.

### Fumble severity rulings (user, 2026-09-25, after planning)
- **No scroll fumble kills outright.** Any fumbled effect that would end the reader instantly is replaced by heavy UNSOAKED damage, **d10 + depth**, plus the Afraid condition. This covers Death, Petrify and Freeze; any kill-if-it-outlasts-the-fight mechanic, such as Ice freezing the reader solid; and any kill-on-a-face mechanic, such as Noxious Vapor. The damage is real HP loss through the normal death path, so it CAN still kill a wounded reader. It is just never an automatic death.
- **Disabling spells cost the hero TURNS.** This covers Doze, Stun, Stupidity, Blind, Shrink and Insane.
  - A fumble applies them to the hero as they would apply to a foe: the hero loses turns while it lasts, with the same duration and wake-on-hit rules the spell imposes on a foe. This is a deliberate exception to the Phase 31 "penalties, never a no-actions state" ruling, for scroll fumbles only.
  - No such effect may outlast the fight.
  - The fight must stay resolvable: a party, summon or auto-resolve path must exist so that a lone hero who has lost turns is never stuck.
  - The effect needs an Oracle line and a chip saying the hero cannot act.
- **A fumbled Summon joins the foes** as a reinforcement, capped at 4 live foes. This matches the planner's reading.
