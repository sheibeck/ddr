## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 — the foe ward and the foe Bubble in the damage seam, the rebound, and the ward tick:** `engine/foeDamage.js#damageFoe` gains two new branches ahead of/inside its existing order: an armed foe Bubble (`foe.ward.mirror`) catches the WHOLE raw blow before any multiplier or soak (`foeBubbleCaught`), stores a hero-side catch (melee/spell/item/reflect) as `foe.rebound`, and pops the ward into a plain `popPool`-hp pool for the rest of that round; a plain (or popped) ward pool absorbs the smaller of itself and the remaining damage after the multiplier/halfDmg and before the natural-armor soak (`foeWardSoaked`, `foeWardBroken` when emptied). `engine/combat.js#foeTurn`'s head throws a stored rebound back at the TOP of the foe's next turn through `applyFoeDamageToPlayer` (`ignoresArmor: true`, `ability: "Bubble"`) — a dead foe drops its rebound silently, a lethal rebound returns at once; its tail ticks a foe's own (non-armed) ward down, fading with `foeWardFaded`. Zero new imports in `foeDamage.js`, exactly one foe-side wp decrement maintained (both invariants the plan's acceptance criteria pin).
- **Task 2 — foe Mirror Self, foe Strength and foe Regeneration:** `engine/derived.js#targetStrikeFaces` gains a sixth (final) term — `mirror > 0` caps `faces` at 1, never raising an already-zeroed `faces` — shared by `playerStrike` and `heroStrikeFacesVs`'s display; `allyTurn`, `alliesTurn`'s legacy branch and `memberStrike` each inline the identical cap (they build their own five-term chain rather than calling the shared helper, so the cap joins them the same way). `playerStrike` additionally names the cap as a negative `"Mirror Self"` mod by a second, zero-draw call to `targetStrikeFaces` with `mirror:0`. `foeLevelBase` folds in `f.might || 0` once per landed blow (never crit-doubled), reaching all three of its call sites (`pursuitStrike`, the member branch, the hero branch) through the one shared term. `foeTurn`'s per-foe loop gains a Regeneration tick — a live foe with `regen` below `maxWP` heals a capped d8 drawn from `derivedRng(cursor, "foeRegen", C.round, foeIndex)` (never the main rng), placed ahead of the asleep/stupid/stunned skips so a sleeping regenerator still heals; its tail gains a matching Mirror Self countdown (`foeMirrorFaded`). `test/unit/roll-high-guard.test.js`'s `engine/combat.js` DRAW_INVENTORY row moves `amount: 17 -> 18` for the one new regen draw.
- **Task 3 — lines, carve-outs and the full suite:** `src/browser/eventNarration.js` and `src/browser/narrationLines.js` both gain builders for all seven new event types, tone-matched to their existing hero-side twins (`wardAbsorbed`/`wardShattered`/`wardFaded`/`mirrorFaded`/`foeHealed`) — a foe's ward/mirror fading is neutral (`beat`), a foe's ward/bubble eating the hero's own blow is bad news for the hero (`miss`/`dodge`), a popped Bubble throwing the caught blow back is a telegraph (`hurt`/`dodge`) ahead of the existing `foeBolted` line that states the hp actually lost. `test/parity/harness/comparables.js#stripFoeAbilityState` (read by all three comparables) now also drops `ward`/`rebound`/`mirror`/`might`/`strengthBoost`/`regen`/`senses` from every foe — a structural tripwire for fields nothing sets yet, front-loaded per the plan's own field list so 75.1-05's resolver never moves a fixture either. ## Decisions Made

- **Every foe-side field mirrors its hero-side twin's shape exactly** (`foe.ward` shaped like `c.ward`, `foe.mirror` like `c.mirror`) so the two sides of the fight read the same rule the same way, and so 75.1-05's resolver has an unambiguous shape to write into.
- **The Mirror Self cap is inlined at three of its four call sites** (`allyTurn`, `alliesTurn`'s legacy branch, `memberStrike`) rather than routed through `targetStrikeFaces`, because none of those three ever called that helper before this plan — they each already inline their own copy of the same five-term chain. Only `playerStrike` (and the display helper `heroStrikeFacesVs`) route through the shared function.
- **`playerStrike`'s "Mirror Self" mod is computed by a second, zero-draw call to `targetStrikeFaces`** with a shallow `{...t, mirror: 0}` clone, rather than changing the helper's return shape to carry mods — keeps `heroStrikeFacesVs`'s plain-number contract (and every other caller) untouched.
- **`foeRegen`'s stream is keyed on `C.round` and the foe's own index in `C.foes`**, not on `state.acts` (the pattern `pilferFumbleRng` established) — a foe's turn-by-turn regen has no natural "action count" of its own, and round+index is deterministic and collision-free across a fight with multiple foes.
