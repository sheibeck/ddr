## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Who a foe's Bubble throws back at.** The hero's own Bubble (75-06) reflects at the attacker. For a foe's Bubble, a blow from the hero's side (weapon, spell, item, or the hero's own reflection) is thrown back at the hero; a party member's or summon's blow is only absorbed, because the damage seam cannot tell which member struck and a summon has no hit points. The throw-back lands at the top of the foe's next turn (the seam cannot run the hero's death path mid-strike). Flagged for the user.
- **Mirror Self on a foe caps weapon strikes only** (hero, summon, party member), mirroring the hero's own Mirror Self, which only affects foes' melee swings. Thrown spells are unaffected. Flagged.
- **A foe ward soaks every damage kind** that reaches the seam, including a foe's blow on another foe (Insane). Instant effects that never pass through damage (Petrify, Death, stone items) still work on a warded foe.
- **Senses on a foe has no further mechanic.** 75.1-05 sets `f.senses`; the hero-side senses rules (initiative, darkness) have no foe analogue once a fight has begun. It is carved out here and left for Phase 77's indicator.
