## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Ice's freeze kills the reader.** Ice freezes a foe solid when its burn runs out; on the reader that is death, unless the fight ends first. See 75.1-02's "kill effects are literal". Flagged for the user.
- **The burn bypasses armor and wards,** like every fumble (the Apprentice backfire precedent).
- **The burn lives on the combat,** not the character, so it ends with the fight and needs no save handling (combat is always null on load).
- **A fight-long hex shows no round count.** conditionsOf reports it with remaining null; today's chip then prints its label with no count. Phase 77's CMBUI-13 owns the indicator wording.
