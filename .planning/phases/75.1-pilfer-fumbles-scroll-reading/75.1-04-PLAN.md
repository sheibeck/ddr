<objective>
Build the reader-side effects a harmful fumble needs (RULES-10, with the user's fumble-severity rulings of 2026-09-25 and the second ruling on turn-loss duration and easy hits):
- The reader's burn (Acid, Ice).
- The heavy blow that replaces every instant kill (d10 + depth, unsoaked, plus Afraid).
- The state in which the hero cannot act and loses at most d4 turns (Doze, Stun, Stupidity, Insane, and Noxious Vapor's sleep), with a turn-loss action that keeps the fight moving.
- Hero Blind and hero Shrink, working on the hero the way they work on a foe.

Purpose: 75.1-CONTEXT's rulings:
- No scroll fumble kills outright.
- Disabling spells cost the hero turns, at most d4 of them. This is a deliberate exception to Phase 31's "penalties, never a no-actions state", for scroll fumbles only.
- Blind and Shrink don't cost turns; they apply their foe-side effect to the hero.
- Foes hit an out hero normally.
- The fight must stay resolvable.

The engine has no "hero cannot act" state today (Phase 31 retired the prototype's frozen hero) and no hero-side blind or shrink, so this plan defines all three precisely. The resolver (75.1-05) sets them; 75.1-09 builds their shell.

Output:
- The burn and the heavy blow.
- `C.heroOut` with the `loseTurn` action and its conversion.
- Hero Blind and hero Shrink.
- The chip data, the bot rule and the death cause.
- Their lines, the carve-outs and tests.
</objective>

## Decided (user rulings, 2026-09-25)

- **At most d4 lost turns.** Every turn-loss fumble lasts at most d4 turns (Doze, Stun, Stupidity, Insane; Noxious Vapor's sleep too), so nothing costs turns for the whole fight. HERO_OUT_MAX (4) clamps `left` and bounds every soft-lock driver; no separate cap is needed.
- **Blind and Shrink don't cost turns.** They work on the hero the way they work on a foe: Blind is a to-hit penalty and Shrink halves hp, both for the fight.
- **Foes hit an out hero normally.** There is no easier-hit bonus.
- **The heavy blow's Afraid** is the Phase 31 condition (AFRAID_ROUNDS), raised, never lowered.

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Hero Blind means one winning face on weapon strikes,** the exact override a blind foe's swing gets. Like a blind foe, the hero's spells, party members and flee roll are unaffected. It is applied inside toHit, so the per-target floors a strike already has (5 winning faces against a dozing or stupid target) still apply after it. Flagged for the user.
- **Hero Shrink never touches max hp.** On a foe, Shrink halves max hp too; on the hero that would outlast the fight, so only current hp (halved where the fumble lands) and weapon damage (halved for the fight) change. Flagged for the user.
- **The burn and the fumble flags live on the combat,** not the character, so they end with the fight and need no save handling (combat is always null on load).
