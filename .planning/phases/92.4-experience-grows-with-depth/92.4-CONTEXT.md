# Phase 92.4: Experience grows with depth - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-02 (orchestrator, user ruling from the device pass)
**Status:** Ready to execute (one plan, 92.4-01)

## User ruling (2026-10-02)
User: "Since monster hitpoints and such go up per floor, we should also increase xp as well. Getting the same xp for a goblin on floor 5 that matches the weaker version on floor 1 is rough." They chose **+10% per floor**, then confirmed "yeah, 10% sounds good". They also said: "if that is going to drastically affect the difficulty curve, we should talk about that."

- **Rule:**
  - Every experience grant from a foe is multiplied by `1 + XP_PER_DEPTH × (depth − 1)`, with `XP_PER_DEPTH = 0.10`. That covers the kill (`killSpFor`, `engine/derived.js` ~2937) and the parley win, which uses the same `killSpFor`.
  - An elite also pays `× (1 + FOE_ELITE.hpPerRank × eliteRank)`, matching its extra hit points.
  - Apply it before the party split and `HERO_SP_SCALE`.
  - Use one rounding at the end, matching the current arithmetic style.
  - The descend bonus and the table-four XP dots are NOT foe grants and stay unchanged (state this).
- **Implementation:**
  - Add a new DIALS key `XP_DEPTH_SCALE: { perDepth: 0.10 }` in engine/difficulty.js with a JSDoc in the house style (identity 0 = today). It must be settable through `setDialsForTuning` / `--dials` so the measurement can compare 0 and 0.10.
  - Pure arithmetic: no new rng draws.
  - The `foeKilled` event's `spGained` reflects it.
- **Text:**
  - Wherever the game states how much experience a kill pays (help, ROLL-LEDGER, docs/RULES or similar), add the depth bonus.
  - EVENT_NARRATION is unchanged unless a line states the formula.
  - Add a patch-notes line in docs/patch-notes/2.3.0.md ("Experience: the same at any depth → +10% per floor below the first").
- **Fixtures:** measure, declare under FIXTURE-INVENTORY "### Phase 92.4 plan 01" and regenerate alone (state pins by label, never `save`).
- **Measurement (bot runs ALLOWED for this plan):**
  - Run `tune-difficulty --seeds=1000` twice on the same commit: with `--dials='{"XP_DEPTH_SCALE":{"perDepth":0}}'` and with the shipped 0.10.
  - Report side by side, plus the 92-04 FINAL (tools/readouts/92-final-1000.txt):
    - p50, mean and p90 death depth;
    - per-floor survival floors 1-12 against the band;
    - the starvation share;
    - the mean hero level reached at floors 3, 5 and 8, if the readout gives it (otherwise from a small script);
    - the class p50s.
  - Save both under tools/readouts/92.4-*.txt.
  - Record them in docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md as a post-pass, user-ruled change.
  - Do NOT retune anything. If the p50 moves by a floor or more, flag it prominently; the orchestrator takes it to the user.
