# Phase 91.1 Plan 01: The value ledger and the batched value checkpoint Summary

A value ledger (`docs/VALUE-LEDGER.md`) covers all 6 races, 24 sub-classes, 21 skills, 9 level-up abilities, Sing and 5 cross-cutting systems, flags everything weak, tiny, one-round or once-per-fight, and records the user's rulings on all 37 batched questions, with a guard test that holds the whole file.

## What was built

- **The ledger.** One row per entity with the systems its text names marked `exists` (with the engine site from the closed audits) or `GAP`, value flags (`once-per-fight`, `one-round`, `low-bonus`, `no-value`, `gap`, `joiner-gap`), a recommendation, the covering question and a verdict. The yardstick (swing damage, hit chance, foe hit points by floor, foe count, parley faces) is computed from the engine and written into "How to read this ledger". K01 to K20 each have a row and a question (K19 and K20 are the two dead-code cleanups with their worklists, owned by 91.1-05).
- **The guard test.** `test/unit/value-ledger.test.js` (26 tests) pins row sets and order, footer entry ids, live ability text, the closed vocabularies, flag-to-recommendation-to-question consistency, part placement, known findings, rulings (all-or-nothing once any is recorded), the ownership table, the closed-mode rules, and the user's actual rulings. It runs doctored-copy tests against `test/fixtures/value-ledger-open.md` (the ledger as it stood at the checkpoint) so every rule keeps being proven after the real doc is ruled.
- **Owner tokens.** `identity-audit.test.js` and `skill-audit.test.js` accept a trailing `value change (91.1-0N)` and reject any other 91.1 owner (one new test each).
- **The checkpoint.** One batched `checkpoint:decision` of 37 questions was returned to the orchestrator; the user answered in one reply and then changed V27.
