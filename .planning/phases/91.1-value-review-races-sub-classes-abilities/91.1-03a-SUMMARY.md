## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Interpretation] V15's numbers over its words.** The ruling says "applies on top of the Illusionist's d20" and gives "a d12 at level 1, a d10 at level 2". Stacked literally the Elf's step on the Illusionist's d20 gives a d12 at level 2, not a d10. I built the ruling's numbers: an Elf Illusionist strikes exactly like any Elf (d12, d10, d8 at level 3), the Illusionist's d20 rule is skipped for a race that has a strike step. If the user meant the literal stacking (d12 at level 2), it is one line in `strikeDie`.

**2. [Interpretation] Joiners follow the shared rules.** The Cleric's heal bonus and the chart's healing and protection bonuses are one rule the hero's and a Joiner's casts share (the +3 was shared before), so a Joiner Cleric heals 4 more (pinned, `joiner-casters.test.js` re-based). The Joiner's ward read in `allyCast` is only reachable by a sung ward (a Bard has no chart row: +0), so it changes nothing today. `strikeDie` is shared, so a Joiner Elf Illusionist strikes the Elf's die too. The Regeneration tick is not a cast and adds nothing (as the old +3 never did).

**3. [Consequence of V20] A Cleric's scroll of Strength is copied.** Strength is learnable now, so the scribe gate copies a Cleric's scroll of it like any learnable spell instead of free-casting it; the Q3 B rule itself (a scroll of a spell the book still closes, a thrown Freeze, free-casts and is never copied) is pinned in a fight by `identity-rulings.test.js` and a closed spell's no-copy in `cleric-offense-ban.test.js`.

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] The "no shield" guards.** `identity-text.test.js` and `identity-footer.test.js` banned the word "shield" from the Cleric blurb (the game has no armour shield). The ruling makes the Shield spell's soak part of the blurb, so the guards now strip "your Shield soaks N more HP" before the ban; an armour shield is still refused.

**5. [Process] Pins and engine landed together.** The pins were written first and the engine changes made to turn them green in one pass; the red-first run was not committed as a separate step (the commit is one feat commit for Task 1). Every row's pin was run against the finished code and several caught my own fixture mistakes on the way (an Apprentice's backfire draw, Duplicate Foe's d4+2).

**6. [Handoff for 91.1-05] GAP: wording.** The two built rows that carry a `gap` flag (Elven `race-strike-step`, Cleric `chart-never`) keep a `GAP:` clause recording the fixed finding ("... was cancelled ..., fixed in 91.1-03"), because the ledger test requires a `gap` flag to name a `GAP:` item; the close plan converts them to `exists` or `accepted` as `Status: closed` demands.
