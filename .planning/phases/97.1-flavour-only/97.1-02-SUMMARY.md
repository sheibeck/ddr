# Phase 97.1 Plan 02: Page and settings draw flavour only Summary

The classic page, the Settings sheet and the settings model no longer carry any RULES machinery: combat SPELLS, ITEMS, ABILITIES and SING rows, the find card, the bag-full drop shelf and the chip, UP YOUR SLEEVE and Company cards show the flavour lead only, and the flavour lookups cross one bridge, `window.__mzFlavor`.
