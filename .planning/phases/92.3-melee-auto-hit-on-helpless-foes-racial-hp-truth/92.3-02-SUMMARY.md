# Phase 92.3 Plan 02: Summons auto-hit, old rule docs, the Thief at 50 in the damage limit Summary

A summoned ally and a legacy sheet-less ally now hit a frozen, stunned, held, stopped, asleep or dozing foe automatically like the hero and the Joiners, the dated "5 winning faces" rule docs say the current rule, and the damage-limit mean hero reads the Thief's final 50 (and the Troll's final 75) instead of the old 40.

## What was built

**1. Summons auto-hit.** `engine/combat.js#allyTurn` (the summoned ally) and the legacy sheet-less branch of `#alliesTurn` compute `helplessAutoHit(t, faces)` right after their strike die, with the winning-faces count they already had. When it names a condition the strike lands: the die is still drawn in its place and ignored (so no later main-rng draw moves), the damage is rolled as usual (`level squared + d6`), the best-face shatter of a Skeleton does not fire on a roll that was skipped, and a foe the ally cannot touch (faces 0) stays untouchable (a summon has no magic weapon or dagger, so a magic-only or dagger-only foe is never hit). The `allyStruck` event carries `helpless` only then. A summon's rounds still count down and it still departs on time. The crit never applied to these two (no natural crit in either path), so nothing else changes. The same list (`foeHelplessKind`) is now used by the hero, Joiners, the summon and the legacy ally: frozen, stunned, stopped, asleep, dozing (Doze), and Pommel Strike's stun.

*Display and text.* No new event type or narration branch: `allyStruck` already says "<foe> cannot dodge" in the Oracle and "(N, it cannot dodge)" on the rail when `helpless` is set (plan 01), so a summon reads the same. The text that states who hits automatically named "your melee blows, and your Joiners'"; it now reads "your melee blows, and your Joiners' and summoned allies', hit it automatically" in the five foe chips (Stunned, Asleep, Dozing, the Frozen/Stunned hold, Stopped) and in Stop Time's spell text, with six chained rows in `docs/narrative-pass/why/v-92.3-02.json` and regenerated `NARRATIVE-PASS.md` and `review.html`. `docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md`'s plan-01 "Who" and "Display" bullets are corrected and a "Follow-up (92.3 plan 02)" bullet records the change (unmeasured, like the rule).

**2. Old rule docs.**
- `docs/ROLL-LEDGER.md`: the `[hero-strike:dozing]` Modifier row now states the automatic hit (the p.27 floor is marked superseded); `[hero-strike:stupid]` is corrected (it said "same as dozing", but a Stupid foe has carried no term since Phase 90 plan 04); the 90-05 Stun line ("a held foe is struck on at least 5 winning faces"), the F1 notes and the per-target-rules row carry the supersession; a new section "Phase 92.3: melee at a helpless foe is an automatic hit" lists the helpless conditions, who hits, what is skipped, the crit decision and the display.
- `docs/SPELLS.md`: the Stupidity paragraph ("struck exactly like a dozing one"), the Freeze paragraph ("easier to hit meanwhile"), the Stun and Stop Time "5 winning faces" sentences and the Summon table row (kept equal to `docs/SPELL-AUDIT.md`'s Rolls cell, which a test generates it from) are updated; a new "Phase 92.3" section is at the end. `docs/SPELL-AUDIT.md`'s Summon row names the summon auto-hit.
- Starting-hp sweep: the Troll 105 and Thief 40 or 56 statements in rule and text documents were already corrected by plan 01 (IDENTITY-AUDIT, REQUIREMENTS IDENT-21, checklist A10, DIFFICULTY-RETUNE's post-pass note); a sweep of `docs/`, `.planning/`, `README.md`, `content/`, `engine/`, `src/` and `tools/` found no other. The remaining hits are measured bot tables in `docs/CLASS-PASS.md` and `docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md` (a "40" in a hit-point column of a dated measurement), which are records of what was measured and were left as they were.

**3. The Thief's 50 (and the Troll's 75) in the damage limit.** New `engine/difficulty.js#startWpMeanFor(cls, race = "Human")` returns `{ wp, final }` by the rule `rollCharacter` applies: a race's `flatWP` (Troll 75) wins and is final, a class's `startWP` (Thief 50) is final (an Elven Thief 30), every other start is the unscaled mean for the caller to scale. `heroMeanMaxWpFor` reads it: a final start is not scaled, every class's level-up gains still are (as `checkLevel`). `tools/damage-curve-audit.mjs#classBarAt` reads it the same way. The audit prints only the Human Magic User and Fighter bars, neither of which has a final start, so its printed bars are unchanged; the helper is race-aware so a Troll or Thief bar would agree with `rollCharacter` the day the audit prints one. The Troll is not a term in the mean hero (it is a Human mean over the three classes).

## Human verification

Device checks (Pixel 7, debug build). The damage-limit change has no on-screen text of its own; the first four cover the summon rule and its text.
1. **Summon at a sleeper.** Cast Doze, Noxious Vapor (or throw a sleep staff) on a foe, then cast Summon (a Magic User or Summoner): on the summon's turn the Oracle reads "<ally> lands a hit on <foe> for N hp. <foe> cannot dodge." and the rail "(N, it cannot dodge)". It never misses a sleeper, however many rounds it has.
2. **Summon at a held foe.** Freeze, Stun or Stop Time a foe with a Summon up: the summon's blows land every round of the hold, and roll again once the hold breaks. A Doze wakes on the first hit, so the summon's next blow rolls again.
3. **Untouchable stays untouchable.** A summon at a magic-only foe (a Ghost, a Spectre) that is asleep still misses (it has no magic weapon).
4. **Chip text.** Long-press a Stunned, Asleep, Dozing, Frozen/Stunned-hold or Stopped foe: the line reads "...your melee blows, and your Joiners' and summoned allies', hit it automatically...". The Grimoire row for Stop Time reads the same.
5. **Nothing else moved.** Hero and Joiner blows at a helpless foe still land and read "cannot dodge"; a Senseless, Stupefied, Blind or Weakened foe is still rolled against; a new Troll still starts at 75 in every class, a Human Thief at 50 (an Elven Thief 30). The only number that changed in the engine is the per-visit damage clamp, one hit point lower at every level, which shows only if a single foe's visit total reaches it.
