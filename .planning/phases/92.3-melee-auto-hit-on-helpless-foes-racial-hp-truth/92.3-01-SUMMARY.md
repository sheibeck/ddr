# Phase 92.3 Plan 01: Racial hit points made true, melee that always hits a helpless foe Summary

A new Troll now starts at exactly 75 hit points in every class and a new Thief at exactly 50 (an Elven Thief 30), with the text, a guard test on the shipped dials and the docs saying so; and a hero's or Joiner's melee blow at a frozen, stunned, held, stopped, asleep or dozing foe now hits automatically.

## What was built

**Ruling 1, starting hit points.** `engine/character.js#rollCharacter` reads `RACES[race].flatWP` (the Troll, 75) and `CLASSES[cls].startWP` (a new Thief field, 50) as FINAL starting values: neither `HERO_HP_SCALE` nor the class `hpMul` is applied. The Elven `wpMul` (0.6) still applies to a class's final value. Nothing draws for either (the Thief's zero dice never drew), so no rng position moves. `checkLevel` is unchanged: every level-up gain, a Troll's and a Thief's included, still goes through the dial (the old audit row's "unscaled" claim was false and is corrected). Shipped starts after the change (measured over 240 seeds per combination, pinned by the guard):

| | Human, Dwarven, Wilmsry, Fridgian | Elven | Troll |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magic User | 36 to 49 | 22 to 29 | 75 |
| Fighter | 71 to 81 | 43 to 49 | 75 |
| Thief | 50 | **30** | 75 |

Text: `CLASS_NOTE.Thief` "Fifty Hit Points", `CLASS_NOTE["Magic User"]` "Thirty-six to forty-nine Hit Points, depending on what the d10 pities you with"; the Troll blurb, note and generated footer already said 75 and are now true. Docs: `docs/IDENTITY-AUDIT.md` (the `race-flat-hp` row, its "unscaled" and "a Joiner Troll too" errors, and a Rulings entry), the IDENT-21 note in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`, checklist A10, and `docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md` ("Post-pass hp change", unmeasured, weaker Troll and Thief). `test/unit/starting-hp-text.test.js` (11 tests, never `setIdentityDials`): a truth table over all 143 race x class x sub-class combinations cross-checked against a closed form from the content tables, the sub-class changing nothing, the ruled numbers pinned, level-ups still scaled, registered claims parsed from digits and number words, a coverage scan that fails on any unregistered hit-point number (allowlist: upkeep, soak, the Knight thresholds, the Pilfer d10), the Elven 60% ratio at every class, and the Hero tab's bar inside the range.

**Ruling 2, melee auto-hit on a helpless foe.**

*Conditions (the one list, `derived.js#foeHelplessKind`, in the order `foeTurn` skips them):*
1. a hold (`foe.held`) of kind **frozen** (Freeze, Ice), **stunned** (the Stun spell) or **stopped** (Stop Time, kind "time");
2. **asleep** (`asleep > 0`): the plain sleeps (Noxious Vapor, Insane's nap, a staff's gas, a song), shown Asleep, and **dozing** (Doze, which a hit wakes), shown Dozing;
3. **stunned** by Pommel Strike (`foe.stunned`, one lost turn).

Not helpless: Senseless and Duplicate Foe (the foe still takes its turn), Stupidity and blindness (it still swings), Weaken (the foes act), a Petrify mark (the foe is dead).

*Who and what.* `combat.js#playerStrike` (and so every ability that is a strike with a to-hit roll: Kata, Feint, Overhead Blow, Last Stand, Pommel, Cutpurse, Silent Step) and `#memberStrike` (a Joiner, abilities included) skip the to-hit roll against a helpless foe. Damage is rolled as usual. A foe the striker cannot touch at all (winning faces 0) stays untouchable. Foes still roll to hit a held or sleeping hero or Joiner. Sweep has no to-hit roll and is untouched. Not included: the summoned ally's strike (`allyTurn`) and the legacy sheet-less ally's, since the ruling names the hero and Joiners (one line each to widen if wanted).

*Crit and draw-order decisions.* **Crit: none from the die.** The engine reads a blow's critical off the same die as the hit (a natural best face, `roll >= atLeastFor(weaponCrit, dieN)`), not a separate roll, so with the roll skipped there is no natural crit, and the other readers of that die (Stealth's and the Ninja's roll crits, a Skeleton's best-face shatter) do not fire either. The unconditional criticals stay: a Thief's opening backstab, the Cutthroat's first blow, a forced-crit ability. **Draw order: draw-and-ignore.** The strike die (and Philly's second die) is still drawn in its place and ignored, the pattern the Cat Burglar and Ninja opener already use, so no later main-rng draw moves and the only drift is the outcomes of blows that used to miss and what follows. No derived stream and no new roll. (Philly's `slow` second die and the Con Artist opener are untouched.)

*Display.* `struck` and `allyStruck` gain `helpless` (the condition's name) only when it applies. The Oracle reads "<foe> cannot dodge. You hit <foe> for N hp." with no to-hit range; the rail reads "You hit X (6, it cannot dodge)"; a Joiner's lines likewise ("Ada lands a hit on X (5, it cannot dodge)."); a grouped round says it only when every landed blow was at a helpless foe. The chips (Stunned, Asleep, Dozing, the Frozen/Stunned hold, Stopped) say "your melee blows, and your Joiners', hit it automatically"; the foe card's odds line reads "You hit it automatically" (and the Stunned and Stopped chips gained an effect line like the Asleep one); Stop Time's spell text lost "the top five numbers of your die" and says the same. Ledger rows, an event-variants toggle, the regenerated NARRATIVE-PASS and the SPELL-AUDIT row are in. `docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md` records it as a post-pass rule the user ruled, unmeasured.

## Deviations from Plan

There was no PLAN.md; the CONTEXT's "USER RULING 2026-10-02" was the spec for hit points (it superseded the text-only diagnosis). Deviations from it:

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The Ninja's crit source pin.** `identity-text.test.js` pins `c.sub === "Ninja" && !opening && roll >= atLeastFor(2, dieN)` in the engine source; the first edit put `!helplessHit` in the middle and broke it. The guard now sits at the end of the condition. No behaviour change.

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Stop Time text number.** The spell text said "the top five numbers of your die", bound to `targetStrikeFaces` by the number-binding guard. The ruling removes that number from the text, so the guard's Stop Time fact now binds only the rounds and asserts the held foe is a helpless kind.

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Audit vocabulary.** `docs/IDENTITY-AUDIT.md` has a closed verdict vocabulary tied to `91-NN` plans, so the `race-flat-hp` row keeps its existing verdict and gets the new Engine, Canon and Pinned by cells plus a Rulings entry, rather than a new `92.3` verdict token.

**4. [Scope] Left as is, on purpose.** `docs/ROLL-LEDGER.md` and `docs/SPELLS.md` still carry their historical "a held foe is struck on at least 5 winning faces" sentences (dated change logs, not player text). `tools/damage-curve-audit.mjs` and `engine/difficulty.js#heroMeanMaxWpFor` still read the canon `baseWP` tables (the mean-hero reference, pinned at 41.67 / 46.17 / 50.00 / 54.50 / 60.00), so `ROUND_DAMAGE_CEILING` does not move.

## Human verification

Device checks (Pixel 7, debug build):
1. **Troll hp.** Roll Trolls until you have a Fighter, a Magic User and a Thief: the Hero tab bar and the blurb both read 75 for each (it was 105). The Troll footer says "starts with 75 HP whatever the class". Level a Troll up once: it gains its class's scaled gain as before.
2. **Thief hp.** A Human, Dwarven, Wilmsry or Fridgian Thief starts at 50 and its blurb says "Fifty Hit Points"; an Elven Thief starts at 30 (60% of 50).
3. **Magic User hp.** A Human Magic User starts between 36 and 49 and the blurb says "Thirty-six to forty-nine Hit Points". A Fighter still starts at 71 to 81.
4. **Sleeping foe.** Cast Doze (or throw a Noxious Vapor, or a sleep staff) on a foe, then Fight: every blow lands, the Oracle reads "<foe> cannot dodge. You hit ..." with no to-hit range, and the rail says "(N, it cannot dodge)". A Doze wakes on the first hit, so the next blow rolls again. A Thief's opening blow on a sleeper still crits (backstab); a plain top-of-the-die blow on a sleeper does not.
5. **Held and stopped.** Freeze, Stun and Stop Time a foe: melee lands every blow while the count runs and rolls again once it breaks. Long-press the foe: the odds line reads "You hit it automatically", and the Frozen, Stunned, Stopped and Asleep chips say your melee blows and your Joiners' hit it automatically. Pommel Strike a foe: the Stunned chip says the same and the follow-up blows (yours and a Joiner's) land.
6. **Joiner.** With a Joiner in the party, a blow of the Joiner's at a frozen or sleeping foe reads "... it cannot dodge" and never misses.
7. **Foes still roll.** A held or sleeping hero (a fumbled scroll, a Doze spell turned on you) is still attacked with the foe's normal rolls: nothing about the foe's side changed.
8. **Not helpless.** A Senseless, Stupefied, Blind or Weakened foe is still rolled against (the chip text does not claim an automatic hit).
9. **Stop Time text.** The Grimoire row reads "...your melee blows, and your Joiners', hit it automatically; then time resumes".
