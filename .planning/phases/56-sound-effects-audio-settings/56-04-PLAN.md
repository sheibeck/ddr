## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Settings sheet tap -> persisted setting -> audio device | A user's "off" must be honoured at the device-construction boundary, not merely at the volume. |
| `dispatchWithNarration` -> audio side effect | A new side effect on the seam every action flows through; a throw here would break gameplay, not just sound. |
| DOM pointer events -> unlock + UI tap | An untrusted stream of taps drives both device construction and playback. |

|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-56-13 | Elevation of Privilege | Sound=Off gate | high | mitigate | The gate sits at the module boundary — `unlockSfx()` bails before `open()` — so no `AudioContext` is constructed at all; asserted by an open-call-count-is-zero test across unlock, dispatch and UI tap. |
| T-56-14 | Denial of Service | `dispatchWithNarration` | critical | mitigate | The audio call is fire-and-forget and fully wrapped; `playForDispatch` returns undefined and cannot throw, so a broken audio layer degrades to silence rather than wedging the seam every action flows through. Asserted by the never-throws tests in 56-03. |
| T-56-15 | Denial of Service | document-level pointerdown listener | medium | mitigate | One capture-phase listener that self-guards and does no allocation on the Off path; `unlockSfx()` is idempotent so a tap storm cannot open repeated devices. |
