# Phase 92.2 Plan 01: Strength potion spells, damage visibility, small fixes Summary

The Strength potion's +8 now rides every damage-spell roll alongside the Strength spell's d10 (one helper, `spellStrengthParts`), damage lines say which Strength parts applied, turning Compete off mid-session is described honestly, and a fresh install no longer sees the patch notes.

## What was built

**Ruling 1.** `derived.js#spellStrengthParts(sheet, rng)` returns `{ strength, might, total }`: the spell's d10 (`strengthRoll`, a derived stream) plus `potionMight` (the potion's +8, from the sheet's own live `item:Strength` record). It replaces the bare `strengthRoll` call at all eight damage-spell sites: `magic.js` Earthquake, Fireballs and the thrown branch (Freeze, Fireball, Lightning and the rest), and `combat.js` iceStorm and the Joiner quake, volley and thrown branches. Rolls are drawn in the old order, so the rng is untouched. DoT ticks never call it. A Joiner reads its own sheet, so it gets the potion only when the record is on that sheet. The repro: Freeze, same seed, none / potion / spell / both now reads none, none + 8, spell, spell + 8 (levels 1 and 3, seeds 1 to 8). Melee stays +8. Potion text reads "+8 damage to blows and spells, 25 squares"; the Oracle and rail start lines and the Strong chip say blows and spells.

**Ruling 2.** `struck`, `spellHit` (the thrown spells and Ice), `earthquake` and `volley` gain `might` and `strength`, each only when above 0 (`strengthFields`; `weaponDamageParts` for the blow). The d10 is computed once into a local and that value is reported; the volley event sums its bolts. Oracle: "(incl. +8 Strength potion, +6 Strength)", and the spell parenthetical reads "(the roll +9, for your level, incl. +8 Strength potion, +6 Strength)". Rail: "incl. +8, +6" (the numbers match the Oracle twin, which the VOX-05 adjacency guard requires), also on the folded Freeze line and the multi-blow line. With no field present every line is byte-identical. A Sorcerer's cap or the floor of 1 that swallowed the bonus reports none. Both builders are null-safe for the voice scan.

**Ruling 3.** The clause "Turning Compete off takes full effect the next time you open the game: Play Games stays signed in until the app closes, and nothing reaches the board" is on both Play Games SDK rows of `store-listing/LISTING.md`, the optional column now reads "Compete off: the SDK never starts the next time you open the game" (the phrase pinned in `store-listing.test.js` moved with it, plus a new two-rows assertion), `ACCOUNT_COPY.sheet.offHelp` carries it (pinned in `account-copy.test.js`), and `docs/PLAY-GAMES-SETUP.md` says it.

**Ruling 4.** `patchNotes.js#readNotesLaunch` counts `ddr.bests.v1` as prior data only when it holds a run or is unreadable (`bestsHoldsRuns`, exported from `settings.js`, the 92.1 rule). The settings blob needed no change: `readSettings` writes it on a first boot only for an install that already held player data, and a real first boot (adapter boot, then `readSettings`) writes none; a test pins that. A fresh install is marked, not shown; any save, graveyard, ranked bests, unreadable bests or settings blob, or a stored 2.2.0 seen version, still shows the notes.

## darktier-studio privacy page finding (ruling 3; that repo was read, not edited)

`src/pages/privacy/apps.astro` (commit `0f39ee3`) needs the same clause. Four statements are absolute about Compete off and are not true for the rest of a session in which the player turns it off:

- the lede (lines ~33-35): "with Compete off, nothing is sent and you are not signed in to Google Play Games";
- the "With Compete off" bullet (~130): "No Google Play Games sign-in is asked for and the Play Games software is not started";
- the Google Play Games section (~172): "With Compete off, the Play Games software is not started, so it collects nothing";
- the Data safety summary (~260-261): "optional, because with Compete off the software never starts".

Suggested addition in each place: turning Compete off takes full effect the next time you open the game, Play Games stays signed in until the app closes, and nothing reaches the board. Nothing is wrong about what reaches the board (it is nothing); the gap is the SDK and sign-in staying alive until the app closes. `src/pages/privacy/delete-data.astro` and `terms.astro` were not read for this clause; the page edit is yours to make.
