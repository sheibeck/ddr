## Deviations from Plan

### Orchestrator-relayed user rulings folded in (engine changes; they override the plan's "no engine file" prohibition)

**1. [User ruling 2026-09-28] A failed climb or leap always hurts at least 1** (commit `992b1dfa`)
- **Found during:** Task 3 (relayed while the first START was running; that run was stopped).
- **Change:** `engine/movement.js`: `hurt = Math.max(1, hurt)` after `scaleHazard` on the `!ok` path. Same draws, same order.
- **Tests:** two new movement tests (an all-d20<=2 climb fail hurts 1; a failed leap at the lowest hazard scale hurts at least 1; a 1-HP hero dies of the minimum fall).
- **Measured zero:** fixture roster byte-identical, parity 66/66, no state pin moved. Full suite 7,577/7,577. Declared in FIXTURE-INVENTORY's "Failed climb hurts at least 1" subsection.

**2. [User rulings 2026-09-28] Freeze deals its damage, then freezes 1d4 rounds; a resist stops only the freeze** (commit `582cd6c7`)
- **The shared tail.** `engine/combat.js#freezeFoe`, called by the hero's cast, a scroll's free cast, a Joiner's `allyCast` and the Birch Staff:
  - the d4 is drawn right after the damage whenever the foe survives;
  - then the intel resist (never before the throw for Freeze);
  - then the RULES-18 control resist;
  - then `holdFoe(..., { rounds })`.
  - `holdFoe` gained the optional rolled-rounds argument, and `foeResistsSpell` an optional `extra` payload (`freeze: true`).
- **Retired:** Freeze's frozen-solid kill path. `frozenSolid` stays, because Ice's last tick emits it.
- **Oracle and rail:**
  - "<foe> resists the freeze: the damage lands, the ice doesn't.";
  - "<foe> is frozen for N rounds. Ice now, grudge later.";
  - the rail folds hit, failed resist and hold into "Freeze hits X (4), frozen for N rounds";
  - the Joiner "frozen solid" branches are removed.
  - Voice ledger `q-260928-frz.json` added (`--check-ledgers --after --coverage`: 0 errors; the other voice checks: 0 hits). The review page is regenerated.
- **Bot:** Freeze scores DISABLE at every depth.
- **Records:** ROLL-LEDGER.md (the new d4 draw position and draw order), SPELLS.md, and FIXTURE-INVENTORY's "Freeze deals damage and freezes 1d4 rounds" subsection.
- **Gate after the change:** full suite 7,589/7,589, parity 66/66, `boot:check` PASS.
- **Interpretation calls:**
  - The d4 is drawn before the resists (whenever the hit foe survives, resisted or not). This follows "a NEW d4 draw, taken right after the damage, only when the foe survives" and keeps the RULES-18 main-draw parity.
  - The Birch Staff has no to-hit and no damage, so its resist means no effect and the line is the plain "no effect".
  - The Birch Staff's `txt` was rewritten: "freezes up to 2 squares of opponents for d4 rounds apiece, unless they resist; then they are just cold and angry". It carries a voice-ledger row.
