<objective>
Move the wall/crevice decision into the engine (CLIMB-01, CLIMB-02): every step toward a wall or crevice pauses on one pending decision with no dice drawn; the player then commits (CLIMB IT / LEAP IT), spends a carried tool (USE LADDER / USE ROPE, unchanged), or turns back at no cost. The commit replays exactly the roll the old single step made, so the only difference is WHEN the player chooses.

Purpose: the user's option-B ruling (2026-09-24, accepted in discuss 2026-09-25): "a pre-roll card with CLIMB/LEAP IT, USE LADDER/ROPE and TURN BACK, no rng until commit, and TURN BACK costs nothing." This is the engine half; the rail card is 78-03, the declarations and relaunch probe are 78-04.

Output: the engine rule and action, the shared odds function, narration entries, the bot's answer, the fixed tests and pins, and before/after readouts.
</objective>
