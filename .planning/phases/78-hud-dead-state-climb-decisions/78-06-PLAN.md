## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **HUD-02 edge probe: unclassified, reviewed manually.** Resolved as the truths above.
- **The MAP tab is inert too.** HUD-02 lists "the other tabs"; the death card (THAT IS THAT) lives on the map, so once the player leaves it for the Oracle or the DEAD tab they cannot return to it. The ways back to the title stay on every live surface (the DEAD tab's new BURY THEM, the ☰ NEW CHARACTER and SAVE & QUIT), and FINAL SHEET is on both the death card and the DEAD tab. If the user prefers the MAP tab to stay reachable only to re-show the death card, that is a one-line change to the lockdown set.
- **FINAL SHEET is also on the death card** (discretion): the requirement names the DEAD screen, and the death card is the first dead screen the player sees; offering it there makes the "explore your character" intent (the user's 2026-09-23 words) reachable without a detour.
- **The sheet is a new overlay, not the Hero tab in a read-only mode.** The Hero tab renders into fixed shell ids with live action rows; a separate pure renderer over the same view models is simpler to prove read-only and cannot drift into a dispatch.
