## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **DESIGN QUESTION FOR THE USER (HUD-07 cannot be built exactly as CONTEXT words it).** CONTEXT says the mark shows on "the 4 orthogonally adjacent squares that hold something ALIVE: foes/encounters with creatures, would-be joiners and the faerie". Two facts in the code make that impossible as written: (1) the four adjacent squares are ALWAYS already revealed (engine/derived.js#revealRadius is at least 1, and the dark view window is the 3x3 around the party), so a mark there would sit on a square the map already shows; (2) a red dot's contents are not decided until the party steps on it (engine/encounters.js#encounterDot draws its d8 table and d10 row at that moment), so "alive" cannot be known without peeking at the dice, which CONTEXT forbids ("no rng"). This plan's default, which keeps every CONTEXT rule that CAN hold (pure, no rng, through walls, never says what, traps and chests silent, gone once revealed or resolved) and the requirement's own words ("squares that hold an encounter"): hear every unresolved encounter dot within 3 squares that the map is not showing. Alternatives: (B) pre-decide each dot's contents on a derived rng stream at floor generation so "alive" is knowable (a large engine change: every dot outcome moves, a declared divergence and readouts); (C) drop the map mark and keep only a flavour line. The user should confirm A or choose B/C before this plan executes (78-09 is the phase's last wave).
- **HUD-07 edge probe: unclassified, reviewed manually** under default A (the truths above).
- **Hero only.** A party member with Acute Hearing does not add hearing (CONTEXT says "a hero with Acute Hearing").
- **"Fades" means the mark is gone on the next paint** once the square is shown or resolved; no animation (so reduced motion is unaffected).
