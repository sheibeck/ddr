## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Four coordinates, not three.** ROADMAP criterion 2 names the HP and hit slopes and the elite HP per rank. The moved plan also searches `CONTROL_AT_DEPTH.resistPerDepth`, because the rotation target in the same criterion depends on it, and the dial's JSDoc says it is "searched by the tail sweep". The design is reused as it stands.
- **Why `autonomous: false`.** The objective is ruled, so nothing pauses before the search. The plan is non-autonomous only because a failure pattern must be handed back in the middle of the sweep. The orchestrator answers a search-parameter hand-back itself (standing authority) and relays to the user only a proposal that would change a rule, a target, a ruled value or the dial set.
- **The targets may be out of reach of these dials.** "Depth 30 basically never" may need more than the knee slopes, the elite HP step and the resistance, for example if ROUND_DAMAGE_CEILING flattens the hit slope. That shows up as a failure pattern and is handed back.
- **Evaluation cost (planning-time probe).** A deep-start run takes 0.1-0.5 s, so the eight non-fresh slices take about 3-6 min per evaluation with 4 workers. The gated 1,000-seed fresh slice adds about 15-25 min to each candidate that meets every slice target. A block of 10 runs about 40-70 min, plus any gated fresh slices.
- **Cycles instead of rewriting history.** An adjustment that changes the walk starts a new cycle with its own log, seeded with the best row so far, so the cycle's start is replayed from the log, never re-evaluated. Every log stays append-only.
