## User ruling (2026-09-27): no healing on descent
- "We shouldn't heal at all when we go down a floor." HERO_REGEN_PER_FLOOR goes to 0 (identity) before this phase runs; that engine change lands in Phase 79 as a quick fix.
- The baseline and FINAL readouts measure the game without it. The sweep must NOT search over HERO_REGEN_PER_FLOOR or re-enable it; the dial is locked at 0.
- The v2.0 baseline HAD 0.25 regen. The record must say so when comparing v2.0 with FINAL, because part of the difference comes from this ruling.
- Implemented in 79-02c: the dial and the event are removed, so there is nothing to lock or search.
