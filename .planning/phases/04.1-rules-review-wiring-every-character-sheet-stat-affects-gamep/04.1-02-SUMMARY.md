# Phase 04.1 Plan 02: Fix the c.dr dead-code bug (Sewing + Master of Arms) Summary

**Replaced the never-true `c.dr > 0` gate in `engine/movement.js` newDay with a live `skillTier(c, "Sewing") || c.sub === "Master of Arms"` check, and added Master of Arms' missing flat +2 weapon-damage bonus to `engine/derived.js` weaponDamage.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~25 min
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 4

## Accomplishments
- Thief **Sewing** (tier 1: d6 back up to 4 times; tier 2: d6+3 back up to 6 times) now actually patches armour during `newDay`, gated by a real `skillTier` check instead of the always-false `c.dr > 0`.
- Fighter subclass **Master of Arms**' "+3 repaired-yourself" armour patch (the pre-existing `d6+3` branch) is now reachable via `c.sub === "Master of Arms"`, no longer dead code.
- Master of Arms' headline **"+2 with every weapon"** — previously unimplemented anywhere in the engine — is now a flat additive in `weaponDamage`, keeping the sheet's DAMAGE preview (`viewModels.damageBracket`) in sync automatically since it mirrors `weaponDamage`.
- The old bare `else { rng.d(4) }` fallback (only ever reachable under the dead gate) is removed entirely, so characters with neither Sewing nor Master of Arms get **no** armour patch and consume **no** RNG draw — closing the T-04.1-03 elevation-of-privilege threat.
- Both changes are logged in-code as `DELIBERATE RULES CHANGE (04.1-02, 2026-09-09, RULE-02)` comments, following the `engine/movement.js:239` precedent.
- Added 5 new tests to `test/unit/movement.test.js` (tier-1 patch + clamp, tier-2 patch, tier-2 patch-exhausted boundary, Master of Arms patch, and the "neither capability -> no patch" regression) and 1 new test to `test/unit/combat.test.js` (Master of Arms +2 weaponDamage delta).
- Full suite: **505/505** passing (up from the prior 499/499 baseline — 6 new tests, 0 removed, 0 failing).

## Decisions Made
- The dead `c.dr` field is not resurrected anywhere (no writer added); the gate now reads a real capability instead of a phantom field, per CONTEXT.md's explicit instruction and the plan's `key_links` note.
- Removed rather than further-gated the bare-else fallback, since keeping it (even behind a tighter condition) would still risk future drift back toward granting free armour patches to arbitrary characters — full removal is the cleanest way to satisfy the T-04.1-03 mitigation.
- Chose `weaponDamage` (not `toHit`) for the Master of Arms +2, per the plan's stated preference ("keep it simple and documented... satisfies the sheet's stated identity and keeps the damageBracket preview in sync").
