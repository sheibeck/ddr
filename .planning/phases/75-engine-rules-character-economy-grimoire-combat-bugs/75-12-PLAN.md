- RULES-15: a day refills spell books only when the party eats, and the player is told when it didn't.
- RULES-12: a tile left unresolved because a wandering monster interrupted the step resolves once the fight and its spoils are settled, if the hero is still standing on it.

Purpose:
- The user: "when you don't have enough rations, you do not recover spells. You need a good night's sleep!"
- The user: "if a wandering monster check triggers when you step on an icon, we need to resolve the icon after combat ends."

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **RULES-12, edge probe: unclassified, reviewed manually.**
  - **"After loot/find" means after they are settled.** The tile resolves on the first action after the fight ends at which no spoils pile, find or store is open, so the loot card comes first and the tile second.
  - **Flee still resolves.** Flee never moves the hero, so a fled hero is still on the tile and it resolves (the todo's recommended reading).
  - **Walking away ends it.** A hero who walks off first leaves the feature on the map for later.
  - **The exit counts.** An exit tile resumed after a fight descends, as a step onto it would.
  - **Transient until Phase 76.** pendingTile is reset to null on load: combat itself does not survive a relaunch until SAV-06, so Phase 76 persists the two together.
- **RULES-15 in practice.** Making camp already refuses without enough rations, so an unfed day only happens on the automatic 100-square day. The rule is written in newDay, so it covers both.
