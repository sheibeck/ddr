## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **RULES-13, edge probe: unclassified, reviewed manually.**
  - **Not a magic weapon.** A wielded staff is not a magic weapon for magic-only and dagger-only foes. Its magic is its charged power, not an enchantment, so c.magicWpn stays 0. Flagged for the user.
  - **Finds stay in the bag.** A found staff is never auto-wielded; the player equips it from the Gear tab (75-11).
  - **One staff at a time.** Wielding a second staff swaps the first into the bag.
  - **The store keeps its hands off.** The store never sells staves, and its weapon auto-equip is suppressed while a staff is wielded, so a purchase is bagged instead of trading the staff away.
  - **Pilfers can't wield.** A Pilfer is a Thief and can never wield a staff, so RULES-09's Pilfer fumble (Phase 75.1) never meets a wielded staff.
- **Still d8, need 0, crit 1.** No canon reason was found for different numbers, so a staff uses 75-CONTEXT's figures.
