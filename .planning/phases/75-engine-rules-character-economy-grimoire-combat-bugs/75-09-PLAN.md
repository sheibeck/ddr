## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Bot policy: first staff wins.** The bot wields the first staff it carries and keeps it; a second staff stays in the bag. This is simplest and deterministic. It never swaps a staff out for a mundane weapon, because a staff's d8 matches the best Magic-User-legal weapon (the Spear) and adds its power.
- **Combat ITEMS is 75-11's.** It shows a bagged staff greyed and the wielded one as EQUIPPED. Until 75-11 lands, a bagged staff row in combat still dispatches, and the engine answers with the notWielded line (never silent).
