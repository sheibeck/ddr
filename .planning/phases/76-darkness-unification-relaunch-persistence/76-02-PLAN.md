Purpose: a light source means the same thing on every surface (ROADMAP Phase 76 criterion 2), and the player can tell at a glance both that the area is dark and that their light is doing the work.

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **When the tint shows.** "While a waiver holds the dark back" is read as "while `darkWaiver(S.c)` is non-null", so every rendered dark tile takes the tint while your light is live, not only the one you stand on. This keeps one rule on every surface. The alternative (tint only while standing in the dark) is a small change if the user prefers it.
- **The chip's scope is unchanged.** The DARK chip still appears only while the Table-7 counter (`c.darkFor`) runs; natural dark tiles are shown by the vignette and the tint, not a chip.
- **Tint colour (Claude's discretion per CONTEXT).** Solid hexes, not an alpha overlay, so the canvas fill stays one `fillRect` per cell and the recording-canvas tests can pin it. The warm cast sits between `floorDark` and `floor`.
