Purpose: on the Pixel 7 the hero-death clip is too loud, and the footsteps and the title theme are too soft. Every one-shot plays at the device master gain today, so each clip's loudness is baked into its mp3. The theme runs at MUSIC_GAIN 0.5.
- D-01: one tunable CLIP_GAIN table in code.
- D-02: MUSIC_GAIN goes to 0.9.
- D-03: MASTER / MUSIC / EFFECTS sliders under the Sound row, persisted and applied live.

Output: the sfx.js level layer (table, buses, live levels), three new settings keys, the slider rows in the settings sheet, and their tests.

Planner rulings (record in the SUMMARY):
- R-01, "removed entirely when Off" (D-03): the slider group is static markup carrying the `hidden` attribute while Sound is Off. The existing `.mw-settings-row[hidden]{display:none}` rule takes it out of layout, tab order and the accessibility tree, so nothing is rendered. No DOM insertion or removal is needed.
- R-02, live vs persisted (D-03): each `input` event re-applies an in-memory settings object (the mirror plus the dragged value) through applySettings and never writes storage. The `change` event (release) persists once through writeSetting. The ui-tap preview plays on EFFECTS release only.
- R-03, foe-die (D-01): `death` is the hero-death cue (EVENT_CLIP_GROUP `died` → death) and `foe-die` is `foeKilled`. So foe-die keeps the 1.0 default and gets no entry.
- R-04, the effects bus and music: the theme's gain node still feeds the master gain directly, not the effects bus. So MASTER scales music and effects, EFFECTS scales one-shots only, and MUSIC scales the theme only.
