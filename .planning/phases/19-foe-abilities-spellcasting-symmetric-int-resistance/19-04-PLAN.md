## Encounter specs (D-15) — the five rows the determinism suite forces

Tier is forced through `startCombat`'s own arithmetic: `maxLvl = clamp(min(c.level, floor.depth), 1, 5)` and each foe's `lvl = maxLvl` or `maxLvl - 1` (a d4 of 1 drops one tier), so setting `state.c.level = state.floor.depth = tier` after `newRun(seed)` makes the wanted roster reachable and the seed search does the rest.

| key | forced type | tier | caster wanted | roster at that tier (content/bestiary.js) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `humans-t2` | Humans | 2 | Krupke | China Wolf, Krupke |
| `magical-t4` | Magical | 4 | Drudge | Drudge (sole entry; tier 3 has no caster) |
| `demons-t5` | Demons | 5 | Djinni | Djinni (sole entry; tier 4: Djinni, Ghost, Spectre) |
| `walking-dead-t5` | Walking Dead | 5 | Vampire | Vampire (sole entry) |
| `beasts-t5` | Beasts | 5 | Stalka Beast | Dread Lock, Stalka Beast |

A spec's pinned `seed` is the FIRST seed in 1..300 whose post-`startCombat` roster contains a foe named `want` with a non-empty `abilities` array. The executor measures each seed once, writes it as a literal, and the suite's first test re-derives and re-asserts it on every run.
