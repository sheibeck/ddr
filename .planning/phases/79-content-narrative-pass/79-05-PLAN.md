## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **A mishap on a 1 stays a 1.** Roll-high canon keeps "a 1 is always the worst face" (docs/ROLL-LEDGER.md Phase 73 verdicts), so a text that says a natural 1 fumbles, backfires or breaks is correct and stays; only a text that says a low face SUCCEEDS is roll-under.
- **Bare signed numbers are fine now.** The ledger's old worry about "−2 to hit" without a cue is closed by Phase 74's sign rule (+ is always better for the player). This plan checks such numbers agree with the engine rather than adding cues.
- **Bestiary notes may reach parity.** A foe's `sp.note` is part of a live fight's state; if a reworded note belongs to a fixture-exposed foe, it gets a named carve-out rather than a fixture edit.
