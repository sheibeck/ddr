<objective>
Run the narrative pass over the lines a player reads when a run ends and when they compare it: death causes, epitaphs, leaderboard and placement copy, account copy and the quip banks (VOX-05), fixing any roll phrasing they carry (ROLL-04).

Purpose: the death screen and the boards are where the game's voice is loudest and where a player most wants the plain fact (what killed me, where did I place). Every line keeps its sarcasm if it already says the fact; the ones that do not are rewritten.

Output: audited banks, a small death-copy guard test, re-pinned tests, and the ledger.
</objective>
