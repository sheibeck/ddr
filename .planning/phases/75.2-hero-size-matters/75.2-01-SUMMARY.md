# Phase 75.2 Plan 01: The size step, the signature masks, and what size does to damage Summary

**Size is now a real engine stat (RULES-11): race sets a base step from `content/races.js`'s existing `size` field, items add to it, and every resolved step moves weapon damage by 2 and a foe's winning faces by 1 — except that a race's own defining trait always survives its own base step (the Dwarven +2 damage survives Small; the Elven thin bones survive despite Small's −2 damage applying), and a Joiner gets size from its own race by the identical rule.**
