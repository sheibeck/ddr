## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Enlarge gets its own activation kind, `enlarge`.** Sharing the Gauntlet's `giant` kind would make the bot's "skip if already live" check treat the two as one buff and block stacking; a separate kind keeps each item's chip and narration its own.
- **The two items stack.** 75.2-CONTEXT's step scale is "extended as items stack"; each item is its own record, so both live is +2 steps.
- **Ruled by the user (2026-09-25, after planning):** item steps are never masked by a race's signature (75.2-01's `sizeAxes` masks the race base only), so a Dwarf using the Gauntlet gains +2 damage on top of its kept +2, and an Elf gains one foe face on top of thin bones.
- **Enlarge is weaker for most heroes now** (+2 damage and easier to hit, where it was +4 damage with no downside). Its price (75) is unchanged; no compensation, per the user.
- **Edge probe (RULES-11):** ordering (two size chips in insertion order) and empty (an old save's record, a chip with no size-stepping item) are covered by the truths above.
