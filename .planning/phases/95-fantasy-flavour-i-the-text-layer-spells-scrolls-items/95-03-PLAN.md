Writer's rules (CONTEXT 'Tone'; enforced by test/unit/flavor-layer.test.js, the safety scan and the hygiene checks):
1. One sentence; at most 100 characters (aim at 90 or fewer); exactly one sentence-ending mark, at the end; colons, semicolons and commas are fine.
2. The house voice: sarcastic, deadpan, family-friendly. No profanity, gore or adult content, and nothing from content/safety-wordlist.js (avoid bloody, damn, crap, ass, balls, bastard and the like).
3. No numbers in any form: no digits, no dN, no %, none of two…twenty, thirty…ninety, hundred, once, twice, half, double; avoid "one" as a count (it is allowed only as an idiom such as "no one"); no durations, ranges or amounts in disguise ("for a day", "doubles", "the whole floor" is fine, "fifty squares" is not).
4. Hint at what it does in plain words; never contradict the rules text; never promise more than it does; never restate the rules it hides.
5. Every line different from every other line in the whole layer (a test checks); siblings stay distinguishable at a glance so a player can still choose: Heal / Major Heal / Regeneration; Doze / Stun / Senseless / Stop Time / Weaken / Blind / Stupidity / Shrink / Size of the Behemoth / Duplicate Foe; Freeze / Ice / Fireball / Fireballs / Lightning / Mangle / Earthquake / Acid; Shield / Bubble / Mirror Self / Door Illusion; Sense Danger / Sense Presence / Map the Floor; Turn Walking Dead / Plane Gate; Strength (spell) vs Strength (potion); Death (spell) vs Death (potion); Fly vs the flight items of 95-04; Healing vs Xtra Healing; Cure Poison vs Cure Disease; Invisible vs the invisibility items of 95-04.
6. A spell line never starts with its niche label (healing, defensive, control…), and no line equals its row's txt.
7. House spelling and typography: British spelling ("armour", "colour"); no doubled spaces, no space before punctuation; no "WP"; no retired working title and no "Maze Master"; plain apostrophes and quotes as the content files already use.
8. Heal is exactly: Closes wounds the polite way: quickly, and without asking how you got them.
