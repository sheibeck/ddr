- 'Data shape and guards': every view-model field the guards read stays byte-identical; the new fields are additive.
- 'Data shape and guards', weapons and armour: one flavour line per type; the generated stat line is their rules layer and sits behind RULES.

The phase invariant this plan follows (the same one 95-05 used for the combat item rows): a RULES body holds exactly the text that slot printed before Phase 95, so nothing the player could read before is lost, and the flavour plus any functional tag (usable-by) is what shows first.

- The armour WORN row keeps its wear note (hp left, destroyed, under the cloak): it is live state, read at a glance like a Phase 94 label. Armour's flavour shows on its BAG card and in its Gear sheet.

The enchantment still shows in the row's value cell ("d8 +2").
- A jewel or cloak SWAP FOR or EQUIP candidate in the sheet now describes the candidate by its flavour; its rules are one tap away in its own sheet.
