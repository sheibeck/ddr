# Phase 94.1: Joiner Level Follows Depth - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-03
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

A Joiner you meet is never more experienced than a hero usually is at that depth (JOIN-01). Its level is still rolled, but the floor caps it: at most ceil(depth / 3), at least 1, at most 5. A Joiner already travelling with you keeps pace as you go deeper. Engine rule change plus its follow-through: hp, abilities, Joiner lines, the fair bot, tests and the 2.4.0 DRAFT patch notes.

Source: todo `2026-10-03-joiner-level-follows-depth` (user, 2026-10-03): "I find that i'm usually still level 1 by the time I reach floor 4-ish. So, Joiners should be at most depth/3 minimum 1 for level."

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### The level rule at the meeting (user ruling, 2026-10-03)
- **A cap, not an exact level.** The user: "you should never find a level 5 Joiner at level 5 dungeon. Max level on find should be dungeon depth / 3 (min. 1)."
- `lvl = min(SPELL_LEVEL_TABLE[d10 - 1], band(depth))`, where `band(depth) = clamp(ceil(depth / 3), 1, 5)`:
  floors 1–3 → always 1; 4–6 → at most 2; 7–9 → at most 3; 10–12 → at most 4; 13+ → at most 5 (the table's own maximum).
- The band replaces today's cap (`state.floor.depth`) in `engine/encounters.js#meetJoiner`; export `band` as one named helper (e.g. `joinerLevelCap(depth)`) so the engine, the re-level rule below and the tests read the same function.
- **The d10 is still drawn and used.** Draw shape and order are unchanged (the Phase 53 JOIN-02 pattern), so no parity fixture moves (no replay site meets a Joiner anyway). Confirm with the full suite; declare in `test/parity/FIXTURE-INVENTORY.md` as a zero-moved rules change.
- Everything downstream keeps reading the one `lvl` binding: `grantLevelAbilities`, the `20 * lvl + d20` wp rolls, `c.joiner`, `pendingJoiner`, `joinerMet` / `joinerRefused`.
- The bot's `forceParty` and the dev start-at-depth recruit call `meetJoiner`, so they follow with no special path.

### Joiners keep pace as you descend (user ruling, 2026-10-03: "Re-level Joiners")
- A Joiner already in the party gains levels as the party reaches deeper bands, up to 5.
- **Claude's call on the shape (flag it in the plan; the user can overrule):** on arriving at a floor whose band is higher than the previous floor's band (floors 4, 7, 10, 13), each Joiner in the party gains **one** level (max 5). A weak roll keeps its place under the band instead of jumping to it, and the hero's own levelling is untouched. If descent ever skips floors, it's one level per band crossed.
- Level-up effects: `level`/`lvl` +1; max hp +20 and current hp +20 (flat, matching the `20 * lvl` part of the meeting formula, no rng); the new level's abilities via `grantLevelAbilities` on its derived stream (never the main rng). A Magic User Joiner's spell access follows its new level wherever spell gates read level.
- No main-rng draws. A new event (e.g. `joinerLevelUp { name, sub, lvl }`) gets an `EVENT_NARRATION` entry and a rail/Oracle line in the house voice (coverage guard). A Joiner card and the Company panel show the new level.
- Saved games: tolerant load only; the level lives on the existing party member sheet (no new serialized field beyond what members already carry; carve out anything new in the `*Comparable()` fns).

### Text, proof and notes
- No change to the meeting line: `joinerMet` already says "a level N …".
- Patch notes (`docs/patch-notes/2.4.0.md`, DRAFT): one Joiners line, old → new: a d10 Level Table roll capped at the floor → the same roll capped at the floor's band (1 on floors 1–3, at most 2 on 4–6, 3 on 7–9, 4 on 10–12, 5 from 13), and Joiners with you gain a level each time you reach floor 4, 7, 10 and 13.
- Tests: rewrite `test/unit/joiner-level-cap.test.js` to pin the cap at every band edge (depths 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13, 20), hp and abilities following the level, and the draw count unchanged; add tests for the descent re-level (one level per band crossed, max 5, hp +20, abilities granted, no main-rng draw, solo runs byte-identical).
- Fair bot: one `--party` readout before and after, recorded in the SUMMARY. Information only; no retune gate.
- Docs that describe the Joiner level (ROLL-LEDGER, DIFFICULTY-RETUNE, any others a grep finds) are updated.

### Claude's Discretion
- The re-level shape above (+1 per band crossed) and the event/line wording.
- Helper name and where `descend` hooks the re-level (engine/movement.js#descend or the floor-arrival path).
