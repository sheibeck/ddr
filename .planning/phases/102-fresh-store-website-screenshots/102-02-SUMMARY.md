## Findings the user should know

1. **7-inch death panel hides the epitaph.** At 960x540 the game's own `.cb-mid` (the dead hero's lines) flex-shrinks to zero height under the Earned strip and buttons, so "Cause of death: ..." cannot be drawn; it is fine on phone and 10-inch. The spec waives it for tab7 only. This is real game layout at that window size, not a harness effect (reported, not changed: no game edits in this phase).
2. **Dev Delver appears** on the board shot at all three sizes (rank 5 on every size, rank 8 also on phone and 10-inch). Avoiding it is impossible: the dev seed holds exactly ten runs, all ten are shown, ranking by depth, days, kills or wilmst gives the same order, and any filter leaves fewer than six rows. Names from the dev seed are the only names on the board; no live board is reached.
3. **Oracle after real moves writes nothing** on explored floor, so the tablet deep shot shows the Oracle's two real lines and no moves were taken (the plan allowed moves; none helps).
4. **Combat log:** the log window clips its oldest line at the top on phone and 7-inch (game design); on 10-inch all three lines are whole.
