<objective>
Deliver the graveyard/run-history screen (VOX-03): wire the existing prototype graveyard panel to re-fetch the persisted tombstones from durable storage on open (and immediately after a death), instead of trusting an in-memory `graves` mirror that the two independent death write paths (the classic script's bury() and the engine adapter's persistGrave()) can silently leave stale. Reuse Phase 2 persistence (window.mzStorage / native Preferences) and Phase 3's bury-all-causes — no new persistence work. Show each fallen adventurer's name, class/race, cause of death, depth reached, and sarcastic epitaph; persist across app restart.

Purpose: Close Pitfall 6 — after an engine-routed death, GRAVE_KEY is correct but the open panel can show stale/missing data until a full page reload. The fix is architectural (re-fetch from storage on open), not a new persistence layer, and it sidesteps the two-writer synchronization problem entirely.

Output: presentation/graveyard.js (pure read/normalize), the mazeworld.html re-fetch wiring, the tools/build-www.mjs change that ships presentation/ to the browser build, and the VOX-03 tests.

## Slice framing (MVP)
This is slice 3 of 3 and is INDEPENDENT of the voice generator (05-01/05-02): the tombstone's epitaph is already produced and persisted by engine/death.js at death time, so the graveyard only displays stored data. It runs in Wave 1 in parallel with 05-01 (no shared files).

**As a** player, **I want to** open a graveyard and see my past adventurers — how they died, how deep they got, and their epitaph — surviving app restarts, **so that** each doomed run leaves a mark I can look back on.
