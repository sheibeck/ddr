<objective>
Restructure the Leaderboards panel per the user rulings of 2026-09-24:

- **BOARD-14:** GRAVEYARD is folded into ME.
- **BOARD-12:** three scope chips, ME | ALL | FRIENDS, replace the hidden local scope.
- **BOARD-11:** signed in with Compete ON, the panel opens on ALL.
- **BOARD-13:** LINEAGE becomes ME-only, moves to the end of the rail, and never reads the global DEEPEST sample.

Purpose: the player always knows whose runs they are looking at. That is a direct cause of the device report "my own run didn't show up on my own boards": the local scope could only be reached by re-tapping the active chip. The boards Play Games cannot back honestly go away.

Output: the edits in files_modified and their tests.
</objective>
