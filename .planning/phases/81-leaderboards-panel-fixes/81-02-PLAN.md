<objective>
Retire LEANEST everywhere in code (BOARD-17, user ruling 2026-09-24).

Why it has to go: steps per floor lets a 1-step death top the board, and "deepest floor, then fewest steps" is exactly DEEPEST's ordering. That leaves no honest LEANEST.

Remove it from:
- engine/records.js: BOARD_IDS, RANKED_BOARDS, compareRuns, the bests record;
- src/browser/boardScores.js: SUBMIT_BOARDS, SCORE_ORDER, boardScore, scoreFallback;
- content/leaderboards.js and content/boards.js;
- the board rail;
- the global controller.

An old `ddr.bests.v1` with a `lean` list, and queued `pgsQueue` entries with a `lean` score or ack, load tolerantly. The `lean` part is dropped and never submitted.

Purpose: nothing is ever submitted to the Season-1 LEANEST board (`CgkIlvbN0YYPEAIQAw`) again, so the user can delete it in Play Console after the update ships. 81-03 writes that step into the docs.

Output: the edits above plus their tests. The docs are 81-03's.
</objective>
