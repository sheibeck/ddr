## Accepted decisions (user, 2026-10-04: "Accept all" on each area)

### Area 1: Rotation and Android plumbing
- **Phone rotation:** a Settings choice "Screen: Portrait / Rotate", default Portrait on phones. The manifest carries no orientation lock; on a phone (smallest width < 600dp) the runtime `ScreenOrientation.lock` (`src/browser/nativeChrome.js:239-240`) honours the setting; tablets, foldables and Chromebooks always follow the device. Play's large-screen notice clears because the restriction leaves the manifest.
- **`appCategory="game"`:** keep it (honest category; it no longer carries the orientation lock).
- **Activity recreation:** keep every `configChanges` entry so rotation, fold and resize never recreate the WebView (open sheet, tab, camera and combat stay put); a test pins the manifest.
- **Viewport units and insets:** switch `100vh` to `100dvh`; honour the left and right safe-area insets on the HUD, tab bar and rail (landscape cutouts, side nav bars).

### Area 2: Landscape play layout (phone on its side, ~360dp tall)
- **Layout:** side-rail layout. The tab bar becomes a vertical strip on the edge (navigation-rail pattern), the HUD compresses to one row across the top, the maze viewport fills the rest, and the rail card docks in a right-hand side panel instead of covering the bottom of the map. Nothing stacks under the map.
- **Combat, store, camp and the encounter panel:** a right-hand panel beside the map (~45% width, scrolls), so the fight and the map are both visible.
- **Sheets and non-map tabs (Hero, Gear, Oracle, Dead, leaderboards, Settings):** a centred readable column (max ~640px) at full height; Hero and Gear flow into two columns where the content splits naturally (dossier / ability lists, worn / bag).
- **Arrow pad:** stays in the bottom corner on the `padSide` setting at the same size; with the rail card in the side panel the pad no longer hides whenever a card shows.

### Area 3: Tablets, foldables and Chromebook windows
- **What decides the layout:** window size classes, never the device. Compact width (< 600dp) = phone portrait stack; short window (height < 480dp) = the Area 2 landscape layout; medium width (600–839dp: portrait tablet, unfolded foldable) = the portrait stack, widened; expanded (≥ 840dp wide and ≥ 480dp tall) = two panes. Resizing a Chromebook window or folding is just a re-layout.
- **The 480 px column:** the letterbox goes. The map fills its pane; text-heavy panels (sheets, store, Hero, Gear) cap at a readable width (~640px) and centre in their pane. `tools/letterbox-check.mjs` becomes a layout check across the size classes.
- **Two panes on wide screens:** map plus a persistent side pane. On expanded windows the map stays visible on the left; the right pane shows the rail card, the encounter/combat panel, and whichever of Hero, Gear or Oracle is chosen.
- **Map scale on big screens:** cells scale up with the window (capped), on top of the existing text-size scale; a tablet shows the same explored maze, larger and easier to tap. Fog still limits what is seen, so difficulty does not change.
