## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Freeze and Stone hold instead of kill past the knee (ruled by the user, 2026-09-25, 75.3-CONTEXT "Design rulings after planning").** In the engine today a landed Freeze, Ice's last tick, Petrify and the stone staves END the foe; from floor 13 they hold it for 3 rounds (holdRounds, a ruled value, never searched) instead, and deep foes also get the resistance roll first. Floors 1-12 are unchanged.
- **Only indefinite controls are capped.** Past the knee a control that would last the whole fight or longer (Freeze, Ice's freeze and stone kills, Stupidity, Blind, the Walnut Staff's weaken, the Birch and Cedar staves' 99-round sleep, the Lullaby's 24) lasts holdRounds (3 at the start). Short rolled durations (Doze, Stun, Weaken, Vapor's sleep, Insane's sleep, Thunder) keep their roll, so their spell texts stay true; every control, short or long, can be resisted.
- **The knee is the same as RULES-17's (ruled by the user, 2026-09-25):** floor 12 on today's rules, floor 13 the first harder floor, with the first resist face and the hold.
- **The unclassified edge probe for RULES-18 was reviewed manually** and resolved into the boundary, precision, stream and cap truths above; flagged for the user's review.
- **Excluded "and the like" rows X1-X7** are listed with reasons; the user may want Death, Vapor's kill face and Plane Gate under the same rule.
- **Weaken is one roll for the room**, keyed on the target the caster aimed at (Weaken is a combat-wide flag, not per foe); every live foe is marked Unmoved when it is shaken off.
- **Resist odds are not added to the foe details card** (75.3-CONTEXT: no UI beyond the rolls and chips these rules print); the roll shows in the Oracle each time.
