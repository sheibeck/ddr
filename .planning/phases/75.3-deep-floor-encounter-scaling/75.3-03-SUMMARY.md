## Decisions Made
- The knee's branch guard requires BOTH `kneeDepth` and `perDepthAfter` to be finite AND `perDepthAfter !== perDepth` AND `d > kneeDepth` — this is what makes a dial with no knee fields at all (`HAZARD_SCALE`, `ABILITY_THREAT`) and an identity override (`perDepthAfter === perDepth`) both fall through to the pre-existing single-slope expression with zero special-casing at the call site.
- `foeTierFor`'s "is an elite in play" test is computed on the CLAMPED-to-`maxRank` overflow (`rankBeforeBleed`), not the raw unclamped overflow — this is the one subtlety that makes `FOE_ELITE.maxRank: 0` reproduce today's exact tier-pick behavior (including the bleed lowering the LEVEL, not a phantom rank) at every depth, satisfying the plan's own "empty and identity" truth for `d40` under `maxRank: 0`.
- Test depths for the `startCombat` elite-wiring cases moved from the plan's literal "depth 20, 2 foes" example to "depth 20, 3 foes" once cross-checked against `FOE_COUNT_DEPTH.atLeastThreeFrom: 20` (RULES-16, landed in 75.3-01) — depth 20 always brings at least 3 foes, so the scripted rng sequences needed a third tier-bleed draw. Documented here rather than silently fixed, since it is a genuine cross-plan interaction the PLAN's own illustrative example did not account for.
- Readout protocol (BEFORE/AFTER 200-seed deep-start bot readouts) skipped per this plan's own orchestrator_notes, itself following the user's 2026-09-26 ruling — see Deviations below.
