## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **The targets are ruled** (user, 2026-09-25, recorded in 75.3-CONTEXT "Design rulings after planning"); for floors 13 and deeper they replace Ruling C's tail rows, whose per-floor survival rises with depth. Floors 1-12 stay Phase 54's fitted objective and are not refitted.
- **Why the fresh slice is gated.** The ruled fresh-start targets need 1,000 seeds (30-75 minutes per run on this machine), while the deep-start slices take minutes. Scoring is lexicographic, so running the fresh slice only for candidates that already meet every slice target never changes which candidate wins, and a PASS always has the fresh slice measured. `--fresh=always` exists for the BEFORE and FINAL rows and for diagnosis.
- **Why a fourth search coordinate.** "Depth 30 should basically never happen" asks for real growth from floor 20 to 30, where the elite rank climbs from 2 to 5, so FOE_ELITE.hpPerRank joins the two knee slopes and the resist slope. The user-ruled values (FOE_COUNT_DEPTH, the knee at floor 12, holdRounds 3) are never searched.
- **Rotation containment has no slack.** "Never outlasts" is read as the rotation slice's median and p90 floors gained both no higher than the fair slice's from the same depth. With 40 seeds against 200 this can be noisy; relaxing it would be a search-parameter change recorded as a ruling-F row, or a question for the user.
- **A deep-start hero is a dev start** (level set by the depth threshold, capped at 5, a depth-scaled purse, no found items), so it is weaker than a natural run that reached the same floor. The slices measure the rules, not a typical survivor.
