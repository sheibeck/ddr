<objective>
Re-route the Phase 32 seam from the Round Card to the whole-fight › log (CSCR-04) and render that log in the current panel slot so Plan 03 can drop it straight into the middle band: `dispatchWithToasts` writes `window.__mzFightLog` through the Plan 01 module (uncapped, narrative + dull refusals, per-entry roll detail), the ending action's lines go to `window.__mzFightEnd` (no toast), `renderFightLog(host)` builds the newest-first › list with in-place tap-to-reveal, `#enc-round-live` announces only new entries, the DR18 CSS rule is scoped so dice can show in the log, and `noteCombat` gains the `over` tags Plan 05's endings need. Every Round Card artifact is retired and its tests replaced.

Decision recorded (Claude's discretion, CONTEXT "Cleared at endCombat/flee/death"): the log is cleared exactly when the post-dispatch state has no combat; the ENDING action's own folded lines (the killing blow, the escape roll, the loot drop) are not lost — they are parked on `window.__mzFightEnd.lines`, which Plan 05 prints inside the over-panel line and which is cleared on the panel's dismissal transition and on a fresh fight. Tap-to-reveal toggles the roll line in place (no renderEncounter call) so a log tap never re-arms the decision buttons.

Purpose: the seam is the single most test-pinned region in the shell (shell-round-card, shell-toast-wiring, round-card-worst-case); landing it alone, with its re-pins, keeps every later wave's diff to layout.
Output: `mazeworld.html` (seam, log renderer, announcer, CSS, DR18 scope, noteCombat tags), `test/unit/shell-fight-log.test.js`, re-pinned `shell-toast-wiring.test.js` and `round-card-worst-case.test.js`, deleted `shell-round-card.test.js`.
