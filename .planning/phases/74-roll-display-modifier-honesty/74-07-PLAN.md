<objective>
Give the hero's condition chips their effect from the player's side, with the resulting range, on the chip-tap card.

Purpose: 74-CONTEXT "Condition chips state their effect from your side, with the resulting range where one exists: 'Afraid: −3 to hit (now 19–20)'". The hero chips are painted by mazeworld.html's classic paintConditions from engine/derived.js#conditionsOf. A tap raises CONDITION_EXPLAIN (the rail out of combat, the combat-legal condition card in a fight). The chip label stays short; the effect leads the tap card.

The effect is measured, not restated. For each chip, a shallow what-if copy of the state drops only that condition. The live and what-if states are then read through the engine's own functions (via rollOdds.heroHitOdds and foeToHitVs), and the difference is formatted through rollRange.js. This design is also the hook Phase 77 (CMBUI-13) needs: adding an ability timer to WHAT_IF gives Smoke/Sidestep/Battle Roar indicators their effect text for free. This plan does not add those; that is Phase 77's work.

Output: conditionEffects.js with tests, the chip-tap wiring and bridge, the harness twin, and the re-pinned status-chit test.
</objective>
