<objective>
Extend src/browser/rollRange.js into the ONE formatter every surface uses for winning ranges and signed modifiers, with the sign keyed on who rolled.

Purpose: 74-CONTEXT "Modifier signs everywhere" and "How odds read on screen":
- Every displayed modifier is signed from the player's side.
- The engine's mods are signed for the roller, so one formatter owns the flip.
- Ranges are "lo–hi", naming the die where it isn't obvious.

73-03 created this module for exactly this extension (its header and the ROLL-LEDGER handoff say Phase 74 extends it rather than re-deriving range math anywhere else). This plan builds only the formatter. The surfaces adopt it in 74-03 through 74-07, and 74-08 guards it.

Output: new exports in rollRange.js and their contract tests.
</objective>
