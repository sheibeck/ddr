<objective>
Put the chip flavour on screen: every chip tap card (HUD strip, YOUR LOT, Company panel) leads with the flavour line and keeps the whole exact tap text behind the shared RULES toggle, reusing the rail line's `rules` property from 95-05; and the first-paint UP YOUR SLEEVE card gets the same treatment. No fixture moves.

Implements these CONTEXT decisions (96-CONTEXT.md):
- 'Abilities and chips: Condition chips': a chip tap's card shows a flavour line first; the exact `CONDITION_EXPLAIN` text sits behind RULES, reusing the rail line's `rules` property from 95-05; this applies to every condition chip, harmful and helpful.
- 'Abilities and chips: Ability descriptions': the ability's txt goes behind RULES wherever an ability description is shown; the planner maps the UP YOUR SLEEVE pool-pick card as one such surface, and an ability chip's tap card as another (its lead is the ability's own flavour, its RULES the ability's txt inside the tap text).
- docs/TEXT-LAYERS.md "Reuse in Phase 96": the rail line's `rules` property for the chip tap card; no new setting.

Design (planner): the call sites keep passing the exact old tap text as their second argument and gain one trailing flavour spec `{ line, id, name }`; a pure function in rail.js swaps them into the card (`lines[0].text` is the flavour, `lines[0].rules` the exact text), so every existing test stub that ignores the extra argument keeps seeing the exact text, and the module script and the test mirrors share one implementation.

Flagged (planner): the Oracle log line written when a fresh run's pool ability is announced is built from the ability text; it now logs the flavour line (beyond the "narration is out of scope" boundary, because it is a description, not a narration). If the user prefers the old text in the log, the one-line change is in `surfaceAbilityPool`.

Purpose: ROADMAP criteria 2 and 4 for chips.
Output: the chip cards wired on four paths, one rail function, surface cases, source-pin re-pins; no fixture moves.
</objective>
