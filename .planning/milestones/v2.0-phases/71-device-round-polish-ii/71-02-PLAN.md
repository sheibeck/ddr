What a store row shows today (src/browser/storeScreen.js, ~70-99):
- `sub` is the stock line's own engine sub. For the repair row it is replaced by armorDisplay(c).wornSub plus the mend text.

- `usable = usableBy(effectParams.item, c)`, shown when rs.showUsable is true.
- The row renders `[sub, rs.compareLine, rs.reasonText].join(" · ")` plus the usable suffix inside `<i>`.

The engine subs come from engine/economy.js#openStore (~349-380):
- weaponLine: WEAPONS[w].lab, for example "d10+2";
- armorLine: `AR ${a.ar}, ${a.wp} hp`;
- premiumLine: `${p.txt} · enchanted`;
- potionLine: p.txt;
- lockpicks: "opens boxes on 1–5", while its item.txt is "1–5 on d10 against any lock";
- the food, repair, scroll and rations lines carry no item.
