### D. Band 2 has to fit a 411px Pixel 7: the slot widths
Pixel 7 CSS width: 1080 / 2.625 = 411px. At text size M, `.mw-hud-item` labels are Press Start 2P at 6.5px (1em advance per glyph) and the numbers are Courier Prime Bold at 16px (0.6em per digit, 9.6px). The four labels total 22 glyphs = 143px. Label-to-number gaps are 4 × 2px and item gaps 3 × 8px. Band 2 adds 28px of horizontal padding, a 10px gap before the ☰, and the ☰'s layout width.
- With 57-01's 5ch slot on all four counters, the counters alone need 143 + 192 + 8 + 24 = 367px. Adding 28 + 10 + a 40px ☰ gives **445px, which overflows 411**, and Rations is clipped at the default text size. That would be a regression from HEAD, where band 2 has the full width.
- **Decision (reconciling D-08 with the mock):** Squares keeps exactly `COUNTER_DIGIT_SLOT` (5ch). Squares is the unbounded counter, and it was the one the 2026-09-21 report was about. Depth, Day and Rations get fixed tabular slots sized to their real bounds: Depth 3 (normal runs end around depth 20, and the dev start-at-depth input caps at 999), Day 3, Rations 2 (content/bags.js caps rations at 60 in the largest bag). The total is 143 + 124.8 + 8 + 24 + 28 + 10 + 40 = **377.8px, which fits 411 with about 33px to spare.** The overflow clamp (`COUNTER_OVERFLOW`, 5 digits) is unchanged for all four counters.
- **At L (×1.25)** the counters need about 367px against 333 available, so Rations still clips by about 34px. HEAD already clips by about 68px at L today, because 5ch × 4 at L is wider than the full band. This is recorded as a deferred device check, not solved here. Wrapping band 2 would contradict D-08's "not wrapping", and that call belongs to the user.
- **The ☰'s 48×48 hit box without a taller band.** The button box is 48×48 (the app's own button floor and Android's 48dp target), with −7px block margins and −8px inline-end margin. Its layout footprint is therefore 34px tall and 40px wide, so band 2's height stays inside its own 8px/9px padding (about 51px in total). The visible ☰ face per the mock (gold on #241d12, a 2px #6b5c3c border, a 0 3px 0 #0d0b08 shadow) is a 34px inner span.

### E. How the menu is built (and why)
- **Scrim, not propagation tricks.** While the menu is open, a transparent `position:fixed; inset:0` scrim (z 5) covers the map, the rail, the condition strip, band 1 and the tab bar. The menu wrapper (z 6) sits above it. A tap anywhere but the menu lands on the scrim, and its onclick closes the menu. The map's gesture tracker never sees the pointer, because the scrim is not inside `#mw-maze-viewport`. That makes "consumed" a structural fact, as 57-01's chip move and 57-03's rail handler were. It also means a tab tap while the menu is open only closes the menu, and a second tap switches tab: standard dropdown behaviour, to be confirmed on device. The scrim closes on `click`, and it does not stamp `lastDismissAt`. Nothing under the thumb ever stepped, so there is nothing to settle, and `shell-map-invariants`' pinned count of three settle stamps stays three.
- **Row routing by id.** The rows are `<button type="button" role="menuitem">` elements carrying the four legacy chip ids. The existing lines `…getElementById("mw-chip-marks").addEventListener("click", openMarksLegend)`, `…("mw-chip-centre")…centerMap`, `…("btn-camp").onclick = openCampSheet` and the module script's `…("mw-gear-btn")?.addEventListener("click", openSettingsSheet)` stay byte-identical. The menu closes on select through ONE delegated onclick on the `#mw-hud-menu` container. The row handler runs at the target and the close runs on bubble.
- **State in the DOM only.** `#mw-hud-menu`'s `data-open` ("1"/"0") is the single source. One writer (`setHudMenuOpen`) writes it together with the scrim's `data-open` and the ☰'s `aria-expanded`, from one boolean. That is the 57-02 "one predicate, one place" shape. CSS reads `[data-open="1"]` and `[aria-expanded="true"]`.
- **The policy is pure and bridged.** `hudMenuNext(open, kind, ctx)` in `src/browser/hudMenu.js` decides the next state for the six event kinds. The classic `hudMenuEvent(kind)` reads the live state and the encounter context, asks the bridge, and writes. If the bridge is missing, the menu can only ever close (fail closed), and a missing bridge can never strand it open over the map.
- **Why the dropdown hangs from band 2 without clipping.** `.mw-hud-counters` keeps `overflow:hidden`, so the ☰ wrapper must be its SIBLING inside a new band-2 row and never its child. Nothing between the wrapper and the root may clip. `.mw-hud` itself has no overflow rule. Only band 1 gets `overflow:hidden`, per the mock.
