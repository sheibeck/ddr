This plan was written against 57-05's stated design: the menu's resting state is `visibility:hidden;opacity:0;transform:translateY(-6px);pointer-events:none`, its open state is `visibility:visible;opacity:1;transform:none;pointer-events:auto`, and its wrap is at z 6. If the landed rules differ, keep the same intent (transitions between the landed resting and open states) and record the difference in the SUMMARY.

**Record the base.** BASE_58 is `git log --diff-filter=A --format=%H -- .planning/phases/58-motion-pacing/58-CONTEXT.md | tail -1`.

**The global hidden rule (verified at 6a6c06e).** The first line of the first `<style>` block is `*{box-sizing:border-box}html{color-scheme:dark}body{margin:0}[hidden]{display:none!important}`. An `!important` declaration beats a non-important one regardless of specificity, so 57-02's `.mw-rail[hidden]{display:block;...}` has never applied. A hidden rail is `display:none`, and a transform transition cannot run from or to `display:none`. The fix is `display:block!important` on `.mw-rail[hidden]`: specificity (0,2,0) beats `[hidden]` (0,1,0) once both are important. Surfaces that SHOULD be `display:none` when hidden (sheets, overlay, screens) are unaffected, because their close keeps them un-hidden until the animation has run. Record this finding in the SUMMARY; 57-02's tests could not see it because they assert source text, not computed style.

**Why the rail must not repaint on hide.** `renderRail()` rewrites the icon, title, lines and buttons on every call, and for a cleared card it writes the idle copy ("Depth N" / the idle line) plus the dull tone and `data-idle`. With a real slide-out, that copy would flash on the departing rail for 120 ms. Skipping the repaint when the computed state is hidden keeps the leaving card on screen until it is gone. The next shown card repaints everything as today.

**The tab cross-fade geometry.** `.mw-screens` is the scroll container, a flex child of `#mw-stage` (which is `position:relative;overflow:hidden`). An absolutely-positioned outgoing screen with `inset:0` has `#mw-stage` as its containing block. It is neither scrolled nor clipped by `.mw-screens`, and it is clipped by the stage. Shifting it up by `.mw-screens`' current `scrollTop` with an inline `translateY` makes the fading layer show exactly what the player was looking at. The layer is pointer-inert and carries no z-index, so the rail (z 4) and the menu (z 6) stay above it.

**A tap during a close.** `closeMarksLegend`, `closeCampSheet` and renderEncounter's true-to-false transition already stamp `lastDismissAt`; keep those stamps at CALL time. `inputGuards.js#DISMISS_SETTLE_MS` (250) is longer than `CLOSE_MS + CLOSE_SLACK_MS` (154), which 58-01 pinned, so a tap that falls through an inert closing overlay onto the map is refused by `window.move`'s settle clause.
</discovery>
