**How a round reaches the screen today** (the module script's `engineCombatAction(type, extra)` and `dispatchWithNarration(action)`; find them by name). One tap dispatches one engine action. The engine resolves the whole exchange (your blow, their blows, deaths, level-ups) and returns `{ state, events }`. `dispatchWithNarration` fires `playForDispatch` once, then folds the events through `fightLogLinesFor(action.type, events, ctx)` and appends them as ONE batch to `window.__mzFightLog`. When the dispatch ENDS combat, it parks the line texts on `window.__mzFightEnd` instead, and the over-panel prints them. `engineCombatAction` then sets the state, runs `noteCombat`, fires haptics, writes the Oracle lines, and calls `window.paint(); window.renderEncounter();`. Everything lands in one frame, and so does every sound, which is the complaint.

**Why lines map to events by first index only.** `linesForAction` (`src/browser/narrationLines.js`) folds several events into one line and sorts lines by PRIORITY, then by index, and with `withIdx` it reports only each line's FIRST constituent event. `fightLogLinesFor` is a 1:1 map over that output. The beat therefore knows each line's first event, and the per-line HP frames and the cue ownership both key on it. Do not touch the fold or its ordering; the Oracle event-order todo is backlog 999.5, not this phase.

**Foe names repeat.** A band of goblins shares one name, and `struck`/`allyStruck` carry only `target` (a name) plus `dmg`, with no foe index (engine/combat.js pushes, ~L792, ~L1466). A frame may therefore move a bar only when the name is unique among the foes still standing, and everything else settles on the last line. That is the discretion clause in D-09, applied honestly.

**Cue ownership rule.** A cue belongs to the line whose first-event index is the greatest index less than or equal to the cue's event index. A cue earlier than every line (including the synthesized step clip at -1) belongs to line 0. With lines sorted by priority this keeps a kill clip with the strike line that folded it and a hurt clip with the foe line that caused it, and nothing is dropped. The union of all lines' cues is exactly the dispatch's cue list.
</discovery>
