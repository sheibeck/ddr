# Phase 25.1 Plan 01: Card vs Toast + Oracle Panel Summary

**The dismissible "Move on" card is now reserved for a new floor or a level-up (`CARD_EVENTS`); every other move/camp event — traps, climbs, chests, gold, teleports, camp results — is a narrative toast carrying the Oracle's own sentence with dice stripped, toasts linger 2x longer and can be tapped away, and the Oracle log fills the screen and always opens at the newest line with a "↑ newer" pill.**

## Accomplishments

- `src/browser/toasts.js`: exported `CARD_EVENTS` (`floorChanged`, `leveled`), `NARRATIVE_ACTIONS` (`move`, `camp`, `resolveJoiner`), the four lifetime constants, `toastLifetime(len, visible)`, and `narrativeToastText(html)` — a pure HTML-to-plain-text sentence stripper (roll span removed first, then tags, then entities decoded with `&amp;` last). `toastsForAction`'s step 7 now computes the narrative-vs-table choice in one line; `tableFour`/`tableFourNoop` moved from `ORACLE_ONLY` to real `TOAST_FOR` builders that pass through the engine's own prose `result`.

## Device checklist (non-blocking, for the orchestrator's phone-push offer)

1. Walk onto a trap → a toast with the Oracle's sentence appears (e.g. "Pit trap finds you first."); no "Move on" card.
2. Step on a teleport square → a toast appears, the map recenters; no card.
3. Descend to a new floor → the card appears.
4. Level up → the card appears.
5. Trigger four toast-worthy events in quick succession → four toasts stack and are readable one by one (cap 4, oldest evicted).
6. Tap a toast → it dismisses immediately (fade-out), no error, no lingering ghost toast.
7. Leave a toast alone → it lingers noticeably longer than the old ~1.5-4.2s window (now 3-9s base, +1.2s per toast already stacked).
8. With Android's "Remove animations" accessibility setting on, confirm toasts still appear/disappear with no slide/fade motion (lifetime timing itself is unaffected either way).
9. Open the Oracle tab → the panel fills the screen (no empty space at the bottom) and lands on the newest line at the top.
10. Scroll the Oracle log down into older entries → a "↑ newer" pill appears near the bottom; tap it → returns to the newest line and the pill hides.
11. Make camp and go hungry / get patched / rest → the result arrives as a toast, not a "Camp" card.
