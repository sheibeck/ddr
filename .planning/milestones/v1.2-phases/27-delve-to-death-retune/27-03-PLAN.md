## What to turn if a band measure misses (one move per measure per iteration; one rationale sentence per knob in the log)

| Miss | Turn |
|---|---|
| Natural median < 5 | the NEXT rung of the ladder above (never two rungs at once) |
| Natural median > 6 | back off the last rung one notch (e.g. `FOE_GRACE_AT_2` 0.5 -> 0.6, `HAZARD_SCALE_AT_START` 0.5 -> 0.7, rations 2 -> 1) — never floors <= 2 in the non-combat knobs |
| Forced-20 encounters survived < 3.0, or floors gained p50 0 / mean < 1.0 | (1) `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` up (16 -> 21 makes depth 20 fully canon — identity is the ceiling of what the deep dials can do); (2) `ENCOUNTER_DOT_CAP` down by 2 (re-pin depths 3-5 if they move); (3) `DARK_BLOB_CAP` / `DARK_RADIUS_CAP` one notch down (3/7 -> 2/6) if "starved in the dark" is among the forced-20 top causes; (4) `FOE_POWER_MAX` / `ABILITY_THREAT_MAX` down only once `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` >= 21 |
| Forced-20 encounters > 5.0 or floors gained mean > 2.0 | `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` down (never below 6) or `ENCOUNTER_DOT_CAP` up by 2 |
| Reach >= 20 < 1.0 % | `ENCOUNTER_DOT_CAP` down by 2 and/or darkness caps down (fewer fights per floor at 6-19); `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` up if < 21 |
| Reach >= 20 > 2.0 % (or reach >= 10 far above the advisory 20 %) | steepen the tail: `FOE_POWER_SOFT_K` 35 -> 25 and/or `FOE_CAP_SOFT_K` 20 -> 15, or `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` down (>= 6), or `ENCOUNTER_DOT_CAP` up by 2 — never floors <= 2 |
| Cannot-act > 0 (smoke or AFTER) | HARD STOP — an engine bug, not a tuning miss; record the cell, commit only the evidence JSON, add a blocker, return blocked |
| Forced 35 / 50 readouts | reported only — no move is ever made for these |

Rules: `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH` >= 6 always; every deep MAX >= its BASE; `foePower` >= `FOE_GRACE_AT_2` everywhere and exactly 1 from `FOE_GRACE_CANON_FROM_DEPTH` to `COMBAT_SCALE_FROM_DEPTH - 1`; the curve never softens past 20 (monotone by construction, enforced by the monotone test); `FOE_LVL_BIAS` stays 0; the reach >= 10 corridor may be tightened after iteration 1 (Claude's Discretion — record the reasoning); the bot, seeds, workers, maxActions and exploreBudget never change.
