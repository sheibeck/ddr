<objective>
Extend the parity harness with a generic, machine-checked "action-path" divergence record — the FID-07 instrument Plans 24-03 (Fridgian hide/whiff on combat/lose, seed 14) and 24-04 (Pickpocket markup on the economy fixture, seed 3) will use to declare divergences whose consequence is a changed action path (a flipped outcome, an unaffordable purchase), which Phase 23's field-strip records cannot express. Wire it into every replay site now, as a no-op, so the two declaration plans can run in parallel touching only their own fixture files.

Purpose: keep the Engine Gate honest — "declared, measured, asserted before it is stripped" — for divergences that are behavioural rather than field-shaped, without rewriting any fixture's seed/actions/scenarios (forbidden by action-script.schema.md).
Output: helpers in comparables.js, wiring in three parity test files, schema doc, a unit test over synthetic data.
</objective>
