<objective>
Land IDENT-02 — every freshly rolled Magic User has at least one level-1, day-one-castable ATTACK spell — by extending `rollGrimoire`'s existing first-day top-up with ZERO new rng draws (D: "Mechanism"), and honor FID-06 for the chargen seeds whose grimoire CONTENT this changes: measure exactly which of the 20 parity-fixture seeds differ (planner's simulation: seeds 15 and 24 only, both chargen-only seeds), declare each in the chargen fixture with a before/after grimoire and rationale, and make both chargen parity replay sites assert those before/after books and strip only that field for only those seeds. Everything else stays byte-identical against the frozen prototype with no carve-out.

Two mechanics feed the content change and both are locked decisions: (a) the attack top-up appends the first attack-kind spell of the ALREADY-shuffled `spare` list when the book has no usable-now attack (seed 24, Apprentice, gains Freeze); (b) `rollGrimoire`'s `usableNow` reads the effective level via `spellLevelFor`, so a Summoner's Summon (and an Illusionist's Phantom Host) now counts toward the "two usable-now spells" requirement — for seed 15 (Summoner) the existing loop therefore stops one spell earlier and the book drops Heal. (b) is the direct consequence of D-"rollGrimoire's usableNow uses spellLevelFor"; CONTEXT anticipated "seed 15 only if its book changes" — it does, and it is declared, not hidden. The `spare` pool predicate itself must NOT use spellLevelFor: the planner's simulation showed that adding Summon/Phantom Host to `spare` lengthens the Fisher-Yates shuffle by one draw and shifts the rng cursor for seeds 8 and 15 (breaking the magic fixture and the maze) — the pool predicate stays exactly `sp.lvl === 1 && schoolGate(sub, sp.s) <= 1`.

Purpose: no Magic User is ever dealt a grimoire that cannot hurt anything (ROADMAP success criterion 2), with the rng order provably untouched (FID-06).
Output: `engine/character.js` edit, `test/unit/guaranteed-attack-spell.test.js`, chargen fixture `divergences` block, `comparables.js` helpers, both chargen parity sites updated, schema doc; `npm test` fully green at the end of this plan (no red-between-waves).
