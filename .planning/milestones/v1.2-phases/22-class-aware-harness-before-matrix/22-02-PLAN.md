Planning-time stuck investigation (already done — do not redo the scan): the 19/400 baseline stuck runs at cap 2,500 are exactly 12 Samurai + 7 Wizard runs. Samurai: below the flee threshold the bot picks `flee`, `engine/combat.js#flee` emits `fleeRefused` (reason samurai) WITHOUT a foe turn, the bot re-picks flee forever — a bot-policy bug (Rule 1). Wizard: with charges left and no castable attack spell the bot picks `attack`, `playerStrike` emits `strikeRefused` (reason wizard) without a foe turn, same loop — the loop is a bot-policy bug (fixed here by reacting to the refusal like a human would: cast something else or flee); the underlying "Wizard cannot act" state is an ENGINE finding for Phase 23 (IDENT-01) and is documented in the ledger by Plan 22-04, NOT changed here. No unreachable-exit or other engine dead-end appeared in the 400-seed scan.
