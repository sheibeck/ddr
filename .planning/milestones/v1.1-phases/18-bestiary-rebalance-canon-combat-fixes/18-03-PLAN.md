## Assumptions locked by this plan

- `source.crit` for the hero's strike is the composite `crit` flag `playerStrike` already computes (natural 1 unless noCrit, plus Silence/Stealth/backstab/Ninja/Cutthroat criticals) — the engine's single "critical hit" concept; D-07's parenthetical names the natural 1 as the canonical way a critical happens, not a second definition.
- Ally and member strikes pass `crit: false` — the engine has no critical concept for allies (no doubling exists on those paths); inventing one would be a rules change outside this phase.
- The `struck` event's `dmg` becomes the applied value (post-soak/halving/multiplier). Every existing `struck.dmg` assertion (combat.test.js lines 318, 335, 409; item-wiring.test.js 121-127) targets foes without ar/halfDmg/multiplier rows, so applied === raw there and they pass unchanged.
- On a soak the caller skips its own damage event (no "You hit X for 0 hp" line); the seam's `foeArmorSoaked` carries the story. `strikeMissed` is NOT emitted for a soaked hit (it was a hit).
- `earthquake`-style aggregate events are 18-04's concern; in this plan `acidTick.dmg`, `allyStruck.dmg`, `wardReflected.amount` and `struck.dmg` all report `applied`.
