transition: singular -> plural. Before this plan the only caster is the player (`castSpell`, player-shaped: grimoire, charges, school gates, backfire) and resistance runs one way (foe resists player). After it, foes cast too and the hero resists.
decision: add-alongside
rationale: `engine/foeAbilities.js` is a separate, foe-shaped resolver (kit order, cooldowns, per-encounter uses, no charges/grimoire) placed NEXT to `castSpell`, sharing exactly one primitive — `resistRoll` in `derived.js` — and the existing damage/targeting seams; `castSpell` is not generalized (FOE-01's explicit boundary, milestone Anti-Pattern 1). What would force a later promote: a THIRD caster kind (party members casting spells, or foe-vs-foe casting) — at that point the shared surface (kit readiness, telegraph, resist, effect dispatch) would be extracted into one caster interface consumed by all three.
</assumption_delta_decision>

## Assumptions locked by this plan (planner discretion per CONTEXT)

- **A1 (D-18 confirmed):** a foe bolt has no to-hit roll; INT resistance + the hero's ward/armor/Hardiness/halfNext are the counterplay.
- **A2:** Hardiness and `c.halfNext` apply to drains (generic landed-blow mitigations); only the armor-soak d20 is skipped.
- **A3 (D-20 realised):** tick-then-check with lazy init to `every`: visit 1 initialises to N and decrements to N-1; the ability is ready when cd reaches 0 (visit N); cast resets to N. `every: N` therefore means "at most once every N of this foe's foeTurn visits", counted per visit (a foe visited twice in one round by the initiative reroll ticks twice — same cadence as ward/mirror).
- **A4 (D-19):** pursuit rolls a normal to-hit + damage (no auto-hit), targets the fleeing HERO only (no `pickFoeTarget`), and fires on all three success exits.
- **A5 (Djinni flee timing):** checked at the start of the Djinni's own visit (after the asleep check, before the ability gate) — it never acts again once below the threshold.
- **A6:** `C.pendingFoes` entries are `{ by, foe }` wrappers so no extra per-foe field is needed for the join narration; after the join `C.pendingFoes = null`.
- **A7:** a summon skipped because of a cap keeps its cd at 0 and fires as soon as the cap clears; a ready summon/heal/every ability that is not first in kit order simply waits.
- **A8:** `c.foeEffect.rounds` counts full foeTurns AFTER the applying one (identity guard), so `rounds: 1` is never a no-op.
- **A9:** the flee-failure cleared-check (`encounterCleared` + `endCombat` when a foeTurn inside `flee()` leaves no live foe) is a gated, deliberate fix required by the Djinni flee; it cannot fire on any fixture (no fixture foe leaves mid-foeTurn).
