## Phase Goal

**As a** Mazeworld player, **I want to** the game to open with its own splash, stay in portrait, and look right with zero network, **so that** it feels like a real native app I can play fully offline in airplane mode.

*(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 `**Goal:**` line under MVP_MODE. This final slice adds native chrome + true offline correctness and produces the phase's green build gate — the installable APK and the AAB PLT-01 names.)*

<objective>
Dress the wrap in native chrome, make the app genuinely offline, and produce the phase's final green build. Copy the provided splash density buckets into the Android project, configure the splash/status-bar/portrait lock (manifest + runtime), generate the launcher icon from the provided 512x512 source, self-host the three Google Fonts families the game currently loads from a CDN, and prove the whole thing compiles to both an APK (`assembleDebug`) and an AAB (`bundleDebug`).
