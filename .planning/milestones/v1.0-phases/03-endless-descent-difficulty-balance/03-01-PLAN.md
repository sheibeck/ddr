<objective>
Create the phase's single source of difficulty truth: a pure `engine/difficulty.js`
exporting `difficultyCurve(depth)` plus named, documented tuning constants, backed by
property tests, and a dev-only headless tuning harness. No consumer wiring yet — this
plan lands the module and its guards so Plan 02 can rewire `genFloor` against a proven,
bounded curve. (RUN-03; honors CONTEXT locked decisions: asymptotic soft-cap over
linear, independent caps on dot-count / dark-blob-count / dark-blob-radius, BREATHER_EVERY
cadence, named tunable knobs.)

Purpose: bound the two floor-generation knobs (`nDots`, darkness) that today grow
linearly and unboundedly with depth, without changing the already-tuned floors 1-5.
Output: engine/difficulty.js, test/difficulty/difficulty.test.js, tools/tune-difficulty.mjs.

## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** roguelike player, **I want to** descend an endless maze whose difficulty scales
fairly with depth and instantly reroll a fresh dice-rolled delver when I die, **so that**
every session is a self-contained 5-10 minute depth-chase with no fixed ending.

(Derived from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal + RUN-01..05; the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line is
outcome-shaped rather than strict user-story form — surfaced here for traceability, not
invented. This plan delivers the difficulty-curve foundation of that story.)
