## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Where the targets come from.** Floor L's target is USER RULING C's target at floor 2L. It is Ruling C at twice the pace, so every S_L is one of the user's own ruled digits and nothing is invented.
  - The curve's 50/50 point moves from Ruling C's floor 6-7 to floor 3-4, and its median death floor is 4.
  - The per-floor death rate climbs through the Filter: 4.9, 16.2, 26.7 and 33.7% on floors 1-4. It peaks in the Wall (37.2, 37.9, 37.1, 34.7%) and eases after that (32.3 down to 22.7%), which is Ruling C's own narrative.
  - Because the tolerances are unchanged, a per-floor PASS on floors 2 and 4 alone (S_2 >= 71.7 and S_4 <= 46.7) already brackets the median to 3-4. Stuck runs can still blur that, so the p50 gate is explicit.
- **Why a shape gate, and why these thresholds.** "Variance-driven, not a flat wall" needs a definition a test can check.
  - A wall means a single-floor cliff in per-floor survival: more than a 20-point drop from one floor to the next across floors 1-5. The user's Phase 54 "that's a wall" was a 26-point drop.
  - A flat Filter means p_1 - p_4 under 10 points.
  - The target itself passes with room to spare: its largest drop is 11.3 points, on floor 2, and p_1 - p_4 is 28.8.
  - These thresholds are the planner's proposal under the p50 ruling. The orchestrator can relay a change to the user.
- **Floors 13+ carry no survival target any more.** Ruling C at twice the pace would need Ruling C floors 26-40, which don't exist.
  - The tail has been judged by TAIL_TARGETS on its own slices since Phase 75.3, so tail rows print `target S_L=n/a` and `dS=n/a`.
  - The readout's `reach-20:` line still carries Ruling C's "band 3.0-5.0%" wording. It is reported only and never judged, and it is left as is. TAIL_TARGETS' ruled 1.0% is the number that counts, and the doc record says so.
- **The tail guard.** TAIL_TARGETS.guard.freshP50Death was the fair-bot 5-7 band from USER RULING D. The 2026-09-27 ruling supersedes it for the fair bot, so it becomes [3, 4]. It is a report-only guard; it never scores or verdicts. The ruled tail targets themselves are untouched.
- **The class constraint** is fit-score.mjs#classConstraints on the fair bot's own class pools: |class p50 - pooled| <= 2 and reach5 within 20. It is the existing constraint ROADMAP criterion 3 names, and it runs inside every evaluation. The tune-classes matrix is 79.1-04's FINAL readout and is not re-run here.
- **The coordinate order** (the planner's call under CONTEXT). The foe-side base levers come first, because they act from floor 1 and weigh relatively most on the early floors:
  1. FOE_HIT_SCALE.base
  2. FOE_LEVEL.base (floor 2 foes reach level 2 at 1.0; floor 1 stays level 1 at every in-bounds value)
  3. HAZARD_SCALE.base (traps and falls: the Filter's bad-drop variance)
  4. FOE_HP_SCALE.base
  5. ENCOUNTER_DOTS.base
  6. HERO_HP_SCALE
  7. HERO_SP_SCALE (the hero-side dials carry the largest parity re-declaration)
  8. FOE_HIT_SCALE.perDepth
  9. FOE_LEVEL.perDepth

  The two depth slopes go last because they steepen the tail most, and the tail already sits at its ruled limits. Steps and bounds are unchanged.
- **Run time (estimate).** A 1,000-seed band evaluation takes about 15-25 min with 4 workers. The tail row with the fresh slice always run takes about 20 min. Row #1 at 200 seeds takes about 3-6 min.
