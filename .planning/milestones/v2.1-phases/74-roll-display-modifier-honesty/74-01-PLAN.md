<objective>
Lift the two to-hit chains the display needs out of engine/combat.js into pure, exported helpers in engine/derived.js, and have the engine itself call them. This is a byte-identical extraction, not a rule change.

Purpose: 74-CONTEXT says the foe-details "right now" ranges must reuse the engine's own derived functions and never re-derive a formula. Two chains live only inline in engine/combat.js today:
- the hero's per-target terms (dozing/stupid floor, the sp.toHit cap, the sp.fast step, magicOnly and daggerOnly);
- the foe's per-swing terms against the hero (blind, the Weaken cap, the insult applied last).

This follows the project's own precedent for display-driven extractions: classNeed (Phase 39) and noCritFor (Phase 61) were both moved into derived.js verbatim so a display could read the same rule the engine uses. Engine behaviour stays untouched: the parity suite, the Phase 73 pins and the Phase 72 direction tests are the proof.

Output: three helpers in engine/derived.js, three call sites in engine/combat.js, and an equivalence test proving the helpers match the engine's real events.
</objective>
