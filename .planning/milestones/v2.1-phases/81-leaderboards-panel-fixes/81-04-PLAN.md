Restructure the Leaderboards panel per the user rulings of 2026-09-24/25:

- **BOARD-12:** three scope chips, ME | ALL | FRIENDS, replace the hidden local scope.
- **BOARD-11:** signed in with Compete ON, the panel opens on ALL.

Purpose: the player always knows whose runs they are looking at. That is a direct cause of the device report "my own run didn't show up on my own boards": the local scope could only be reached by re-tapping the active chip. The local-only boards (LINEAGE, GRAVEYARD) are honest about being local.

Output: the edits in files_modified and their tests, plus the doc corrections for the text 81-03 wrote before the reversal.
