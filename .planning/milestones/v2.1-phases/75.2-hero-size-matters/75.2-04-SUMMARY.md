# Phase 75.2 Plan 04: Stepped-size chips, honest size narration, and the race-note/sign-guard close Summary

**Every surface now says what size does (RULES-11): a live Gauntlet of the Giant or Enlarge chip is labelled with the hero's resulting size ("Large"/"Huge") and its tap card leads with the measured "−N vs their swings" effect before stating exactly what a step does; the Oracle and rail narrate a size item's start from the event's own size/damage fields with the old corridor promise gone; and the Elven/Dwarven/Troll race notes plus a five-test cross-surface sign guard prove the size modifier reads the same everywhere.**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 — the stepped-size chips and the size lines in the Oracle and the rail:** `mazeworld.html`'s `paintConditions` label chain now labels a chip carrying a non-empty `cn.size` with that resulting size name (before the generic `CONDITION_COPY[cn.key]?.label || cn.key` fallback) — a Human's live Gauntlet chip reads "Large", both the Gauntlet and Enlarge live together read two "Huge" chips (each counting its own item down, in `c.timers` insertion order), and a chip with no `size` field (a Ring of Power's `power` chip, a Strength `might` chip) is completely unaffected. `CONDITION_COPY.giant`'s fallback label became "Enlarged" (matching `enlarge`'s own row); `CONDITION_EXPLAIN.giant`/`enlarge` both now read "One size larger while it lasts: +2 damage, and one face easier for foes to hit." — the old "Mind the ceilings"/overhead-clearance sentence and the placeholder "for a while" wording are both gone. `src/browser/eventNarration.js` and `src/browser/narrationLines.js`'s `itemEffectStarted` maps replace the old `giant` "too large for the corridor" line and add a matching `enlarge` entry: when the event carries `size`, both read "`<left>` squares one size larger: you are `<size>`. `<signedText(sizeDmg)>` damage, and one face easier for foes to hit. You are, on reflection, a bigger target." (the Oracle wrapped in the existing `hit` span; the rail keeping its `magic` tone); without `size`, both fall back to "One size larger for `<left>` squares." `test/unit/size-voice.test.js` (new, 13 tests at this point) proves the chip half end to end through the real shell sandbox (a state carrying genuine `startEffect` records) and the narration half by calling `EVENT_NARRATION`/`LINE_FOR` directly with events shaped like 75.2-02's own `itemEffectStarted`. `test/unit/status-chit-combat.test.js` and `test/unit/narrationLinesTable.test.js` needed no edits (confirmed by grep before touching anything — neither pins a `giant`/`enlarge` string literal), and the R-32 vocabulary scan (every `conditionsOf` key resolves to its own non-default `CONDITION_EXPLAIN` sentence) passes with the real sentence replacing the placeholder the orchestrator had added to keep master green after 75.2-02.
- **Task 2 — the race notes and the size scenario in the cross-surface sign guard:** `content/races.js`'s `note` and `content/flavor.js`'s `RACE_NOTE` for Elven, Dwarven and Troll are extended to the net truth 75.2-01's identity contract already proved: the Elven note keeps "thin-boned and easy to hit" (the masked face axis) and adds "being small costs it 2 damage" (the unmasked damage axis); the Dwarven note keeps "+2 damage" and "armor wears at half the rate" (the masked damage axis) and adds "being small makes it one face harder to hit" (the unmasked face axis); the Troll note becomes "+11 damage (being large adds 2)" and "one face easier to hit" (both axes unmasked, stacking with the Troll's own +9/harder-to-target-nothing trait). Human/Wilmsry/Fridgian are untouched. `test/unit/roll-sign-consistency.test.js` gains four new scenarios (5 tests) driven through the real engine like its neighbours: a Troll's `foeTurn` event shows "size −1" on the Oracle line, `oracleDetailText`'s fight-log reveal, and `foeDetailsCard`'s "it hits you on" line, AND `characterSheetViewModel`'s SIZE row detail reads "−1 vs their swings" — five agreeing surfaces, one more than the plan's own four-surface minimum; a Dwarven hero's shows "size +1" on the Oracle line and foe details; an Elven hero's own "Elven −1" carries no size token at all (`signedTokens(text, ["size"])` is empty on both the Oracle line and the foe details line — the signature mask keeps size off the Elf's foe roll); a Human with a live Gauntlet of the Giant's `conditionsOf` chip measures `conditionEffectText` to exactly "−1 vs their swings". **`npm test`: 6490/6490, 0 failures. `npm run boot:check`: 4/4 PASS.**
