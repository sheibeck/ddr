<objective>
Show the hero's size on the hero sheet and make the sheet's damage honest about it (RULES-11): a SIZE row with the size name and, when it matters, what it does to damage and to the foe's odds from the player's side; the DAMAGE range and the `#s-dmg` line include the size term.

Purpose: 75.2-CONTEXT's display rule ("the hero sheet shows the size (e.g. 'SIZE Small')") and the standing rule that every to-hit or damage number a surface shows comes from the engine's own derived functions. 75.2-01 changed damage for every Elven, Dwarven and Troll hero; until this plan the sheet still shows the old range.

Output: the view-model row and copy, the damage bracket and `#s-dmg` term, the markup line, tests, and two declared snapshot regenerations.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **The SIZE row's wording** ("Large · +2 damage, −1 vs their swings · Human by birth") is Claude's discretion; it reuses Phase 74's "vs their swings" phrase from conditionEffects.js so the sheet and the chips read alike. Per the user's signature ruling it shows only the axes that actually apply, so a Dwarf's row never claims a damage loss and an Elf's never claims to be harder to hit.
- **Found, not fixed: the sheet's DAMAGE range omits Master of Arms' +2** (weaponDamage adds it; damageBracket never did), and `#s-dmg`'s bonus omits Heft, Master of Arms and might. That drift predates this phase; this plan adds only the size term. Reported for a quick task or Phase 79.
- **Edge probe (RULES-11):** encoding (U+2212, never an ASCII hyphen-minus) is covered by the truths above.
