<objective>
Make size a real stat in the engine (RULES-11): race sets a base step, items add to it, and every step changes weapon damage by 2 and the foe's winning faces by 1, except that a race's base step never cancels one of that race's defining traits (the signature rule). The size modifier travels in the foe's `mods`, signed for the roller, so every surface Phase 74 built shows it. Joiners get the same rule from their own race.

Purpose: the user's rulings of 2026-09-25 (75.2-CONTEXT, including "Race signatures and Joiners"). Today the hero's size is read in one place, a Gauntlet-only damage bump, and `content/races.js`'s `size` field is never read. This plan builds the one size read with its per-axis signature mask, wires it into damage and the foe's odds, records the audit of every size-flavoured rule, and measures the balance shift before and after.

Output: the size helpers and masks, their engine reads, the member-branch term, the direction rows and ledger rows, the re-pinned identity contract and CLASS-PASS rows, the fixture measurement, and four readouts.
</objective>

- **Size names above Large** (Huge +2, Giant +3, reachable only by a Troll using the Gauntlet and Enlarge) and Tiny −2 (unreachable today) are Claude's discretion. The name reads the total step, so a Dwarf is "Small" even though its damage axis is dropped.
