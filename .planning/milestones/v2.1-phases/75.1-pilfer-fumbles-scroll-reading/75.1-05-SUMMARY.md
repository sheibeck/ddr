# Phase 75.1 Plan 05: The scroll-fumble resolver (RULES-10) Summary

**One resolver, engine/scrollFumble.js#resolveScrollFumble, turns a fumbled scroll against its reader (harmful), the reader's whole side (area — Earthquake/Lightning/Fireballs), or the combat's targeted foe (helpful) — reading 75.1-02's SCROLL_FUMBLE table and wiring straight into 75.1-03's foe-side and 75.1-04's reader-side mechanics, narrated on both the Oracle and the rail with zero fixtures moved**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 — the shared reinforcement record and the harmful branch:** `engine/foeAbilities.js` exports `SUMMON_MAX_LIVE` and a new `buildReinforcement(type, tier, rng)` helper, extracted byte-identically from `resolveFoeAbility`'s own summon branch (same fields, same values, same one `rng.pick` draw) — its existing summon-ability tests pass unchanged. `engine/scrollFumble.js` is created with `resolveScrollFumble(state, sp, srng, rng, events, now)`, dispatching on `SCROLL_FUMBLE[sp.n]`'s side and implementing every harmful effect: plain damage (the spell's dice × the level multiplier, unsoaked, direct hp loss — can kill through the ordinary hp-reached-0 path, never an instant kill); `fumbleHeavyBlow` for every heavy row (Freeze/Petrify/Death) and Noxious Vapor's killing face; `C.selfDot` for the two dot rows (Acid, Ice — `by` the spell's own name lowercased, Ice carrying `then: "heavy"`); `C.heroOut` for the four out rows (Doze/Stun/Stupidity/Insane), clamped to `HERO_OUT_MAX`; `C.heroBlind`/`C.heroShrunk` for Blind/Shrink (no turn loss); the same `spell:weaken` timer machinery for Weaken; and Noxious Vapor's own three-branch table (forced-4 heavy blow, drawn-4-then-1 sleep, or a non-4 d6 skipping the d10 draw entirely). `test/unit/roll-high-guard.test.js` adds `engine/scrollFumble.js` to `ENFORCED` with its live draw-tag counts.
- **Task 2 — the area and helpful branches:** `resolveArea` builds the victim order (live `C.allies` members, then `C.ally`, then the reader) and applies Earthquake's single roll to everyone, Lightning's per-victim rolls (the ally drawing no magnitude of its own — unmade by any hit), or Fireballs' round-robin `d8` bolts (a downed member's or unmade ally's later bolt spends the round-robin slot but draws nothing, mirroring `castSpell`'s own volley precedent; a lethal hit on the reader ends the resolution at once). With no party and no summoned ally, every bolt/roll lands on the reader alone. `resolveHelpful` calls `normalizeTarget` and applies heal (capped at `maxWP`), Shield's plain ward or Bubble's armed mirror, Strength's one-time double, Mirror Self's own `d6`, Regeneration, Sense Presence, the fumbled-Summon reinforcement (via `buildReinforcement`, capped at `SUMMON_MAX_LIVE` or an already-pending summon), and Map the Floor/Sense Danger's wasted no-op.
- **Task 3 — lines, a voice test and the full suite:** `src/browser/eventNarration.js` and `src/browser/narrationLines.js` both gain builders for `fumbleOnReader` (switching on effect), `fumbleOnSide` (naming you, the party member, or the unmade summon) and `fumbleOnFoe` (naming the foe and what it gained, or that the spell was wasted/wandered off) — tone "hurt" for the reader's side, "miss" for the foe's gain, deadpan and family-friendly. `test/unit/scroll-fumble-resolve.test.js` gains a dedicated voice test asserting both the Oracle and rail lines name the victim (or "you") and the amount, across a sample of every effect and every `who`. Measured: `test/parity/**` 56/56 (zero fixtures moved — the resolver is not yet called from `readScroll`). **`npm test`: 6348/6348, 0 failures.**
