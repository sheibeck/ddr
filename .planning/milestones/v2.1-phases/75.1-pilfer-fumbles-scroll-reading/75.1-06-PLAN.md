## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **A failed read spends the turn in combat,** like any read: the scroll was read and crumbled, so the foes act.
- **The failed read keeps the spell secret.** A plain failure's line never names the spell (the reader couldn't make it out); the event payload still carries it for tests and tools.
- **Reading reveals the spell before the roll only for automatic readers.** Magic Users and Runes/Signs holders keep today's scrollRead line naming the spell; an intel reader's success names it through scrollDeciphered and scrollCast, and a fumble names it through scrollFumbled.
- **The bot reads out of combat, never in combat,** for every class (unchanged policy, now open to all).
- **The Phase 77 todo for scrollTooAdvanced's refusal-sounding line stays Phase 77's** (CMBUI criterion 3); only Magic Users reach that event.
- **Edge probe (RULES-10):** empty (no scrolls, a missing intel, a single scroll) and encoding (the one formatter, U+2013) are covered by the truths above.
