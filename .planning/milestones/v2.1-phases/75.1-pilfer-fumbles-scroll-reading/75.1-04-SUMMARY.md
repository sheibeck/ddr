# Phase 75.1 Plan 04: The reader's burn, the heavy blow, turn loss and hero Blind/Shrink (RULES-10) Summary

**Every harmful scroll-fumble effect now has its reader-side machinery under the user's severity rulings: a real burn, a heavy blow that replaces every instant kill, at-most-d4 turn loss with a soft-lock-proof `loseTurn` action, and hero Blind/Shrink working exactly like their foe-side twins — narrated, carved out of parity, with zero fixtures moved**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 — the reader's burn, the heavy blow and the scrollFumble cause:** `engine/combat.js` exports `fumbleHeavyBlow(state, spell, how, rng, events, now)` — the ONE replacement for every instant-kill scroll fumble (user ruling 2026-09-25: "no scroll fumble kills outright"): a d10 from its own derived stream plus the floor's depth, subtracted directly from `c.wp` with no armor/ward/Hardiness soak, plus Afraid raised to at least `AFRAID_ROUNDS` (never lowered); it kills only through the normal death path (`die(state, "scrollFumble", ...)`), so a hero with more than `10 + depth` hp always survives it. `foeTurn`'s head gains the reader's own burn (`C.selfDot`), ticking right after the hero's own regeneration: a bonus-or-dice amount subtracted directly from `c.wp`, narrated `selfDotTick`, removing itself at 0 `left` and — when it carried `then: "heavy"` (Ice's promised freeze) — handing off to `fumbleHeavyBlow`. `content/epitaphs.js` gains `CAUSE_TEXT.scrollFumble`, `CAUSE_TEXT_TOKENS.scrollFumble` and a four-line `EPITAPHS.scrollFumble` bank; `src/browser/scoreTag.js`'s `TAG_CAUSES` appends `scrollFumble` (never inserted, per its append-only contract).
- **Task 2 — turn loss (at most d4), loseTurn, hero Blind and hero Shrink:** `engine/combat.js` exports `HERO_OUT_MAX` (4) and `loseTurn(state, rng, events)` — the ONE action a hero who cannot act (`C.heroOut`) may take: clamps `left` into `[1, HERO_OUT_MAX]`, narrates `heroLostTurn` then `heroCameTo` at 0, and always runs `afterPlayerAction` so the summon, the party and the foes keep the fight moving — proven never to soft-lock by three drivers (a lone hero against stupefied foes, a low-hp hero against a foe that hits, a hero with a party). `engine/derived.js#toHit` gains hero Blind as its LAST term (`C.heroBlind` caps the hero's own weapon to-hit at exactly 1 winning face, read identically by `playerStrike`, `heroStrikeFacesVs` and `rollOdds.heroHitOdds`); `playerStrike`'s damage line gains hero Shrink (`C.heroShrunk` halves landed weapon damage, `Math.ceil`, mirroring a shrunk foe's own halving — current hp only, max hp untouched). `conditionsOf` gains `heroOut`/`heroBlind`/`heroShrunk` chip descriptors. `engine/engine.js#applyAction` converts every hero combat action (attack/castSpell/drinkPotion/readScroll/useItem/useAbility/flee/parley/sing) to `loseTurn` while `C.heroOut` is set, ahead of the dispatch switch, which itself gains one new `loseTurn` case. `engine/actions.js#ACTION_TYPES` gains `loseTurn` (no payload, like `fight`). `tools/lib/tuning-bot.mjs#decideAction` returns `loseTurn` whenever `state.combat.heroOut` is set, ahead of every other combat decision.
- **Task 3 — lines, carve-outs and the full suite:** `src/browser/eventNarration.js` and `src/browser/narrationLines.js` both gain builders for `selfDotTick`, `fumbleHeavyBlow`, `heroLostTurn` and `heroCameTo`, tone-matched (hurt/hurt/hurt/hit) to the plan's own voice direction; `actionRefused`'s reason map in both files gains `notOut` ("there is no turn to lose"). `test/parity/harness/comparables.js#stripFoeAbilityState` now also drops the combat-level `selfDot`, `heroOut`, `heroBlind` and `heroShrunk` fields beside `pendingFoes` — a structural tripwire, since nothing sets any of them until 75.1-05's resolver. ## Decisions Made

- **`fumbleHeavyBlow`'s and the burn's `c.wp -=` lines are named `amount`/`burn`, never `dmg`** — preserves `test/unit/foe-damage.test.js`'s standing pin that `engine/combat.js` contains exactly one literal `c.wp -= dmg;` (the ordinary `struckByFoe` decrement).
- **`loseTurn` draws zero rng in every branch** — the countdown is pure arithmetic; any draws in a `loseTurn` dispatch come from `afterPlayerAction`'s own summon/party/foe turns, identically to any other player action reaching that function.
- **`applyAction`'s hero-combat-action conversion is an if/else placed immediately before the dispatch `switch`**, rather than rewritten into the switch's own cases — every existing case stays untouched; the switch itself gains only the one new `loseTurn` case.
- **Hero Blind is `toHit`'s LAST term** so it wins over every other modifier and is read once by `playerStrike`, `heroStrikeFacesVs` and `rollOdds.heroHitOdds` alike, keeping the hero sheet, the combat menu and the actual roll in permanent agreement. A named `"blind"` mod entry (mirroring Mirror Self's own pattern) was deliberately **not** added to `playerStrike`'s `mods` array — the plan's own action step frames this as conditional ("if playerStrike records named to-hit mods"), and no pinned behavior requires it.
- **Hero Shrink halves current-hp landed weapon damage only** (`Math.ceil`), at the exact point a shrunk foe's own blow is halved — max hp is never touched, per 75.1-CONTEXT's own flagged assumption.
