<objective>
Replace the Pilfer's heal-only lockout with a fumble risk (RULES-09): a Pilfer uses jewelry, cloaks and staves like anyone else, but each use rolls a d20, and on a 1 the use fails, the item explodes for d10 to the Pilfer only (no armor soak) and turns to dust.

Purpose: the user's ruling of 2026-09-24/25 ("it no longer works and it turns to dust", "d10 damage on magic item explode", the blast can kill). The Pilfer keeps its good (traps and locks). This plan also moves the bot onto the new rule and measures it.

Output: the engine rule, its death cause and epitaphs, the Oracle and rail lines, the rewritten Pilfer blurb and identity rows, the bot change, tests, and the before/after readouts.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Wards don't soak the blast either.** The ruling says armor does not soak it; this plan treats the blast as direct hp loss from the Pilfer's own hands, so a Shield ward and Hardiness don't soak it either (the Apprentice backfire precedent).
- **Staves are covered but unreachable.** `PILFER_FUMBLE_KINDS` includes "staff" as ruled, but after Phase 75 (75-07/75-09) only a Magic User can wield a staff and a Pilfer is a Thief, so the wrongClass refusal always fires first. A test pins both facts.
- **A fumble is a free action like any item use.** A non-lethal fumble in combat gives the foes no extra turn, matching every other item use.
- **Edge probe (RULES-09):** adjacency, empty and ordering edges are covered by the truths above (plain criteria, each pinned by a test).
