## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Committed base snapshot.** The "before" of the review page is a committed JSON snapshot (expected a few hundred KB) rather than a re-render of an old checkout at close. It is the simplest provable before; its size is recorded in the SUMMARY.
- **The raw sweep is heuristic.** String literals outside registered banks are kept when they look like copy (letters and at least one space, or sentence punctuation) and dropped when they look like code (ids, CSS, paths, regex sources, single kebab or camel tokens). It is a net for copy that lives inline; the registered banks and builders stay the authoritative corpus.
- **Pattern calibration.** The ROLL-04 patterns are calibrated on the base corpus in this plan: a base hit that is not a roll phrase (for example a ration count after "need") either tightens its pattern or becomes an exception with a reason. The exception list is pruned for rot at close (79-12), not here, so later waves never share an edit to it.
