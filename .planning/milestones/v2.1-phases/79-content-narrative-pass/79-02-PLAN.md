<objective>
Make every number a gain line or a Table 4 line prints the number that actually happened (the two routed number-honesty todos, under VOX-05 rubric point 4: "accurate to what the engine actually did").

Purpose: a hero three HP below full who drinks a potion rolling 8 reads "+8" and gains 3; and the user's device death log read "Table 4, roll 7: The dice decide — -15 HP." then "The maze extracts a toll you did not agree to. 19 hp." — one event, two numbers, no minus sign ("I'm not sure where the 19 damage came from"). The engine already knows both true numbers; it just does not say them.

Output: additive event fields and number-free Table 4 prose in the engine, the honest lines on both narration surfaces, a new test file, the extended sweep, and the ledger.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Which Table 4 fix.** The todo offers two: name the row by its effect on the roll line, or have the engine emit the scaled amount on `encounterRolled`. This plan does the first (presentation) plus additive fields on `tableFour` (the event that already knows the amount), because `encounterRolled` is pushed before the row resolves and the roll line has no need for a number once it names the effect.
- **Unit spelling.** The todo writes "−19 HP"; the house line shape in docs/CLARITY.md and every existing Oracle line write lower-case "hp" ("−4 hp"). This plan keeps "hp" (the HP-not-WP ruling is about the unit's name, not its case). One word for the user's review page.
- **`gained` everywhere, even where equal.** `rested`, `floorRegen` and `cloakRegenerated` already report the clamped amount. They get `gained` too, so one field carries the truth for every gain and one guard can sum it.
- **Table 4 prose moves its number out.** `tableFour.result` stays engine-authored voice (Phase 04.2's choice) but no longer carries the number; the builder appends the signed amount. Greenfield: no dual path, no fallback to the old prose.
