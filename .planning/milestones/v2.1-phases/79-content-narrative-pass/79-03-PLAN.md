<objective>
Make every sub-class and race description name both what it is good at and what it pays for (VOX-04), with a compact footer generated from the rules tables under each blurb, and bring the blurbs themselves in line with the roll-high reading (ROLL-04) and the rubric (VOX-05).

Purpose: the user's Pixel 7 report ("summoner description doesn't mention that they don't get spells till 3. All sub class descriptions need to clearly tell their advantages/disadvantages"), since superseded in its Summoner detail by the 2026-09-25 ruling that removed the offense gate and gave the Summoner half-strength healing instead. A footer read from MU_CHART, the spell-level overrides and RACES cannot drift from the rules; the rules no table holds are authored once, each tied to the test that proves it.

Output: the traits table, the footer generator, the audited blurbs, the dossier and roller footers, the tests, the refreshed CLASS-PASS table, two declared snapshot regenerations and the ledger.
</objective>
