## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **CMBUI-10 edge probe: unclassified, reviewed manually.** Resolved as the event-order contract in the truths: ordering is by each line's earliest event index; equal positions cannot occur (one line per earliest event); "identical" means the same final text AND tone; the ×N suffix follows the existing " ×N" shape; a fold never crosses an intervening line.
- **Which surface is "the Oracle".** The pasted log in the todo carries the fold's table text with no dice ("Ned hits you 1 of 3 (2)", "Ned falls (+10 XP). ×2"), which is the combat fight log (at that build, the in-panel log; today THE FIGHT SO FAR sheet and the round strip). The ORACLE tab (#log) already prints one line per event in engine order and never folded; this plan pins that and changes only the fight-log fold. If the user meant #log should also fold identical back-to-back lines, that is a small shell follow-up.
- **The rail keeps its priority summary.** Out of combat the rail card is a one-card summary (Phase 35); CONTEXT's fix is about the combat record, and the todo says "the rail/toast summary keeps the priority fold".
- **Fewer summaries in the fight log.** Without cross-time aggregation, a foe with two swings reads as two lines instead of "hits you 1 of 2"; that is the cost of strict time order, and the round strip still shows the last three lines of the round.
