## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **"Guard" in CONTEXT's ability list is read as Brace** (the `braced` stance), the only guard-like effect with a live state. The Guard sub-class's always-on "+1" is identity, not an effect, and gets no chip; if the user wants permanent sub-class or race traits shown as chips, that is a separate ruling.
- **Member chips carry no measured lead.** conditionEffectText measures the HERO's odds; a member's Sidestep changes the foe's roll against that member, which the hero-side what-if cannot read. A member chip's sheet (77-08) states the ability's own content text instead (ABILITY_BY_ID[id].txt, already mechanical), plus its rounds and source.
- **The darkness chips.** When the persistent Darkness counter (`c.darkFor`) is running, its existing `darkness` chip covers the fight too; `fightDark` is emitted only for a fight on a dark SQUARE with the dark cap live and no counter running, so one cause never shows two chips. The measured lead for `fightDark` uses a what-if with Sense Presence up, which lifts exactly the dark cap (documented in the WHAT_IF comment).
- **Insulted has no measured number.** The insult modifies each foe's swing (foeSwingVsHero), not the base foeToHitVs the chip lead reads; its explanation (77-08) says what it does in words, and the number shows on the foe details odds line as "insulted −1" (Phase 74).
- **The summoned ally and a queued summon get no chip.** The ally is its own YOUR LOT card with its rounds; `c.pendingAlly` is a summon waiting for the next fight and appears as that card when the fight starts. Both go on NOT_A_CONDITION with those reasons.
- **The HUD strip shows the new keys raw until 77-08.** paintConditions falls back to the descriptor key as the label; between this plan and 77-08 (wave 3), a fight with Smoke up shows an "ability" chip in the strip. No build ships in between (the device round is at milestone close), and 77-08's guard then requires a label and an explanation for every key.
- **The "(now 19–20)" lead becomes "(19–20 instead of 17–20)"** on every chip tap (HUD strip and YOUR LOT alike), following CONTEXT's Dazed example, which is newer than Phase 74's example.
