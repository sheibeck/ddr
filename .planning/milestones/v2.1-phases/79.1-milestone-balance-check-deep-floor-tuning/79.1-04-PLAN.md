## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **FINAL is taken after the lock, not at the phase base.** The four tuned dials act only from floor 13, so floors 1-12 (the median, reach 5+ and 10+, and the per-floor rows 1-12) are the same before and after the lock. Measuring once on the locked code gives the shipped numbers with no duplicate readout set.
- **The fresh-start tail numbers come from the 1,000-seed text,** with the definitions 79.1-01 fixed (the same as `summarizeSlice`: every run counted, stuck included). If the locked row also measured the fresh slice, the two must agree. They are the same 1,000 runs, so a mismatch is a STOP.
- **Band verdicts use only targets already on record.** The per-floor pass band is the readout's own (±8 on floors 1-10, ±3 on 11-12). The four-band shape comes from the depth-20 target: median 5-7, p90 about 10-13, reach-20 at 1% or less (the ruled unicorn). Band shares are reported, not judged; no band-share target exists.
- **No isolated per-phase effects.** The per-plan BEFORE/AFTER readouts deferred from 75.2, 75.3, 76-01 and 78-01 (including 78-01's per-seed comparison) need mid-milestone baselines, which CONTEXT forbids. Their changes are measured together, v2.0 → FINAL, and the record says so rather than inventing a split.
- **Run time (planning-time probe):** 200 seeds 15-20 min, 1,000 seeds 75-100 min, the class matrix 15-25 min, the plain Sorcerer 1-3 min. The record takes 45-75 min.
