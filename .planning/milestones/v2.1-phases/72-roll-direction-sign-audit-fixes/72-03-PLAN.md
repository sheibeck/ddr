Site facts (verify each against the source; line numbers are approximate at eee9e10):
- Hero armor soak, engine/combat.js#applyFoeDamageToPlayer (~L2061, soak at ~L2169). A d20 is drawn ONLY when av.wp > 0, av.ar > 0 and the foe does not ignore armor. Soak succeeds on `soak <= soakAr`, where soakAr = armorSoak(c).ar, doubled (capped at the die ceiling) under Taunt. Probe it through foeTurn: draw 0 is the foe to-hit (use a draw value that lands and is not a crit, confirmed by events), then the damage dice (the foe's sp.dmg notation or a d6), then the soak d20. Outcome: an `armorSoaked` event.
- Foe natural soak, engine/foeDamage.js (~L97): `roll <= foe.sp.ar`, drawn ONLY for a non-crit landed blow against a foe with `sp.ar`. Probe it through playerStrike against a content foe with `sp.ar` (Drat, Krupke, Google, Craig, Herman). Outcome: `foeArmorSoaked`.
- Thrown spells, engine/magic.js#castSpell (~L480-540). A non-Apprentice MU with a thrown attack spell in the grimoire and a free charge, in a non-pending fight: the first draw is the to-hit die. Success is `roll - bonus <= target`. Outcome: `spellHit`. A member thrown spell is `allyCast` (~L1873), run from alliesTurn for an MU member. Outcome: `allySpellHit`.

Mods: Thief +5 (FLEE_THIEF_BONUS), FLEE_CLASS_MOD (MU −1), FLEE_RACE_MOD (Elven +1, Dwarven/Fridgian/Troll −1), armor bulk −bulk, FLEE_NEED_MOD added to the need. Avoid a pursues foe, a Master of Arms and the tracked branch.
