## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **Grant-time legality means the school gate only.** This follows the REQUIREMENTS RULES-03 text ("a grimoire never holds a spell from a school gated above the hero's level") and ROADMAP criterion 2. A book still holds higher-LEVEL spells, as canon does, because:
  - rollGrimoire deals a d10 book across spell levels;
  - the must-have grants (Summon lvl 2, Fireball lvl 3, Major Heal lvl 3) are above level 1 by design;
  - RULES-04's hide-in-combat rule exists precisely because books hold level-locked spells.
  75-CONTEXT's phrase "or spell level exceeds the hero's level" is read as the cast-time level lock that canCast already enforces. Flagged for the user.
- **The one draw-count change is the gate removal itself.** Removing the Summoner's gate lets its level-1 offense spells into rollGrimoire's day-one `spare` pool. That lengthens the existing shuffle for every Summoner, which moves seed 15's draws, and this is declared. The new legality filter never reorders a draw (it skips while walking), so 75-CONTEXT's "take the re-roll from a derived stream" clause is never triggered.
- **The Apprentice's level-3 reveal** drops book spells whose school is gated above 3 for its new sub-class (Warlock protection 4, Sorcerer healing 4, Court Mage divination 4); they can be learned again later. Joiners are rolled through the same level-1 rollGrimoire, so a Joiner's book is always legal at level 1.
- **VOX-04's text is stale.** The Phase 79 requirement still cites "the Summoner's no offense spells before level 3" as an example; the orchestrator should amend it. Not edited here.
