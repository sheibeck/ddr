# Phase 75.3 Plan 05: Control at depth — hero spells and items Summary

**Past floor 12, the hero's Freeze no longer kills on a hit. The foe first gets a roll-high resist, and if that fails it is held frozen for three rounds. Petrify, Stupidity and the stone items hold the same way, Blind and the sleep staves become timed, and Doze, Stun, Weaken, Shrink and the Vapor / Insane sleep outcomes can be shaken off. All of it uses 75.3-04's helpers. Floors 1-12 are pinned to the pre-plan engine, and the main dice never move because of a resist. The bot now treats a deep Freeze as a disable, not a kill.**

## Accomplishments

- `engine/magic.js#castSpell`: 10 `resistControl` sites and 3 `holdFoe` sites, each with a RULES-18 comment naming its audit id.
  - **Freeze (C1):** a blow that leaves the foe at 0 hp or below kills as today. Otherwise the foe gets a resist, then a frozen hold, and none of killFoe's reward draws happen.
  - **Petrify (C5)** and **Stupidity (C17):** a stone / stupid hold.
  - **Blind (C18):** timed with `blindFor` and `rounds` on `blinded`.
  - **Doze (C7), Stun (C8), Vapor's sleep (C10), Insane's face 4 (C11):** the die is drawn first, then the resist. `stunned.count` counts only the foes that slept.
  - **Shrink (C19):** a resist per foe, and `shrunk.count` counts only the halved.
  - **Weaken (C14):** one resist for the room. If it is shaken off, every live foe is marked Unmoved.
- `engine/items.js#useItem`:
  - **Birch freeze and Cedar gas:** a resist per foe, and a landed sleep lasts `controlCapRounds(depth, 99)`.
  - **Oak Staff and Amulet of Stone:** a resist per target, then a stone hold. `foeStoned` names only the killed foes.
  - **Walnut weaken:** one resist for the room. A landed weaken past the knee starts a three-round `spell:weaken` timer.
- Texts: Freeze, Ice, Stupidity, Blind and Petrify, the Birch, Oak and Cedar staves, and the Amulet of Stone each add a deadpan "past floor 12, … three rounds" clause. Every niche prefix is kept.
- `docs/SPELLS.md`: new "Phase 75.3 (RULES-18): control at depth" section. It covers the knee, the resist, the hold, timed-not-forever effects, the in/out audit ids, and how the Oracle and chips teach the resist.
- `tools/lib/tuning-bot.mjs`: a Freeze is KILL tier only while `controlHoldRoundsFor(depth) === 0`. Past the knee it scores 230 / 450 as a DISABLE, is allowed against a single foe, and `hasCastableKillTier` ignores it. The opt-in rotation is unchanged.
- `test/unit/control-spells-depth.test.js` (new, 22 tests): every spell and item row, the floor-12 pre-plan digests, the floors 1-12 loop, main-draw parity, the scroll path and the text-to-dial pin.
