# Phase 75.3: Deep-Floor Encounter Scaling - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-25 (user answers during the Phase 73 run)
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The number of foes in a fight grows with depth (RULES-16). Today `engine/difficulty.js#foeCountFor` makes 50% of fights solo on every floor (a d4 of 1-2 gives 1 foe), and depth never changes the count. A user's Troll Summoner on floor 23 met mostly solo fights and looked set for floor 30, well past the depth-20 unicorn target.

**EXPANDED 2026-09-25** (user: "balance our 12-20 levels and dial those up"; a friend's human Sorcerer reached depth 42 on a Freeze/Weaken/Doze rotation, meeting ~14 hp foes). The phase now also covers RULES-17 (the curve from floor 12) and RULES-18 (control spells at depth).

**Not in scope:** new hand-authored foe types (elite VARIANTS of existing foes are allowed), and UI beyond the rolls and chips these rules print.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions (user, 2026-09-25)
- **Solo fights fade with depth:**
  - Floors 1-4: as today (50% solo).
  - Floors 5-9: solo only on a d4 of 1 (25%).
  - Floors 10-19: at least 2 foes.
  - Floors 20+: at least 3 foes.
- **Same draws:** the existing d4 first roll and the table draw are kept. The depth rule clamps or reshapes the result. The planner must keep each path's draw count identical to today (no extra draw on a formerly-solo path, or a derived stream if one is unavoidable), so unrelated fixtures don't reorder.
- It applies to every fight that calls `foeCountFor` (wandering, dots, lairs). Encounters with an authored fixed count (named solo bosses, fixed rosters) keep it; the planner enumerates them.
- **Engine gate:**
  - Measure, declare and regenerate the moved fixtures. Never edit the prototype master.
  - Take a 200-seed readout before and after, with Phase 75.2 in place, recorded in `docs/DIFFICULTY-RETUNE.md` against the depth-20 unicorn / floor 5-7 average target.
  - Report per-band reach (5+, 10+, 20+). If the median falls well below 5, flag it for the user; don't auto-compensate.
- Written roll-high on Phase 73's helper, where the d4 is a selection draw, not a check (tag it accordingly).

### The deep-floor curve — RULES-17 (user, 2026-09-25)
- **Today:** `FOE_LEVEL` 0.9 + 0.29/depth is clamped 1..5, so the tier maxes at depth ~14. `FOE_HP_SCALE` 0.9 + 0.015/depth reaches ~1.53x at depth 42. `FOE_HIT_SCALE` is 0.6 + 0.01/depth.
- **Add a second, steeper slope from floor 12** for HP and hit: a piecewise dial `{ base, perDepth, kneeDepth: 12, perDepthAfter }`, identity below floor 12, so floors 1-11 don't move.
- **Keep the roster escalating past the level-5 tier:** either raise the clamp (only if bestiary tiers exist above 5), or add ELITE variants of existing foes at depth (+HP, +hit, a name tag such as "Elder" or "Dread"), which needs no new authoring.
- **Values come from 200-seed readouts** (checkpointed fit protocol) against the depth-20 unicorn / floor 5-7 average target. Engine-shape choices are reported to the user; the orchestrator only tunes search params.

### Control spells at depth — RULES-18 (user, 2026-09-25)
- From floor 12, foes increasingly RESIST control: Freeze, Stone, Doze/Sleep, Weaken, Stupid and the like.
- **Recommended:** a depth-scaled roll-high resistance check on Phase 73's helper (resistance faces grow with depth past 12), plus capping "indefinite" Freeze/Stone to a few rounds at depth. Diminishing returns on re-applying control to the same foe is optional.
- A foe that resists gets its own Oracle line and chip (Phase 77). The bot readout confirms a control-rotation Sorcerer no longer outlasts other builds.

### Design rulings after planning (user, 2026-09-25)
- **Freeze and Stone past the knee:** a landed Freeze, Ice's last tick, Petrify and the stone staves HOLD the foe for 3 rounds instead of killing it, and deep foes also get the resistance roll. Floors 1–12 are unchanged.
- **The knee:** floors 1–12 stay exactly as tuned, and **floor 13 is the first harder floor** (the Phase 54 fit boundary).
- **Wandering monsters get the depth minimum:** 2 on floors 10–19 and 3 on floor 20 and up, with no new draw. They stay solo on floors 1–9.
- **Tail targets.** The user's words: "Living past floor 20 should be exceedingly rare. 20 is the unicorn run. Player getting to depth 30 should basically never happen." The sweep's measurable targets, set by the orchestrator from that ruling:
  - Fresh start (floor 1), 1,000 seeds: reach floor 20 at about 1% or less (the unicorn), reach floor 21 or deeper under 0.5%, and reach floor 30 at 0 or 1 run in 1,000.
  - Floor-12 start slice: reach floor 20 at no more than 5%.
  - Floor-20 start slice: median floors gained of 2 or fewer, and reach floor 30 at 1% or less.
  - Floor-30 start slice: median floors gained 0, p90 of 1 or fewer.
  - The Freeze/Weaken/Doze rotation Sorcerer never outlasts the fair bot from the same depth.
  - Floors 1–12 are not refitted (no compensation). The average run still ends on floors 5–7 (the depth-20 target memory).
