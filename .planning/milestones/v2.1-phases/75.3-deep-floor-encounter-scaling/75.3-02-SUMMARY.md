## Decisions Made
- The rotation containment constraint (rot20 vs deep20's median, rot30 vs deep30's p90) is computed inside `scoreTail` as its own `constraints` field, but the REJECTED-candidate override (score → `Infinity`, verdict → `"MISS"`) is applied by `tailEvalRow`, not `scoreTail` itself — this mirrors `fit-score.mjs`'s own `classConstraints`/`evalRow` split exactly, keeping `scoreTail` a pure, directly-inspectable function.
- `summarizeSlice`'s `reach20`/`reach21`/`reach30`/`reachCount30` are computed over ALL rows (stuck included) per the plan's own spec; `p50Gained`/`p90Gained`/`meanGained`/`p50Death` are non-stuck only — a stuck run that hit the action cap at floor 30 still proves the depth was reached, even though its own "floors gained" isn't a completed result.
- Missed-target terms use `1 + max(0, delta)` so an exact-boundary "below" miss (e.g. `reach21 === 0.5`) is still a strictly positive term, and slice-tier misses are multiplied by a large factor (`1,000,000`) so any slice miss always outscores every possible combination of fresh-tier terms — satisfying the plan's lexicographic requirement without a more complex two-field score.
