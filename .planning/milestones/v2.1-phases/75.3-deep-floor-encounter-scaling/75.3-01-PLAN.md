## Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not silently decided)

- **"50% solo" on floors 1-4 is really 62.5% today.** The shipped FOE_COUNT_SKEW is 1, whose table row [1, 2, 2, 3] gives a solo fight on a second-draw face of 1, so P(solo) = 0.5 + 0.5 x 0.25. The ruling says "as today"; today's number is kept and reported as 62.5%.
- **Floors 20+ are always exactly three foes** (non-wandering). The count table tops out at 3, so "at least 3" is exactly 3. Four or more would be a new rule, not planned.
- **Wandering fights get the depth minimum (ruled by the user, 2026-09-25, 75.3-CONTEXT "Design rulings after planning").** 2 foes on floors 10-19, 3 on floors 20+, solo on floors 1-9, with no new draw (a wandering fight has no d4 to reinterpret; the code never routed it through foeCountFor).
- **Sub-class routs still apply after the count.** A Knight's under-5-hp rout, a Con Artist's lowly-foe rout and the Court Mage's rule can leave fewer foes than the drawn count; they are the sub-class's identity, not a count rule.
- **The thresholds are a dial with an "off" identity of 0**, like the other DIALS keys the identity column pins; fitting them is deferred (75.3-CONTEXT Deferred).
