Writer's rules (CONTEXT 'Tone' of Phase 95, restated; enforced by flavor-layer, the safety scan and the hygiene checks):
1. One sentence; at most 100 characters (aim at 90 or fewer); exactly one sentence-ending mark, at the end; colons, semicolons and commas are fine.
2. Sarcastic, deadpan, family-friendly; nothing from content/safety-wordlist.js.
3. No number in any form: no digit, no dN, no percent sign, none of two..twenty, thirty..ninety, hundred, once, twice, half, double; "one" only as an idiom. So a cooldown, a bonus, a duration and a limit are hinted in words ("saved for emergencies", "a favour the foe will remember") or left to RULES.
4. Hints at what the ability does in plain words; points the same way as its txt; never promises more; never restates a rule the toggle hides.
5. Every line different from every other line in the whole layer. Siblings stay distinguishable at a glance: Kata vs Feint (identical rules sentence), Sidestep vs Smoke vs Brace vs Taunt vs Riposte (defence), Second Wind vs Last Stand, Death Touch vs Silent Step vs Overhead Blow, Hamstring vs Mark vs Dirty Trick, Poisoned Edge vs Cutpurse. The Sing line must say the song picks the spell.
6. British spelling ("armour"); "HP" never "WP"; no "Maze Master"; no retired working title; plain apostrophes.
7. A skill line does not repeat its ability twin's wording (the twin has its own line) and does not open with the skill's own name.
