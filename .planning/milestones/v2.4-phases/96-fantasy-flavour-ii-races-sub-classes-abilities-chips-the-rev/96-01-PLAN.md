<objective>
Lay the identity half of the two-layer model: the record shape that carries a blurb plus the good and bad it hints at, the per-domain length and sentence rule, the tag guard (with teeth), and then write and ledger the race and class lines. Nothing on screen changes yet (the roller and the Hero tab read these maps in 96-05).

Implements these CONTEXT decisions (96-CONTEXT.md):
- 'Race, sub-class and class blurbs: Shape': up to two sentences, about 200 characters, no numbers, dice or percentages; the identity domains get their own length and sentence rule in flavor-layer, the other domains keep the one-sentence rule.
- 'Good and bad are guarded by tags': each identity entry carries its line plus the identity good and bad ids it hints at (ids from `identityEntries`, the Phase 24 identity contract); a test checks every race blurb is tagged with at least one real good and one real bad; the map's shape still lets `lines()` return plain strings.
- 'Class blurbs are included': `CLASS_FLAVOR`, plain strings, no tags (a class has no identity good and bad table).
- 'Surfaces' (the data half): `RACE_NOTE` / `CLASS_NOTE` and the mechanical footer stay untouched for RULES.
- 'The review': every new line passes the safety scan and the voice-inventory checks (roll-under, hygiene and safety count 0) and gets a new-line ledger row on the regenerated review page.

Claude's discretion used: the record shape is `{ line, good, bad }` with an optional `neutral` id, adapted by `lines()`; the tag vocabulary is exactly the `id` values `identityEntries` lists.

Purpose: FLAVOR-03 data for races and classes, plus the machinery 96-02 reuses for the 24 sub-classes.
Output: one shell-module extension, three test files, one content block, one ledger, two regenerated pages.
