<objective>

Ships with unit tests only: **no player-visible behaviour yet** (ROADMAP SC-6 is the gate).

Per CONTEXT §"engine/effects.js Timer Model" (every point locked): lazy `c.timers` like `f.cd`; the entry shape above keyed by a consumer-chosen id (e.g. `ability:whirl`, `item:Cloak of Light`, `spell:reveal`); parity carve-out now in all three `*Comparable()` fns plus the local duplicates; load tolerant of an absent field (never injected); pure functions, no classes; tick functions RETURN the list of ids that expired or flipped (`{ id, from: "effect", to: "cooldown" | null }`) so the CALLER pushes events — effects.js emits no events; tick sites: per-step `tickSquares(c, stepCost)` (pass the cost — Phase 41's 2-move water squares tick squares-based timers consistently for free, TERR-02), round tail `tickRounds(c)`, `endCombat` → `clearRoundTimers(c)` (Phase 31 ward/afraid "cleared unconditionally at endCombat" precedent); squares-cadence timers survive combat; duration→cooldown pairs flip automatically at `left === 0` when `cd` is set (then `left = cd`), the record is deleted when the cooldown expires, both transitions reported.

Discretion resolved here (CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion" — internal layout): test file is `test/unit/effects.test.js`; a record's `cd` is kept on the record through its cooldown phase (harmless, simpler); once `c.timers` exists the key stays (an empty map is legal — mirrors `f.cd`); a multi-square tick (`n > 1`) floors `left` at 0 and does NOT carry overshoot into the cooldown; invalid inputs are silent no-ops (never throw — engine V5 discipline).

Purpose: Phases 38–40 share one timer shape instead of inventing three bespoke ones (Pitfall 2: per-step vs per-round desync and save/load loss).
Output: `engine/effects.js`, the three tick-site calls, `clearStaleTimers` in `saveState.js`, `stripTimersField` in the harness, `test/unit/effects.test.js`.
