## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Interpretation] "A second song 5 rounds after the first" is 5 rounds of the fight's round counter.** Sung in round 1, due in round 6 (rounds 2 to 5 rest). The Joiner Bard sings its second song on its own first turn from round 6; the hero's and the Joiner's clocks are separate.

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] `songResting` refusal.** The ruling is silent on what a too-early SING says. A second SING inside the gap used to be `sungThisFight`, which would now lie, so the gap gets its own refusal reason with the rounds left (narrated on both surfaces, in a voice-guard toggle) and `sungThisFight` is kept for after the second song ("Two songs a fight.").

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Five tests relied on the Cloaker's parting blow.** The Cloaker's vanish was a convenient zero-draw route into `pursuitStrike`; they now use the tracked round-1 withdrawal (same draws, same expected numbers). The two condition coverage guards, which scan every flag the engine assigns, needed `sangAt` listed as a reasoned NOT_A_CONDITION.

**4. [Process] Pins and engine landed in one commit.** As in part A, the pins were written with the engine and run green together rather than committed red first; the three text pins went in the text task's commit.

**5. [Handoff for 91.1-05] GAP wording.** The Cloaker row keeps a `GAP:` clause recording the fixed finding (K15, "fixed in 91.1-03") because the ledger test requires a `gap` flag to name a `GAP:` item; the close plan converts it. `docs/SKILL-AUDIT.md`'s Sing row and `SING_TEXT` in `test/unit/skill-audit.test.js` (91.1-05's doc drift) still pass and were left alone.
