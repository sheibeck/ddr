## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Interpretation] Taunt's "two rounds".** Taunt is a "this round" ability, so its two rounds are the use round's foe turn and the next one (two foe turns, `abilityEffectTicks("taunt") = 2`, the chip reads 1 right after the use), not the "two rounds after the use round" every "for two rounds" ability gets (Sidestep, Battle Roar, Smoke: three foe turns). I read the ruling ("Taunt lasts two rounds", from one) as doubling the foe turns. If the user meant the Sidestep convention, it is `THIS_ROUND_ABILITIES` losing `taunt` (three foe turns, text "for two rounds").

**2. [Interpretation] Mark's level is the marker's.** "Your level" is read as the level of whoever laid the Mark (the hero's `c.level`, a Joiner Thief's own level), stored as the number on the foe's existing `marked` flag, so a Joiner's strike on a foe the hero marked adds the hero's level.

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Foe chip text.** The Marked chip's description stated "2 more damage"; it now says extra damage by the level of whoever studied it (found by the voice corpus change, ledgered).

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug, tests] Existing pins that assumed the old numbers.** Runes reads in `scroll-read` and `cast-refusals` assumed the scroll was always spent (the fake rng reads cursor 0, where the keep fires at acts 0); they now search `state.acts` for a spent read. `conditions.test.js` pinned `braced === true`. All re-based with dated comments.

**5. Commit layout.** Four commits (the plan's three tasks plus one small follow-up for `cast-refusals`); the new-event narration and the part-B text landed in Task 1 with the engine (the coverage guards need them), as in part A.
