# Phase 90 Plan 12: The text-vs-engine guard, the audit close and the phase gate Summary

**Every number a spell, ability or skill text states (179 of them across 41 spells, 20 abilities and the passive skills) is claimed by a fact and equals what a real cast, use, foe turn or chest roll does; both audit tables are closed with 108 distinct real pins and no open verdict; the Phase 90 close sections and the draw list are written; the full suite is 9,222 tests with 0 failures.**

## What was built

### Task 1: the guard (commit 462cb6f5)

- `test/unit/spell-skill-text-engine.test.js` (88 tests). `numbersIn(text)` reads digits, number words, dice (`d10`, `2d6+2`, `d4+1`), U+2212 signs and U+2013 ranges (so `−2 to hit` is -2 and `16–20` is the range 16 to 20), and `checkRow(name, text, facts)` fails a changed number, an unclaimed number, a number two facts claim and a vanished sentence.
- `SPELL_TEXT_FACTS` (one entry per spell, 41) and `SKILL_TEXT_FACTS` (one per ability, passive skill and `txt2`, 32). Each `value()` is measured: a recording rng on a real `castSpell` gives the dice a spell draws (a Doze reach is the first d4 and sleeps exactly that many foes), the foe's winning faces through `foeSwingVsHero` give every d20 range, plain and insulted, `foeTurn` gives every "half damage", `openChest` gives the lock ranges and the free chest, `newDay` the Sewing numbers, `heardSquares` Acute Hearing's reach, `fight` the Hardiness phobia shrug, and the school stretch is the difference between two real casts divided by the chart's bonus step.
- Two guards on the guard: changing any one of the 179 stated numbers fails on exactly that number (the doctored text is run through `checkRow` for every token of every row), and dropping any fact leaves an unclaimed number. The dice rows facts read from content (Heal, Major Heal, Freeze, Acid, Ice, Earthquake, Fireballs, Fireball, Lightning, Mangle) are checked against the engine's minimum and maximum, and `FREEZE_HOLD_DIE`, `KATA_FEINT_NEED_SHIFT`, `SWEEP_MIN_FOES` and `DURATION_ROUNDS` are pinned.
- **No drift was found.** No text changed, so no `docs/narrative-pass/why/90-12.json` was written; `node tools/narrative-review.mjs --check` is in sync.

## Flagged assumptions for the user's review (collected across the phase)

- **90-02, Pommel Strike (ABIL-07).** A hit is any landed blow of the strike on the target (a double strike stuns once, a killing blow or a shatter stuns nobody, a miss stuns nothing); the 4-round cooldown is spent whether or not the blow lands; a hit armour soaks (no damage) still stuns; a foe that revives from the blow is stunned.
- **90-03, Strength.** Earthquake adds ONE d10 to its one roll (every foe takes it; the caster's backlash stays the dice alone); a critical doubles the whole roll with the d10 in it; a Joiner sheet holding a Strength record would get the d10 on its blows.
- **90-04, one resist, Blind, Stupidity.** Blind's "only on the maximum roll" is read as a hard cap applied AFTER the insult (an insulted party faces only the top face; before, two); a Stupidity'd foe in a Bard's party is sent straight at the hero (intelligence 3 or less, `pickFoeTarget`); Dirty Trick's line said "two rounds" on a spell-blinded foe until 90-10 fixed it.
- **90-05, Doze, Stun, Ice.** "A hit wakes it" means ANY damage the dozing foe takes through `damageFoe` (a ward or armour that absorbs it wakes nobody); Doze's reach starts at your current target; a longer live hold stands against a shorter new one; Ice has no up-front resist and counts as an attack spell (a Wizard who knows it refuses melee; a Joiner may pick it).
- **90-06, removals and gates.** The chargen rng order moved for the Wizard, Illusionist and Apprentice (39 to 36, 34 to 33, 38 to 37; declared and regenerated, not padded); three magic parity scenarios were re-picked onto unchanged sub-classes and lose-apprentice declares an `rngShift`; the Wizard's `SUB_NOTE` still says "Every school of magic" (91-02); no ledger row exists for the new `spellSchoolLocked` forbidden line; the bot's `campFailed` loop was exposed.
- **90-07, the four Special spells.** The footer does not state the Special stretch; `eventNarration.js` imports `content/spells.js`; chip labels read "Open/Lock" and "Enchanted"; Open/Lock in a fight only arms the next chest.
- **90-08, the control spells.** Weaken's three-face to-hit cap is not applied to a misdirected swing (only its damage halving); a misdirected blow ignores the per-round damage ceiling; the Q6 stretch is +0 for both Illusion spells because the chart gives the Illusion school 0.
- **90-09, the Illusion spells and the book.** The Illusionist's grants come after the day-one top-ups (so the rolled part of every book is unchanged); Door Illusion is never greyed in the real menu (only a Samurai reading a scroll reaches the refusal); no "Fled" foe-card state; Chameleon Tongue pays whatever `parley()` pays (PARLEY-01 applies to it).
- **90-10, Joiners.** A Joiner's Enchant Character does not give its staff blows the +2 to hit; a Joiner's Ambidextrous is the plain strike only; a Joiner's Stealth dark ban applies to the Stealth crit only; a fixated Walking Dead's ability bolts use the plain pick; the Joiner form of `spellEffectStarted` covers haste and enchant only.
- **90-11, the wording.** The Behemoth's d8 example is gone (one d20 example); Blind and Dirty Trick state no insulted case on purpose; Stealth and a fumbled Mirror Self say "the top number(s) of your die"; the parley insult chip, `parleyDesc`, the Bard's song chip and the Ninja line were not touched (Phase 91's).
- **90-12, the guard (mine).** "Phobias halved" is measured as the Hardiness shrug-off chance of a real fight opening (1 face of a d2); "a quarter" for Last Stand is the largest allowed hp over max hp; the four non-number idioms listed above are excluded by name, nothing else.
