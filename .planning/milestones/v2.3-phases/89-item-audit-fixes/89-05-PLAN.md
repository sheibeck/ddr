The automatic in-fight policy is 89-06; the Company panel buttons are 89-07.

Implements these CONTEXT decisions (89-CONTEXT.md, "Joiners use their items and soak hits (ITEM-07; accepted 2026-09-30)"):
- "Both ways: automatically in combat, and player-directed outside combat ... Outside combat: a USE on the Hero tab's Company panel lets the player make the Joiner drink a potion or use a worn item." (this plan builds the engine action the panel dispatches)
- "Carried gear is worn on joining: a cloak or jewel in a Joiner's bag (the Thief's starting cloak) goes into a free slot when the Joiner joins, so it can be used."
- "Item effects a Joiner starts carry src and end through Phase 88's endSourceEffects like the hero's (the link already works on any sheet)."
- "Every new event gets its EVENT_NARRATION entry and rail twin; any new roll comes from a derived stream."
- The 89-01 checkpoint rulings Q2 (the Joiner's movement and sense items) and Q3 (the Magic User Joiner's scroll), recorded in docs/ITEM-AUDIT.md ## Rulings.
