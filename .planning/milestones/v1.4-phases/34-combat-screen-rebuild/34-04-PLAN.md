<objective>
Replace the old 7-button action bar with the mock's four-action grid and submenus (CSCR-05) driven by `combatMenuViewModel`, rework the keyboard map, and move the last two shell-side in-combat toasts (potion at full health / no potions) into dull › log entries so that after this plan no toast can fire during a fight (CSCR-04's "no toasts anywhere" completes here). Every new tap target goes through `guardTap` (CSCR-08) and the tests that pinned the old ids are re-pinned (CSCR-09).

Discretion decisions: the ITEMS submenu rows are built by the same `cbRow` builder as SPELLS/SOCIAL (whole-row tap in the mock's vocabulary) rather than a fifth `renderCarriedList` host — potions/scrolls are counters on `S.c`, not carried items, so a mixed list would have needed two row styles; `renderCarriedList` keeps exactly its loot host with `guard: true`. Rows with no engine refusal path (a Potion at 0 or at full health — engine/magic.js#drinkPotion is silent in both cases) get a shell-side dull entry through `fightLogRefuse` so nothing ever happens silently. A tap on a disabled grid button whose `opens` is null (Fighter ABILITIES) is a guarded no-op — there is no engine action to refuse and the sub-line already says NOTHING UP YOUR SLEEVE.

Purpose: the player-facing action model of the new screen; Plan 05 finishes the endings.
Output: `mazeworld.html` (grid/submenu renderer, dispatch table, dull refusal helper, keydown), `test/unit/shell-combat-actions.test.js`, four re-pinned suites.
</objective>
