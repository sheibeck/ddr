# Skill and ability audit (ABIL-06, and the TEXT-01 inventory for skill rows)

**Phase:** 90-spell-skill-audit (plan 90-01)
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** open for fixes. The audit is written and its two balance calls (Q10 and Q11) are ruled by the user on 2026-09-30. No engine, content or shell file was changed: the audit records, plans 90-02 to 90-12 fix.

Every Fighter skill, every Thief skill, every pool ability and the Bard's Sing has one row: what its text promises, what the engine does, its cooldown or once-per-fight rule, whether it auto-hits or forces a critical, its bonus terms, who can use it (the hero, and a Joiner), what canon says, and a verdict. A mismatch is never waved through: it is fixed by a named plan, or it is a recorded ruling. The spell twin of this table is `docs/SPELL-AUDIT.md`.

## How to read this table

**Text** is what the player reads: the `txt` (and `txt2`) in `content/skills.js` and `content/abilities.js`, the Hero tab's special-skills list and ability rows (`src/browser/heroTab.js#abilitiesViewFor`: the txt plus its state "ready", "cd N rounds" or "once a fight"), and the combat menu's ABILITIES row (`src/browser/combatMenu.js#abilityRows`: READY, READY · ONCE PER FIGHT, ONCE PER FIGHT · SPENT or N ROUNDS, and the txt as its description).

**Engine** names the file and function that runs the skill and what it does in numbers.

**Rule** states the cooldown (rounds) or "once per fight" (`cd: "fight"`, cleared by `endCombat`), whether the strike auto-hits or forces a critical, the to-hit shift in roll-high form ("+3 to hit", "−2 to hit"), the damage bonus, the targets and the minimum foe count.

**Hero** names the engine site that runs it for the hero (the `useAbility` case, or the passive read). **Joiner** names the engine site for a Joiner (`engine/combat.js#resolveMemberAbility`'s case and the `pickMemberAbility` tag) or says "cannot" and why (a passive no Joiner code ever reads). "Who can use it: a Joiner uses a skill the way its text describes" is the standing rule (90-CONTEXT).

**Joiner use policy** (`engine/combat.js#pickMemberAbility`, no rng): in round 1 the first ready ability tagged opener; else against a foe above half hp the first ready ability tagged damage (Last Stand only at or below a quarter of its hp); else, below half its own hp, the first ready ability tagged defensive; else a plain strike (or a spell, for a Magic User). A Joiner uses only abilities of its own class that its sheet owns.

**Canon** cites the rulebook (`mazeworld.pdf`, printed page) and the frozen prototype (`test/parity/prototype-master.js.txt`, line, read only). The user's rulings override both, and the Canon cell names the ruling when it does. Most abilities are Phase 38 additions with no rulebook entry.

**Default fix direction:** the engine does what the text promises, unless the text is a typo or the user ruled otherwise. A wording-only fix is the text's; a fix that moves balance materially is a balance call and goes to the user before it is built.

**Verdict vocabulary** (`test/unit/skill-audit.test.js` accepts exactly these): `match`; `fix engine (90-NN)` and `fix text (90-NN)`; `balance call (Qn)`; `ruled (Qn, 2026-09-30) -> 90-NN`; `ruled (2026-09-30) -> 90-NN`; `Phase 91 (IDENT-NN)` and `Phase 92` for rows handed on. When 90-12 closes the audit the open `fix` verdicts turn into `fixed engine (90-NN)` and `fixed text (90-NN)`. `Pinned by` is `—` until a fix lands.

**Plan owners:** 90-02 Pommel Strike, hero and Joiner (ABIL-07); 90-04 the depth-rising resist, which also lets a blinded foe's top-face hit stop being a critical (Blind and Dirty Trick share the flag); 90-10 every other audit engine fix (Joiner skill use included); 90-11 TEXT-01 wording for every skill row (faces become "+N to hit" or "foes −N to hit you" with the d20 range named); 90-12 the text-vs-engine guard and the audit close.

**Two facts the rows lean on.** A duration ability with a cooldown runs its effect first and then its cooldown (`engine/effects.js#startEffect`), and the foe turn of the use round ticks the fresh timer once, so "for two rounds" covers the use round and two more (`abilities.js#abilityEffectTicks`). And an ability's cooldown starts when it is used: `cd: "fight"` means spent until the fight ends.

Rows follow `FIGHTER_SKILLS` key order, then `THIEF_SKILLS` key order, then `ABILITY_POOL` (Fighter, then Thief), then Sing. A table skill whose `active` marker names a catalog ability is one row (its catalog entry is the same line), never two. `test/unit/skill-audit.test.js` pins that order and fails on a missing, duplicate or unknown row.

### Q10 Do Joiners use their combat passives (answered A)

**Rows:** Stealth, Hardiness, Ambidextrous (Fighter passives). **Today:** a Joiner is a full character sheet and rolls these skills like you do, but no Joiner code ever reads them: `playerStrike` (Stealth, Ambidextrous) and `applyFoeDamageToPlayer` (Hardiness) are the hero's alone, and `applyFoeDamageToMember` leaves Hardiness out on purpose. The standing rule is that a Joiner uses a skill the way its text describes. The other passives (Locks, Sewing, Night Vision, Acute Hearing, Cooking, Runes/Signs) are exploration and upkeep jobs the hero does, and a Joiner's Heft already works for its damage and its mail.

- **A (recommended; answered): a Joiner uses them as the text describes.** Hardiness: each blow that lands on it takes 3 less (floor 1); Ambidextrous: a second swing every round; Stealth: its opening landed blow crits on a roll in the top two numbers of its die (never in plate, never in the dark). Joiners get tougher and a little stronger, which Phase 92 measures once.
- **B: they stay the hero's alone.** A Joiner's Stealth, Hardiness and Ambidextrous stay dead weight (its Joiner cell keeps reading "cannot"), and the skill text keeps saying "you".

### Q11 What a missed Death Touch costs (answered A)

**Row:** Death Touch. **Today:** the text says "your next landed blow doubles, and finishes anything under 15 hp", but the engine makes the use ONE swing: if it misses, the once-per-fight use is spent and nothing waits for a later blow (Kata and Feint behave the same, and their text says "this strike").

- **A (recommended; answered): keep the engine and reword it** ("one swing: if it lands it doubles, and it finishes anything under 15 hp; once per fight"). No power change; the text stops promising a wait that never happens.
- **B: the engine follows the words:** the doubling waits for the next landed blow, so a miss costs only the turn and the once-per-fight use is spent on the blow that lands. Death Touch gets stronger, and a Joiner's version needs the same wait.
