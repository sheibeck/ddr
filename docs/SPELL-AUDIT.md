# Spell audit (SPELL-08, and the TEXT-01 inventory for spell rows)

**Phase:** 90-spell-skill-audit (plan 90-01)
**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** open for fixes. The audit is written and all nine spell balance calls are ruled by the user on 2026-09-30 (Q1 to Q6 before the audit ran, Q7 to Q9 at the batched checkpoint; see Rulings). No engine, content or shell file was changed: the audit records, plans 90-02 to 90-12 fix.

Every spell in the game has one row, and so does every spell the user ruled in (the ten-spell Special and Illusion slate): what its text promises, what the engine does, the real odds in roll-high form, what canon says, and a verdict. A mismatch is never waved through: it is fixed by a named plan, or it is a recorded ruling. The plan that builds each fix is named in the Verdict column. The skill and ability twin of this table is `docs/SKILL-AUDIT.md`.

## How to read this table

**Text** is what the player reads: the `txt` line in `content/spells.js`, the Grimoire row (`src/browser/heroTab.js#grimoireViewModel`: `L{level} name` and the txt), the combat menu row (`src/browser/combatMenu.js#combatMenuViewModel`: `LVL n`, the txt, and for a spell cast on a foe the hint `{target} resists on {range}`, which prints the current target's half-intelligence resist range) and the chips the spell puts on the hero (`src/browser/heroConditions.js`) or on a foe (`src/browser/foeConditions.js`).

**Engine** names the file and function that runs the spell and what it does in numbers. A Joiner's cast is named where `engine/combat.js#allyCast` handles the spell (it casts only `ATTACK_SPELL_KINDS`: status, thrown, stun and weaken, chosen by `derived.js#bestAttackSpell`).

**Rolls** states, in roll-high form (every to-hit and resist reads "hits on X–20 (d20)" or "+N to hit", with the real odds named; a damage die never reads like a to-hit roll): the to-hit roll or "no to-hit roll"; the resist or "never resisted"; the damage dice and multipliers (the level² term every damage spell adds since 2026-09-28, the school bonus, the p.26 level multiplier where it still applies) or "no damage"; the duration; and the backfire and fumble odds (the Apprentice's 1 in 8, the Summoner's 1 in 8 on its doubled summons, the scroll fumble row). The depth-rising resist note ("depth-rising resist, no floor-12 extras -> 90-04") is the user's ruling at the Phase 89 checkpoint (docs/ITEM-AUDIT.md Q1) and sits in every row whose spell a foe can resist.

**Canon** cites the rulebook (`mazeworld.pdf`, printed page) and the frozen prototype (`test/parity/prototype-master.js.txt`, line, read only). The user's rulings override both, and the Canon cell names the ruling when it does.

**Default fix direction:** the engine does what the spell text promises (the text is the promise to the player), unless the text is a typo or the user ruled otherwise. A wording-only fix is the text's; a fix that moves balance materially (a number that changes a lot, a reach that widens, a limit that goes away, a spell that gets weaker) is a balance call and goes to the user before it is built.

**Verdict vocabulary** (`test/unit/spell-audit.test.js` accepts exactly these):

- `match` — text, engine and canon (as overridden by rulings) agree.
- `fix engine (90-NN)` and `fix text (90-NN)` — a mismatch owned by plan 90-NN (an engine fix that does not move balance materially, or a wording fix).
- `balance call (Qn)` — a balance-moving mismatch waiting on the user (see Balance calls).
- `ruled (Qn, 2026-09-30) -> 90-NN` — a balance call the user ruled, with the plan that builds it.
- `ruled (2026-09-30) -> 90-NN` — a user ruling of that date settles the row and plan 90-NN builds it (the Doze and Stun swap, the depth-rising resist, the reworks and removals named in 90-CONTEXT).
- `new (90-NN)` — a ruled-in slate spell that plan 90-NN builds.
- `removed (90-06)` — a ruled-out spell that 90-06 deletes.
- `Phase 91 (IDENT-NN)` and `Phase 92` — handed to a later phase.
- When 90-12 closes the audit the open `fix` verdicts turn into `fixed engine (90-NN)` and `fixed text (90-NN)`.

`Pinned by` is `—` until a fix lands; the fix plan fills it with `test/unit/<file>.test.js: <test title>`.

**Plan owners** in the Verdict column: 90-02 Pommel Strike (skills doc); 90-03 Strength and spell-sourced timed effects; 90-04 the one depth-rising resist for every spell (no floor-12 extras), Petrify, Blind and Stupidity; 90-05 Doze, Stun and Ice; 90-06 Lesser Summon and Phantom Host removed, the Summoner's Summon from level 1, the Wizard's lost Illusion school, the school-gate guard; 90-07 Open/Lock, Fly, Enchant Character, Speed of Sound; 90-08 Stop Time, Senseless, Duplicate Foe; 90-09 Door Illusion, Chameleon Tongue, Size of the Behemoth and the Illusionist's starting book; 90-10 Joiner casters for the new and reworked spells and every other audit engine fix; 90-11 TEXT-01 wording for every spell row (faces become "+N to hit" with the d20 range named, "squares of" becomes how many foes an area spell hits, parley wording where it appears); 90-12 the text-vs-engine guard and the audit close.

**Two facts the rows lean on.** A spell is the round's action, so the foe turn that follows the cast in the same dispatch ticks a fresh timer once: a "d4 rounds" effect covers the foe visits of the cast round and the rounds after it, d4 visits in all. And a fight holds at most three foes (`engine/difficulty.js#FOE_COUNT_TABLE`), so a reach of "d6 foes" reaches every foe on 4 rolls in 6.

## Cross-cutting rules

**The universal intelligence resist (as it stands).** Every spell cast on a foe, damage spells included, rolls `combat.js#foeResistsSpell` once per targeted foe (user ruling 2026-09-27): half the foe's intelligence in winning faces on a d20, `max(1, round(intelligence / 2))`, so the foe resists on `21 − faces` or better. Intelligence 1–2 resists on 20 (5%), 3 on 19–20, 6 on 18–20 (15%), 10 on 16–20 (25%), 16 on 13–20 (40%), 20 on 11–20 (50%). A resisted spell does nothing to that foe; the turn and the charge are spent. The roll comes from a derived stream, so it never moves the main rng. The caster's own kinds (`SPELL_SELF_KINDS`: summon, ward, might, regen, heal, reveal, foresee, mirror, senses) are never resisted. Past floor 12 the control sites (Doze, Stun, Stupidity, Blind, Shrink, Ice's freeze, Noxious Vapor's sleep, Insane's sleep, Petrify) also roll `resistControl` (1 face in 20 at floor 13, one more a floor, up to 15), and a landed control is capped at three rounds (the RULES-18 knee, `engine/difficulty.js#CONTROL_AT_DEPTH`).

**The ruled replacement (one depth-rising resist, no floor-12 extras -> 90-04).** The user ruled at the Phase 89 checkpoint (docs/ITEM-AUDIT.md Q1, and again after 89-08 for damage spells: "I'm good with that.") that every spell and spell-like effect uses ONE resist that rises with depth, and that no spell keeps a "past floor 12" special effect. `engine/derived.js#risingResistFaces(depth, intel)` is the helper (faces = `min(19, round(a + c − a·c/20))`, `a` the half-intelligence faces, `c` the floor faces from floor 13), and `combat.js#foeResistsEffect` is the gate, both built in 89-08. Intelligence 10 resists on 16–20 up to floor 12, 15–20 at floor 13, 10–20 at floor 20 (55%) and 5–20 at floor 27. A landed spell is its floor-1 form at every depth: no three-round cap, no hold, no second resist. 90-04 moves every spell site onto the helper, and moves the combat menu's resist hint and the foe card from `resistFaces(intel)` to `risingResistFaces(depth, intel)`. The spell sites still on the old machinery today: in `engine/combat.js` the Bard's `sing` (Phase 91), a Joiner's Doze and Stun in `allyCast`, `foeTurn`'s Ice payoff and `holdFoe`'s default hold; in `engine/magic.js` stun, stupid, blind, shrink, vapor, insane, status (Doze) and petrify.

**Damage: the dice plus level².** A damage spell adds the caster's level squared (`derived.js#spellLevelSq`, user 2026-09-28): once to a thrown hit, to each foe a Lightning throw hits, to each foe an Earthquake damages, to the first bolt that strikes each foe in Fireballs, and to the first tick of Acid and Ice. It is never added to a heal, to the Earthquake backlash, to an Apprentice's backfire or to a fumbled scroll. Canon p.26's multiplier (`max(1, caster level − spell level)`) survives in two places: Stun's reach (the count goes away with the SPELL-11 swap) and a fumbled scroll's damage to the reader (`scrollFumble.js`). Strength's +d10 (Q1) adds one d10 to each damage roll the hero makes as it lands, and none to a damage-over-time tick.

**The school bonus.** `castSpell`'s thrown branch and `allyCast` add the chart's school bonus to a thrown spell's winning faces and nowhere else (the prototype did the same). Rulebook p.18 says the bonus also adds to damage, number affected, additional healing or squares; the user's Q6 stretches only the new Special and Illusion spells (+10 squares a point on a square-timed spell, +1 round a point on a round-timed one).

**Backfires.** An Apprentice's every cast goes wrong on a 1 on a d8 (1 in 8, 12.5%): the spell does nothing, and a thrown spell also costs the caster half its damage roll. A Summoner's doubled summon (Summon and Phantom Host, not Lesser Summon) turns on the Summoner on a 1 on a d8 for level² + d6. Both are `rng.d(8)` rolls tagged `roll:mishap-on-1`, deliberately not mirrored to a roll-high form (a 1 is always the worst face). A Joiner's cast never backfires (Findings, Phase 91).

**Scroll reading (RULES-10) and the scroll pool.** Anyone may read any scroll and the scroll is consumed on every attempt. A Magic User reads automatically (and copies the spell into the book when the gates allow); a Runes/Signs reader reads automatically with no copy; everyone else rolls a d20 on `scrollReadBands`: intelligence 14 reads on 8–20, a roll below half the target (1–3) fumbles and anything between is garbled. A fumble in a fight turns the spell on the reader or its side per `content/scroll-fumbles.js` (one row per spell); outside a fight it fizzles. A combat-only spell read outside a fight is consumed and refused. The scroll's spell is `rng.pick(SPELLS.filter(sp => sp.lvl <= min(5, depth + 1)))`. Today that pool holds 16 spells at depth 1 (levels 1 to 2), 25 at depth 2, 30 at depth 3 and 33 from depth 4. After Phase 90 (Lesser Summon and Phantom Host out, ten spells in) it holds 19, 29, 36 and 41, and the user's mid-run ruling pins that both new schools appear at each band the level allows and the removed spells never do.

## Balance calls

Written for the user: each question has the rows it covers, the numbers, and a recommended answer to accept or change. Q1 to Q6 were put to the user before this audit ran, Q7 to Q9 are the questions this audit found, and all nine are answered (see Rulings). Q10 and Q11 are in docs/SKILL-AUDIT.md (numbers are unique across the two docs).

### Q1 Strength's reach (answered A)

**Rows:** Strength, and every damage spell row (the +d10 joins their damage roll). **Today:** one flat d10 rolled at the cast and added to each weapon blow until the day ends, plus doubled hit points. **Ruled (SPELL-09):** +d10 on damage, rolled each time you deal damage, no hit points, 100 squares from the cast.

- **A (recommended; answered): every damage roll the hero makes as it strikes or casts gets its own +d10:** each weapon blow (both blows of a double strike) and each damage roll of a damage spell as it lands (each foe of an area spell, each Fireballs bolt); a damage-over-time tick is not a new roll and gets none.
- **B: weapon blows only** (canon: "any damage done by hand to hand combat is at +d10").

### Q2 Petrify's experience (answered A)

**Row:** Petrify. **Ruled (SPELL-12):** "It dies. You gain no loot from it."

- **A (recommended; answered): the stone foe dies and pays its experience like any kill, and drops no coin or treasure.**
- **B: no experience either** (today's removal).

### Q3 Which sleeps wake on a hit (answered A)

**Rows:** Doze, Noxious Vapor (and Insane's nap and the Cedar Staff's gas). **Ruled (SPELL-11):** a hit wakes a dozing foe.

- **A (recommended; answered): only Doze's sleep wakes on a hit;** every other sleep stays a plain sleep.
- **B: every sleep a spell, scroll or staff lays on a foe also wakes on a hit.**

### Q4 Doze's reach (answered A)

**Row:** Doze. **Ruled (SPELL-11):** Doze sleeps d4 foes for d4 rounds.

- **A (recommended; answered): exactly d4 foes, your target first.**
- **B: d4 × max(1, your level − 1),** canon p.26's level multiplier, as Stun's count scales today.

### Q5 Ice's shape (answered A)

**Row:** Ice. **Ruled (SPELL-12):** "the area version of the level-1 Freeze".

- **A (recommended; answered): no to-hit roll; every foe takes its own d10 + your level², then a survivor is frozen for d4 rounds unless it resists** (Freeze's rule: the resist stops only the freeze).
- **B: each foe first needs Freeze's to-hit roll** (hits on 5–10 on a d10 before bonuses).

### Q6 The school bonus's square step (answered A)

**Rows:** the ten new spells (Open/Lock, Fly, Enchant Character and Speed of Sound are square-timed; Stop Time, Senseless and Duplicate Foe are round-timed). **Ruled:** each point of the chart's school bonus stretches the new spells.

- **A (recommended; answered): each school bonus point adds +10 squares to a square-timed new spell** (an Illusionist's Fly lasts 70 squares, its Enchant Character 90) **and +1 round to a round-timed one** (its Stop Time 6 rounds).
- **B: +1 square per point.**

### Q7 Which foe Stupidity and Death hit (answered A)

**Rows:** Stupidity, Death. **Today:** both hit the FIRST live foe in the list, whichever foe you have picked (the prototype did the same), while Blind, Acid, Ice, Petrify, Insane, Freeze and the thrown spells hit the foe you picked, and the combat menu's resist line names the foe you picked. Doze joins the picked-foe group by your Q4 answer. Stupidity's own rework (intelligence to 1 for the fight, no floor-12 language) is already ruled and stays with 90-04 either way.

- **A (recommended; answered): both aim at the foe you picked** (a dead pick falls to the first live foe), so Death kills the foe you chose (still 25 hp, still a charge, still resisted) and Stupidity's resist line names the foe it really hits.
- **B: they keep hitting the first foe in the list** (the prototype's rule); the resist line and the text then say "the first foe".

### Q8 A Joiner's Lightning (answered A)

**Row:** Lightning. **Today:** a Magic User Joiner of level 4 or more prefers Lightning (its highest-level attack spell) and throws it at ONE foe, while the text and your own cast hit every foe, each on its own to-hit roll and its own resist.

- **A (recommended; answered): a Joiner's Lightning hits every foe, like yours,** so the text is true for both of you. A Joiner caster gets stronger against groups.
- **B: a Joiner's Lightning stays one foe,** a deliberate cap on Joiner power; the difference is recorded and the text keeps describing your cast.

### Q9 Turn Walking Dead's fixation (answered B, against the recommendation)

**Row:** Turn Walking Dead. **Today:** the Walking Dead the spell could not turn get a Fixated chip whose own copy says "It fights exactly as before"; the rulebook says they attack only the caster. It only matters when a Joiner is in the fight.

- **A (recommended): keep it as flavor** and record the deviation: the chip stays honest and the spell keeps its strength (the fixation would only be a drawback).
- **B (answered): canon:** every remaining Walking Dead swings only at you for the rest of the fight (a Joiner is never picked while one lives).
