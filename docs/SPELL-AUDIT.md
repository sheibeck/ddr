**Closed:** 2026-10-01 (plan 90-12): every row reads its shipped text and a final verdict, the two removed spells sit in the Removed table, and every pin below names a test file and a test title that exist (78 distinct pins). `test/unit/spell-audit.test.js` fails on any row still reading `fix engine`, `fix text`, `new (` or `balance call`, on a pin whose file or title is missing, on a spell with no row, and on a School gates row that disagrees with `MU_CHART`; `test/unit/spell-skill-text-engine.test.js` fails the day a spell's text and the numbers the engine rolls drift apart.

Every spell in the game has one row, and so does every spell the user ruled in (the ten-spell Special and Illusion slate) and, in the Removed table, every spell the user ruled out: what its text promises, what the engine does, the real odds in roll-high form, what canon says, a verdict and the tests that pin it. A mismatch is never waved through: it is fixed by a named plan, or it is a recorded ruling. The plan that built each fix is named in the Verdict column. The skill and ability twin of this table is `docs/SKILL-AUDIT.md`.

## How to read this table

**Text** is what the player reads: the `txt` line in `content/spells.js`, the Grimoire row (`src/browser/heroTab.js#grimoireViewModel`: `L{level} name` and the txt), the combat menu row (`src/browser/combatMenu.js#combatMenuViewModel`: `LVL n`, the txt, and for a spell cast on a foe the hint `{target} resists on {range}`, which prints the current target's half-intelligence resist range) and the chips the spell puts on the hero (`src/browser/heroConditions.js`) or on a foe (`src/browser/foeConditions.js`). Since 90-11 a spell cast on a foe also carries ONE resist sentence after its txt, on the Grimoire row (`heroTab.js#grimoireViewModel`'s `resistNote`) and the menu row, from `heroTab.js#GRIMOIRE_COPY.resistNote`: "a foe may resist this on its intelligence, and the deeper the floor, the likelier it does" (`test/unit/spell-skill-text-wording.test.js`: every spell a foe can resist says so on its Grimoire row and its combat menu row, deeper floors included; a spell on yourself says nothing of it).

**Engine** names the file and function that runs the spell and what it does in numbers. A Joiner's cast is named where `engine/combat.js#allyCast` handles the spell (it casts only `ATTACK_SPELL_KINDS`: status, thrown, stun and weaken, chosen by `derived.js#bestAttackSpell`).

**Rolls** states, in roll-high form (every to-hit and resist reads "hits on X–20 (d20)" or "+N to hit", with the real odds named; a damage die never reads like a to-hit roll): the to-hit roll or "no to-hit roll"; the resist or "never resisted"; the damage dice and multipliers (the level² term every damage spell adds since 2026-09-28, the school bonus, the p.26 level multiplier where it still applies) or "no damage"; the duration; and the backfire and fumble odds (the Apprentice's 1 in 8, the Summoner's 1 in 8 on its doubled summons, the scroll fumble row). The depth-rising resist note ("one depth-rising resist and no floor-12 extras (built in 90-04)") is the user's ruling at the Phase 89 checkpoint (docs/ITEM-AUDIT.md Q1) and sits in every row whose spell a foe can resist.

**Canon** cites the rulebook (`mazeworld.pdf`, printed page) and the frozen prototype (`test/parity/prototype-master.js.txt`, line, read only). The user's rulings override both, and the Canon cell names the ruling when it does.

**Default fix direction:** the engine does what the spell text promises (the text is the promise to the player), unless the text is a typo or the user ruled otherwise. A wording-only fix is the text's; a fix that moves balance materially (a number that changes a lot, a reach that widens, a limit that goes away, a spell that gets weaker) is a balance call and goes to the user before it is built.

**Verdict vocabulary** (the closed vocabulary `test/unit/spell-audit.test.js` accepts; no open verdict is left):

- `match` — text, engine and canon (as overridden by rulings) agree.
- `fixed engine (90-NN)` and `fixed text (90-NN)` — the engine (or the wording) was wrong; plan 90-NN built the fix and a test pins it. A ruled-in slate spell that plan 90-NN built reads `fixed engine (90-NN)` too.
- `ruled (Qn, 2026-09-30) -> 90-NN` and `ruled (2026-09-30) -> 90-NN` — a user ruling settles the row and the plan 90-NN named built it. (No spell row needs one after the close: every ruling is built and reads `fixed engine`.)
- `removed (90-06)` — a ruled-out spell that 90-06 deleted; it sits in the Removed table.
- `Phase 91 (IDENT-NN)` and `Phase 92` — handed to a later phase, with the hand-off named in Findings for other phases.

`Pinned by` is a list of `test/unit/<file>.test.js: <test title>` separated by `; `. Every row is pinned by the text-vs-engine guard (`test/unit/spell-skill-text-engine.test.js`: every number the text states is claimed by one fact and equals the engine); every fixed engine or fixed text row names the test that would fail if the fix were reverted. `test/unit/spell-audit.test.js` reads each named file and each title, so a pin cannot name a test that is gone or renamed.

**Plan owners** in the Verdict column: 90-02 Pommel Strike (skills doc); 90-03 Strength and spell-sourced timed effects; 90-04 the one depth-rising resist for every spell (no floor-12 extras), Petrify, Blind and Stupidity; 90-05 Doze, Stun and Ice; 90-06 Lesser Summon and Phantom Host removed, the Summoner's Summon from level 1, the Wizard's lost Illusion school, the school-gate guard; 90-07 Open/Lock, Fly, Enchant Character, Speed of Sound; 90-08 Stop Time, Senseless, Duplicate Foe; 90-09 Door Illusion, Chameleon Tongue, Size of the Behemoth and the Illusionist's starting book; 90-10 Joiner casters for the new and reworked spells and every other audit engine fix; 90-11 TEXT-01 wording for every spell row (faces become "+N to hit" with the d20 range named, "squares of" becomes how many foes an area spell hits, parley wording where it appears), built: the reworded rows are pinned by `test/unit/spell-skill-text-wording.test.js` (area effects say how many foes: Doze d4, Shrink up to d6, Plane Gate d6, Fireballs d8 bolts spread across the foes, Ice and Weaken every foe); 90-12 the text-vs-engine guard and the audit close.

**Two facts the rows lean on.** A spell is the round's action, so the foe turn that follows the cast in the same dispatch ticks a fresh timer once: a "d4 rounds" effect covers the foe visits of the cast round and the rounds after it, d4 visits in all. And a fight holds at most three foes (`engine/difficulty.js#FOE_COUNT_TABLE`), so a reach of "d6 foes" reaches every foe on 4 rolls in 6.

## Joiner casters

Built in 90-10 (SPELL-10, 90-CONTEXT "Joiner Magic Users cast the new spells when useful"). A Magic User Joiner casts from its OWN book (`canCast({ c: view }, sp)`, so the school gates and levels hold) and pays from its OWN sheet's charges, never the hero's. Its turn runs in this order (`engine/combat.js#alliesTurn`): Phase 89's item policy (a ready worn item in round 1; a potion at or below a third of its hit points), then its class ability (`pickMemberAbility`, Fighters and Thieves), then `pickMemberSpell` (pure, no rng), then its best attack spell (`bestAttackSpell`, Ice included), then its staff. `pickMemberSpell` tries, and the first castable pick wins (the higher effective level inside one step, then `SPELLS` order):

1. **A heal.** At or below half its hit points, a castable healing spell on itself (`memberHealed`: the spell's dice from a derived stream, the Cleric's +3, a heal2x race's doubling, then the sub-class's healMul, clamped to the Joiner's own maximum).
2. **A room control.** Three or more live foes: the highest-level castable of Stop Time (`timestop`), Size of the Behemoth (`behemoth`) and Doze (`status`), unless it is already in force on every live foe (all held in time, all cowering, all asleep).
3. **A round-1 buff.** Round 1 only: a `timed` row whose act kind is `haste` (Speed of Sound) or `enchant` (Enchant Character) that is not already live on its sheet; a `spell:` record on its own sheet, stretched by its own school bonus (`spellEffectSquares`), ticking by the squares the party walks and fading with `spellEffectFaded` naming it.
4. **A single control.** A live foe at or above the Joiner's level with more than half its hit points (the hero's current target first): Duplicate Foe or Senseless (`misdirect`) or Stun (`stun`), whichever is castable and not already on that foe (Senseless needs another live foe to turn it on).
5. **Nothing:** the best attack spell, then the staff.

Each pick resolves through the hero's own shared tail (`stopTime`, `behemothRoar`, `misdirectFoe`, `stunFoe`, `dozeFoes`, `startSpellEffect`), the Joiner's name on every event (`by`, or `member` on `spellEffectStarted`). ## Cross-cutting rules

**The universal intelligence resist (as it stands).** Every spell cast on a foe, damage spells included, rolls `combat.js#foeResistsSpell` once per targeted foe (user ruling 2026-09-27): half the foe's intelligence in winning faces on a d20, `max(1, round(intelligence / 2))`, so the foe resists on `21 − faces` or better. Intelligence 1–2 resists on 20 (5%), 3 on 19–20, 6 on 18–20 (15%), 10 on 16–20 (25%), 16 on 13–20 (40%), 20 on 11–20 (50%). A resisted spell does nothing to that foe; the turn and the charge are spent. The roll comes from a derived stream, so it never moves the main rng. The caster's own kinds (`SPELL_SELF_KINDS`: summon, ward, might, regen, heal, reveal, foresee, mirror, senses) are never resisted. Until 90-04 the control sites (Doze, Stun, Stupidity, Blind, Shrink, Ice's freeze, Noxious Vapor's sleep, Insane's sleep, Petrify) also rolled `resistControl` past floor 12 (1 face in 20 at floor 13, one more a floor, up to 15) and capped a landed control at three rounds (the RULES-18 knee, `engine/difficulty.js#CONTROL_AT_DEPTH`); both are gone from every spell.

**The ruled replacement (one depth-rising resist, no floor-12 extras; built in 90-04).** The user ruled at the Phase 89 checkpoint (docs/ITEM-AUDIT.md Q1, and again after 89-08 for damage spells: "I'm good with that.") that every spell and spell-like effect uses ONE resist that rises with depth, and that no spell keeps a "past floor 12" special effect. `engine/derived.js#risingResistFaces(depth, intel)` is the helper (faces = `min(19, round(a + c − a·c/20))`, `a` the half-intelligence faces, `c` the floor faces from floor 13), and `combat.js#foeResistsEffect` is the gate, both built in 89-08. Intelligence 10 resists on 16–20 up to floor 12, 15–20 at floor 13, 10–20 at floor 20 (55%) and 5–20 at floor 27. A landed spell is its floor-1 form at every depth: no three-round cap, no hold, no second resist. 90-04 moved every spell site onto the helper (`combat.js#foeResistsSpell` is `foeResistsEffect` under its spell name: the hero's casts, a scroll's free cast and a Joiner's `allyCast`), removed the Ice payoff's knee branches, and moved the combat menu's resist hint and the foe card from `resistFaces(intel)` to `risingResistFaces(depth, intel)`. The one caller of the RULES-18 helpers left is the Bard's `sing` (Phase 91, IDENT-17); `holdFoe`'s default hold has no live caller (pinned by `test/unit/spell-depth-resist.test.js`).

**Damage: the dice plus level².** A damage spell adds the caster's level squared (`derived.js#spellLevelSq`, user 2026-09-28): once to a thrown hit, to each foe a Lightning throw hits, to each foe an Earthquake damages, to the first bolt that strikes each foe in Fireballs, and to the first tick of Acid and Ice. It is never added to a heal, to the Earthquake backlash, to an Apprentice's backfire or to a fumbled scroll. Canon p.26's multiplier (`max(1, caster level − spell level)`) survives in two places: Stun's reach (the count goes away with the SPELL-11 swap) and a fumbled scroll's damage to the reader (`scrollFumble.js`). Strength's +d10 (Q1) adds one d10 to each damage roll the hero makes as it lands, and none to a damage-over-time tick.

**The school bonus.** `castSpell`'s thrown branch and `allyCast` add the chart's school bonus to a thrown spell's winning faces and nowhere else (the prototype did the same). Rulebook p.18 says the bonus also adds to damage, number affected, additional healing or squares; the user's Q6 stretches only the new Special and Illusion spells (+10 squares a point on a square-timed spell, +1 round a point on a round-timed one).

**Backfires.** An Apprentice's every cast goes wrong on a 1 on a d8 (1 in 8, 12.5%): the spell does nothing, and a thrown spell also costs the caster half its damage roll. A Summoner's doubled summon (Summon; Phantom Host and Lesser Summon are gone, 90-06) turns on the Summoner on a 1 on a d8 for level² + d6. Both are `rng.d(8)` rolls tagged `roll:mishap-on-1`, deliberately not mirrored to a roll-high form (a 1 is always the worst face). A Joiner's cast never backfires (Findings, Phase 91).

**Scroll reading (RULES-10) and the scroll pool.** Anyone may read any scroll and the scroll is consumed on every attempt. A Magic User reads automatically (and copies the spell into the book when the gates allow); a Runes/Signs reader reads automatically with no copy; everyone else rolls a d20 on `scrollReadBands`: intelligence 14 reads on 8–20, a roll below half the target (1–3) fumbles and anything between is garbled. A fumble in a fight turns the spell on the reader or its side per `content/scroll-fumbles.js` (one row per spell); outside a fight it fizzles. A combat-only spell read outside a fight is consumed and refused. The scroll's spell is `rng.pick(SPELLS.filter(sp => sp.lvl <= min(5, depth + 1)))`. At master it held 16 spells at depth 1 (levels 1 to 2), 25 at depth 2, 30 at depth 3 and 33 from depth 4. With Lesser Summon and Phantom Host out (90-06) it holds 15, 23, 28 and 31; with the four Special slate spells in (90-07: Open/Lock and Fly from depth 1, Enchant Character from depth 3, Speed of Sound from depth 4) it holds 17, 25, 31 and 35, with 90-08's three (Senseless from depth 1, Stop Time from depth 2, Duplicate Foe from depth 4) it holds 18, 27, 33 and 38, and with 90-09's three (Door Illusion from depth 1, Chameleon Tongue from depth 2, Size of the Behemoth from depth 3) it holds 19, 29, 36 and 41. The user's mid-run ruling pins that both new schools appear at each band the level allows and the removed spells never do: `test/unit/scroll-pool.test.js` (90-09) pins it for all ten slate spells at depths 1, 2, 3, 4 and 9 (both schools represented at every band, Lesser Summon and Phantom Host never), and the reading rules (a Fighter's free cast, a Warlock free-casts an Illusion scroll without copying it, a Wizard copies Special but not Illusion, an Illusionist copies when castable); `test/unit/special-timed-spells.test.js` pins the Special half as well.

## Balance calls

### Q1 Strength's reach (answered A)

**Rows:** Strength, and every damage spell row (the +d10 joins their damage roll). **Today:** one flat d10 rolled at the cast and added to each weapon blow until the day ends, plus doubled hit points. **Ruled (SPELL-09):** +d10 on damage, rolled each time you deal damage, no hit points, 100 squares from the cast.
